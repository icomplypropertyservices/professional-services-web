## Qualifications and registration to look for

"Architect" is a protected title in the UK. Only people on the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance. Many practices are also RIBA Chartered Practices. Architectural designers and technologists can do excellent work too, but they are not registered architects, so it is worth knowing which you are hiring.

Before you appoint anyone, check the ARB register and ask for a written appointment that sets out services, fees and responsibilities. Building regulations approval, planning permission and party wall matters are separate processes that your architect can help you navigate.

## Understanding conservation area controls

In a conservation area, the local planning authority has extra powers to protect the area's special character. This means some work that would normally be allowed without planning permission – such as certain extensions, cladding or demolitions – may need consent. Article Four directions can remove further permitted development rights, so it's important to check with the council what applies to your property.

Materials and details matter a great deal in these areas. The planning authority will expect new work to respect the local building traditions, and something that looks out of place is more likely to be refused. An architect with conservation area experience can help you design something that respects the area while meeting your needs.

## FAQs about conservation area architect near me

### Do I always need planning permission for work in a conservation area?

Not always, but the rules are stricter than elsewhere. Some work that would normally be permitted development may need planning permission, and Article Four directions can remove further rights. It's best to check with your local planning authority before you start.

### What is an Article Four direction?

An Article Four direction is a special planning regulation that removes some permitted development rights in a specific area. It means you may need planning permission for certain changes that would otherwise be allowed without it. Your local planning authority can tell you if one applies to your property.

### Can I use any architect for a conservation area project?

You can use any architect, but it's wise to choose one with experience of conservation areas. They will understand the planning constraints and how to design sympathetically. iComply can introduce you to architects who work in this field.

### How do I check if an architect is properly registered?

You can check the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register online to confirm someone is a registered architect. Only those on the register can call themselves architects. It's also worth asking for proof of professional indemnity insurance.

### Will an architect guarantee planning permission?

No one can guarantee planning permission. A good architect will explain local policy, the risks and how to give your application the best chance.
