## Checking physiotherapists are qualified and regulated

"Physiotherapist" and "physical therapist" are protected titles in the UK. Anyone using them must be registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC), and you can check the HCPC register online. Most physiotherapists are also members of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP), shown by the letters MCSP.

Sports therapists, massage therapists and personal trainers can be helpful for some needs, but they are not physiotherapists. For an injury, post-surgery rehabilitation or a neurological condition, check that you are booking a HCPC-registered physiotherapist.

## FAQs: a musculoskeletal physiotherapist

### Do I need a referral from my GP to see a musculoskeletal physiotherapist?

In the UK, you can usually see a private physiotherapist without a GP referral. However, some insurance policies or specific services may require one, so it's worth checking with the physiotherapist when you enquire.

### How do I check that a physiotherapist is properly registered?

You can check the HCPC register online to confirm that anyone using the title 'physiotherapist' is registered. Most physiotherapists are also members of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP), which is shown by the letters MCSP after their name.

### What's the difference between a physiotherapist and a sports therapist?

Physiotherapists are HCPC-registered healthcare professionals who can diagnose and treat a wide range of musculoskeletal problems. Sports therapists and massage therapists are not physiotherapists and are not regulated by the HCPC, so for an injury or post-surgery rehabilitation, check that you are booking a HCPC-registered physiotherapist.

### Can a musculoskeletal physiotherapist help with long-standing back pain?

Yes, MSK physiotherapists commonly treat persistent back pain. They will assess your movement and strength, help you understand what's contributing to the pain, and guide you through exercises and load management to improve your function.

### Can physio help if I have already had a scan?

Yes. Bring the report with you. A physiotherapist will combine it with their own assessment to plan treatment.
