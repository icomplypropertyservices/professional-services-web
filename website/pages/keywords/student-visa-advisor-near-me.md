## Checking immigration advisers are qualified and regulated

It is a criminal offence to give immigration advice in the UK unless you are qualified and regulated. Advisers must be registered with the Immigration Advice Authority (IAA, formerly the OISC) at the right level for your matter, or be a solicitor, barrister or chartered legal executive authorised by their own regulator. Check the IAA's adviser finder or the SRA register before you share documents or pay.

IAA advisers are registered at levels that limit what they can do, from basic applications to appeals and representation. Ask what level an adviser holds and whether it covers your type of case.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## FAQs: a student visa adviser

### Can anyone near me advertise as a student visa advisor?

No. Giving immigration advice in the UK is a criminal offence unless the person is qualified and regulated, either through the Immigration Advice Authority or as a solicitor, barrister or chartered legal executive authorised by their own regulator. Always check the IAA's adviser finder or the SRA register before sharing documents or paying.

### How long do I need to hold my funds for a student visa?

There is a required holding period for the funds you rely on as financial evidence, and the rules set out exactly how the money must be held and evidenced. Because the details matter and change, ask a regulated adviser to confirm the current requirement for your circumstances rather than relying on what you have heard.

### Can I switch to a student visa without leaving the UK?

Switching depends on the visa you currently hold and the rules that apply to it, and not every route allows switching from inside the UK. A regulated adviser can tell you whether switching is possible for you or whether you would need to apply from abroad.

### What happens after my course ends?

Graduate routes have their own rules, separate from the student visa, and they set out what you can do once your studies are complete. An adviser can explain how those routes apply to your situation and what you would need to prepare.

### Is it safe to use an unregulated immigration adviser?

No. Giving immigration advice without regulation is a criminal offence in the UK, and you may have no protection if something goes wrong. We only introduce regulated advisers.
