## What to understand about opticians near you

An eye examination is carried out by an optometrist, while dispensing opticians fit and supply glasses and, with extra training, contact lenses. Both must be registered with the General Optical Council, which you can check online before you book.

In many areas of the UK, community optometrists also provide minor eye condition and urgent eye care services, so it is worth asking what your local practice offers. If your eye is painful, suddenly changed or injured, seek urgent help rather than waiting for a routine appointment.

## How to check you are in safe hands

Optometrists and dispensing opticians must be registered with the General Optical Council (GOC), which you can check online. Optometrists carry out eye examinations; dispensing opticians fit and supply glasses and, with extra training, contact lenses.

Some optometrists hold extra qualifications that let them manage more eye conditions or prescribe certain medicines. In many areas of the UK, community optometrists provide minor eye condition and urgent eye care services.

iComply is not a regulator and does not provide the professional service itself. We introduce you to professionals who hold their own registrations, and we encourage you to check them on the public register.

## How long things usually take

Routine eye tests can often be booked within a few days. Sudden loss of vision, a curtain over your vision, or severe eye pain needs urgent same-day assessment at an eye casualty or urgent eye service.

## Key things to know about an optician

A few points come up again and again with an optician:

- Do you provide minor eye condition or urgent eye care services at this practice?
- Can you talk me through the options for contact lenses and what fitting involves?

## FAQs about optician near me

### Do I need an eye examination before I can order new glasses?

Usually you will need a current prescription before new glasses can be made up, so the practice will advise whether an examination is needed first. If you already have a recent prescription, tell them when you enquire.

### What is an OCT scan and should I ask about one?

An OCT scan is a detailed imaging scan of the back of the eye that some practices offer alongside a standard examination. It is worth asking whether it is available and whether it would add anything in your situation.

### Can an optician near me help with a sore or gritty eye?

Ask the practice what they can deal with directly, and seek urgent help if your eye is painful or your sight has suddenly changed.

### How do I check that an optician is properly registered?

Optometrists and dispensing opticians must be registered with the General Optical Council, and you can check the register online. It is a quick step worth taking before you book.

### How often should I have an eye test?

Usually every two years for adults, or more often if your optometrist recommends it, for example if you have diabetes or a family history of glaucoma.
