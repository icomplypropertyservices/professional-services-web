## The key thing to understand about work visa advice

In the UK, it is a criminal offence to give immigration advice unless the adviser is qualified and regulated. Advisers must be registered with the Immigration Advice Authority (IAA, formerly the OISC) at the right level for your matter, or be a solicitor, barrister or chartered legal executive authorised by their own regulator.

IAA advisers are registered at different levels that limit what they can do, from basic applications to appeals and representation. Always ask what level an adviser holds and whether it covers your type of case. You can check the IAA's adviser finder or the SRA register before you share documents or pay.

## FAQs: a work visa adviser

### Do I need a licensed sponsor for a skilled worker visa?

Yes, skilled worker visas require a licensed sponsor. Your employer must be on the register of licensed sponsors, and you should check this before applying.

### How can I check if an adviser is regulated?

You can check the Immigration Advice Authority's adviser finder or the Solicitors Regulation Authority register. Always do this before sharing documents or paying any money.

### What salary threshold applies for a skilled worker visa?

The salary threshold depends on the occupation code and the going rate for the role. An adviser can help you understand the specific requirement for your job.

### Can iComply give me immigration advice?

No, iComply is not regulated to give immigration advice. We introduce you to a suitable UK immigration adviser who can advise you and quote you directly.

### Does a work visa adviser need to be near me to help?

Not necessarily. Many advisers work by phone, email or video call, and documents can usually be shared electronically. Being local can still help if you prefer in-person appointments or need to hand over original documents.

### What should I bring to a first meeting with a work visa adviser?

Bring your job offer or contract if you have one, details of your employer and their sponsor licence, your occupation and salary information, and any English language test results. Also bring copies of your passport and any previous immigration documents.

### Can a work visa adviser help if my employer is not yet a licensed sponsor?

An adviser can explain what sponsorship involves and what your employer would need to do, but they cannot make an employer a licensed sponsor. Skilled worker visas require a licensed sponsor, so this is usually the first thing to clarify.

### Is it safe to use an unregulated immigration adviser?

No. Giving immigration advice without regulation is a criminal offence in the UK, and you may have no protection if something goes wrong. We only introduce regulated advisers.
