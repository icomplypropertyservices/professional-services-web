## What to understand about online conveyancing and local knowledge

Conveyancing in England and Wales is a reserved legal activity, carried out by solicitors regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority or by licensed conveyancers regulated by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers. Both must hold professional indemnity insurance and follow strict rules on handling client money. In Scotland, property transactions are handled by solicitors under Scottish law.

Many lenders only work with conveyancers on their panel, and the Law Society's Conveyancing Quality Scheme is a common accreditation. Before you instruct an online conveyancer, check that they are on your lender's panel, and always verify bank details by phone before sending any money.

## Online Conveyancer Near Me: FAQs

### Can I use an online conveyancer if I am buying with a mortgage?

Yes, but you need to check that the conveyancer is on your lender's panel. Many lenders only work with conveyancers on their approved list, so confirm this before you instruct.

### How do online conveyancers verify my identity?

They usually ask for scanned copies of documents such as your passport and a recent utility bill, and may use an online verification service. Always check the firm's own process and make sure you are comfortable with it.

### Is it safe to send money to an online conveyancer?

Conveyancers must follow strict rules on handling client money, but fraud is a risk. Always verify bank details by phone before sending any money, using a number you have independently confirmed.

### Do I need a conveyancer near me if I am working online?

Not necessarily, but some people prefer a firm with local knowledge of the property's area. You can ask about their experience with the local area and whether they can visit the property if needed.

### Can an online conveyancer visit the property if needed?

Some can arrange a visit, and some will not need one because the work is done through searches and documents. Ask about this early if you think a physical visit might be necessary, so you know where you stand before instructing.

### Will I ever need to meet my conveyancer in person?

Usually not, because identity checks and signatures can be handled remotely. However, some people prefer at least one face-to-face conversation, so ask whether that is possible and how it would be arranged.

### Does working online change how quickly things move?

It can make sending and receiving documents quicker, but the pace of a transaction depends on many things, including searches and other parties. Ask the conveyancer to explain how they keep things moving and how they will update you.

### What is the difference between a solicitor and a licensed conveyancer?

Both are regulated and insured. Licensed conveyancers specialise in property transactions, while solicitors can also advise on wider legal issues. Either can handle a standard purchase or sale.

### Can everything be done online?

Often much of it can. Some steps may need a visit, depending on the service. The practice will tell you what can be done remotely.
