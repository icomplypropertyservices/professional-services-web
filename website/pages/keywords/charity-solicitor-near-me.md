## How to check you are in safe hands

Solicitors in England and Wales are regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA), and you can check an individual or firm on the SRA's register. In Scotland solicitors are regulated by the Law Society of Scotland, and in Northern Ireland by the Law Society of Northern Ireland. Regulated firms must hold professional indemnity insurance and have a complaints procedure; if that does not resolve things, the Legal Ombudsman can look at service complaints in England and Wales.

The word "lawyer" is not protected on its own, so check that whoever handles your matter is a solicitor, a chartered legal executive (CILEx) or another authorised professional. Reserved activities such as conducting court litigation and certain probate and conveyancing work can only be carried out by authorised people.

## FAQs: a charity solicitor

### Do I need a solicitor to set up a charity?

It is not a legal requirement, but a solicitor can help you choose the right structure, prepare the governing document, and ensure the charity meets Charity Commission requirements. This can save time and reduce the risk of problems later.

### What are trustee duties in law?

Trustees have personal duties, including acting in the charity's best interests, managing resources responsibly, and complying with charity law. A solicitor can explain these duties and help you avoid personal liability.

### When must a serious incident be reported to the Charity Commission?

Trustees must report serious incidents that have occurred or are likely to occur, such as fraud, financial loss, or harm to beneficiaries. A solicitor can advise on what needs reporting and how to handle the situation.

### How can I check that a charity solicitor is properly regulated?

You can check the Solicitors Regulation Authority register for solicitors in England and Wales, the Law Society of Scotland for Scotland, or the Law Society of Northern Ireland for Northern Ireland. This confirms the individual or firm is regulated and holds professional indemnity insurance.

### Can I get legal aid?

Legal aid is still available for some criminal, family (especially where there is domestic abuse), housing and immigration matters, subject to means and merits tests. A matched firm can tell you whether you are likely to qualify.
