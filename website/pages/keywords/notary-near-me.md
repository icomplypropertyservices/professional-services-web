## What to understand about finding a notary near you

Notaries public in England and Wales are qualified lawyers regulated by the Faculty Office of the Archbishop of Canterbury, which keeps a register of practising notaries. They must hold insurance and follow practice rules. In Scotland, notaries are solicitors admitted as notaries public. Because notaries are regulated, you can check that the person you are introduced to appears on the relevant register.

Notaries do not all offer the same services, and some specialise in particular countries or types of document. When you search for a notary near me, it helps to know that the notary will need to see original documents and may need to arrange legalisation or an apostille through the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office. ## FAQs: a notary public

### Do I need a notary or a solicitor for a document going abroad?

A notary public is specifically trained to authenticate documents for use abroad, while a solicitor may not have that expertise. If the receiving authority has asked for notarisation, you will usually need a notary. iComply can introduce you to a suitable notary for your needs.

### What is an apostille and will I need one?

An apostille is a certificate that confirms the authenticity of a notary's signature and seal for countries that are part of the Hague Apostille Convention. Whether you need one depends on the destination country's requirements, so check with the authority requesting the document. A notary can arrange an apostille through the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office.

### Can a notary come to my home or hospital?

Some notaries offer mobile appointments, but this varies and may depend on your location and circumstances. When you enquire through iComply, mention if you need a notary who can visit you, and we will try to introduce you to one who can help.

### How do I know the notary is properly qualified?

Notaries in England and Wales are regulated by the Faculty Office of the Archbishop of Canterbury, which keeps a register of practising notaries. You can check the relevant register to confirm a notary's status before your appointment.

### What is an apostille?

A certificate from the FCDO confirming a UK public official's signature or seal, so a document is accepted in countries that are part of the Hague Convention.
