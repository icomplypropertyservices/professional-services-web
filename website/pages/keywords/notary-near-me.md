## Qualifications and registration to look for

Notaries in England and Wales are qualified lawyers regulated by the Faculty Office of the Archbishop of Canterbury, which keeps a register of practising notaries. They must hold insurance and follow practice rules. In Scotland, notaries are solicitors admitted as notaries public.

Notaries authenticate documents for use abroad, witness signatures, verify identity and arrange legalisation or apostilles through the Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office.

## FAQs: a notary public

### What is an apostille?

A certificate from the FCDO confirming a UK public official's signature or seal, so a document is accepted in countries that are part of the Hague Convention.

### Is a notary the same as a solicitor?

No. Notaries are a separate branch of the legal profession, specialising in documents for use abroad. Some are also solicitors.
