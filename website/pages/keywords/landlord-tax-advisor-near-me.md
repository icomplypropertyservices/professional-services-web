## How to check you are in safe hands

"Tax adviser" is not a protected title in the UK. The Chartered Institute of Taxation (CTA qualification) and the Association of Taxation Technicians (ATT) are the main specialist bodies, and many accountants and solicitors also advise on tax. Members of these bodies follow Professional Conduct in Relation to Taxation, a shared code covering honesty, confidentiality and dealing with HMRC.

Tax advisers who provide services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes. HMRC has also been tightening standards for paid tax agents, so it is reasonable to ask about qualifications, supervision and professional indemnity insurance before you go ahead.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## Landlord Tax Advisor Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a specialist landlord tax adviser or can my usual accountant help?

Many accountants advise on landlord tax, and some tax advisers specialise in property. It is reasonable to ask about their experience with rental income, capital gains and ownership structures before you decide.

### What does 'near me' mean for tax advice – do I need to meet in person?

Tax advice can often be given remotely, but some landlords prefer a local adviser they can meet. Tell iComply your preference and we will introduce you to a suitable professional.

### Is incorporation always the best way to hold rental property?

No, incorporation is not right for everyone. A tax adviser can look at your personal circumstances and explain the pros and cons of owning property personally or through a company.

### How are gains on residential property reported?

Gains on residential property must be reported quickly, and there are deadlines for both reporting and paying any tax due. A tax adviser can tell you the exact rules that apply to your sale.

### I already have an accountant – is it worth seeing a landlord tax adviser as well?

It can be, especially if your accountant deals mainly with your rental accounts and you want help with capital gains, inheritance tax or ownership structures across a portfolio. Some landlords use a specialist for one-off decisions while keeping their accountant for the annual return. Ask both how they would work together so nothing is duplicated.

### Can a landlord tax adviser help if I only own one property?

Yes. A single rental flat or house still raises questions about deductible expenses, joint ownership and what happens if you sell. A short conversation can be enough to set things straight before a decision is made.

### Should I reply to HMRC before speaking to an adviser?

Note the deadline on the letter, but avoid sending a detailed reply before you have advice if the matter is complex. An adviser can often ask HMRC for more time where it is reasonable.
