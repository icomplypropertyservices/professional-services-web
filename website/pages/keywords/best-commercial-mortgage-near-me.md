## Qualifications and registration to look for

Mortgage advice is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Any broker or adviser you use should be authorised, or be an appointed representative of an authorised firm, and you can check this on the FCA register. Advisers typically hold a qualification such as CeMAP.

Brokers must tell you whether they search the whole of the market or a limited panel of lenders, and how they are paid, whether by lender commission, a fee, or both. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

## Best Commercial Mortgage Near Me: FAQs

### Is a commercial mortgage regulated?

Most commercial lending is unregulated, which means you have fewer protections than with a residential mortgage. However, mortgage advice is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), so any broker you use should be authorised or an appointed representative, and you can check this on the FCA register.

### How much deposit do I need for a commercial mortgage?

Deposits for commercial mortgages are usually larger than for residential mortgages, but the exact amount depends on the lender and the property. A broker can explain what different lenders typically require for your situation.

### Can I get a commercial mortgage if my business is new?

Some lenders are more flexible than others, and a broker can help you find lenders who are willing to consider newer businesses. Be prepared to provide a solid business plan and financial projections.

### What is the difference between a whole-of-market and a limited-panel broker?

A whole-of-market broker searches across many lenders, while a limited-panel broker only has access to a selected group. Brokers must tell you which type they are, so you can decide what suits you best.

### Does 'near me' mean I should only use a broker in my town?

Not necessarily. What matters is that the broker understands commercial lending and is easy for you to speak to, whether that is face to face or by phone. Many business owners work with a broker further away if the fit is better.

### Can a broker promise me the best or cheapest commercial mortgage?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Commercial lending is mostly unregulated and terms depend on the lender's view of your business and the property, so compare what each broker says about lenders, deposit and fees before deciding.

### What if my business has only just started trading?

Some lenders are more open to newer businesses than others, and a broker can explain which are worth approaching. Expect to provide a business plan and financial projections, and be ready to discuss a larger deposit.

### Is a broker better than going to my bank?

A broker can compare many lenders and may find options your bank does not offer. Your bank can only offer its own products. Either can suit you; a broker gives you a wider view.
