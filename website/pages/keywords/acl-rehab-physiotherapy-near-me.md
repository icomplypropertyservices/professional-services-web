## How to check you are in safe hands

"Physiotherapist" and "physical therapist" are protected titles in the UK. Anyone using them must be registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC), and you can check the HCPC register online. Most physiotherapists are also members of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP), shown by the letters MCSP.

Sports therapists, massage therapists and personal trainers can be helpful for some needs, but they are not physiotherapists. For an injury, post-surgery rehabilitation or a neurological condition, check that you are booking a HCPC-registered physiotherapist.

## FAQs about ACL rehab physiotherapy near me

### Can ACL injuries be managed without surgery?

Some ACL injuries are managed without surgery, with rehabilitation to build strength and control around the knee. Whether that suits you depends on your injury, your knee and your goals, and a physiotherapist or surgeon can advise on your situation.

### How long does ACL rehabilitation take?

ACL rehabilitation often takes many months, and return to sport usually takes many months too. Your physiotherapist can explain what progress looks like at each stage for you.

### Why does strength testing matter in ACL rehab?

Strength testing helps guide progression, showing whether your knee is ready for the next stage of rehabilitation. It gives you and your physiotherapist something concrete to base decisions on rather than guesswork.

### How do I check a physiotherapist is properly registered?

"Physiotherapist" is a protected title, so anyone using it must be registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC). You can check the HCPC register online before you book.

### Do I need a GP referral for private physio?

No, you can usually book directly as a self-paying patient. Your insurer may require a referral, so check your policy.
