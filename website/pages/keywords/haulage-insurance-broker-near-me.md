## Why goods in transit is separate from your vehicle cover

Haulage insurance is not one product but several. Your HGV insurance covers the vehicle itself and third-party liabilities, but the goods you carry are not automatically covered. Goods in transit cover is a separate policy, and if you carry loads for customers you should check whether you need it.

As an O-licence holder, you must have suitable insurance in place, and the cover you choose should reflect the size and type of your operation. A broker can explain the key features and exclusions of each policy and help you compare what different insurers offer.

## FAQs: a broker for haulage insurance

### Do I need goods in transit cover if I already have HGV insurance?

Yes, goods in transit cover is separate from your HGV insurance. Your vehicle policy typically covers the vehicle and third-party liabilities, but not the goods you carry. If you transport customer goods, you should consider a separate goods in transit policy.

### How does my O-licence affect the insurance I need?

As an O-licence holder, you must have suitable insurance in place to meet your licence conditions. The cover you need will depend on the type and size of your operation. A broker can help you choose a policy that satisfies your obligations.

### Will my drivers' history affect my premium?

Yes, driver history is a key factor that insurers consider when quoting for haulage insurance. Claims, convictions and experience levels can all influence the premium and which insurers will offer cover. A broker can explain how this applies to your situation.

### How do I check that a broker is authorised?

Insurance brokers and intermediaries in the UK must be authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), or be appointed representatives of an authorised firm. You can check them on the FCA register. Many brokers also belong to the British Insurance Brokers' Association (BIBA).

### Does a broker need to be near my depot to arrange haulage cover?

Not strictly, but a local broker may be more familiar with the routes, customers and conditions your vehicles deal with. It is worth asking any broker how much haulage work they handle in your area before you decide.

### Can a broker help if my drivers' histories make cover difficult to find?

Driver history is one of the factors insurers weigh when quoting for haulage, so a broker can explain how yours is likely to be viewed and which insurers may consider it. They may not be able to find cover for every situation, but they can talk you through the options honestly.

### What should I have ready before I speak to a broker?

It helps to know your vehicle types, whether you hold an O-licence, the goods you carry and your drivers' experience and claims history. Having these details to hand means the broker can understand your demands and needs properly from the start.

### What is non-disclosure?

Failing to tell an insurer something relevant. It can reduce or void a claim, so answer questions fully and ask your broker if unsure.
