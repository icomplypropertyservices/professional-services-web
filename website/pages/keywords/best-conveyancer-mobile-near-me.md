## Qualifications and registration to look for

Conveyancing in England and Wales is a reserved legal activity. It can be carried out by solicitors regulated by the SRA or by licensed conveyancers regulated by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers (CLC). Both must hold professional indemnity insurance and follow strict rules on handling client money. In Scotland, property transactions are handled by solicitors under Scottish law.

Many lenders only work with conveyancers on their panel, and the Law Society's Conveyancing Quality Scheme (CQS) is a common accreditation. Check that your chosen firm is on your lender's panel before you instruct them.

## FAQs: a conveyancer

### How do I judge which conveyancer is best for me?

Ask about contact arrangements, who handles your file, panel membership and how fees are set out, then compare the answers. Best is personal to your move, so weigh reachability and clarity as much as price.

### Can a conveyancer work with me by mobile?

Many conveyancers take calls on a mobile and keep in touch by phone, though arrangements vary by firm. Ask before you instruct whether you will have a direct number and a named contact.

### Does near me matter for conveyancing?

Much conveyancing is done by phone, post and online, so distance matters less than reachability and panel membership. A local firm can still be convenient if you prefer to drop documents in.

### Why should I verify bank details by phone?

Fraudsters sometimes impersonate conveyancers to redirect payments, so always ring the firm on a number you have looked up yourself before sending money. Never rely on bank details given in an email alone.

### Does a mobile number mean my conveyancer will always answer straight away?

Not necessarily. Many conveyancers take calls on a mobile, but they may be in appointments or with other clients. Ask who your named contact will be and how quickly they aim to return calls, so you know what to expect.

### Should I choose a conveyancer near me or one I can reach by mobile?

The two are not mutually exclusive, and much conveyancing is done remotely anyway. A local firm can be convenient for dropping off documents, while reachability by mobile matters for day-to-day updates. Ask about both before you decide.

### What should I ask about contact before I instruct a conveyancer?

Ask whether you will have a named contact, whether you can ring them on a mobile, and how they usually keep clients updated. Also check they are on your lender's panel and confirm which regulator they are regulated by, such as the SRA or the Council for Licensed Conveyancers.

### When should I instruct a conveyancer?

As soon as your offer is accepted, or before you list a property for sale. Instructing early helps you avoid delays.
