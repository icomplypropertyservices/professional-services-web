## One key thing to understand about LLP accountants

The word accountant is not a protected title in the UK, so anyone can call themselves one. Protected titles include Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA), and members of those bodies are bound by their ethics, training and complaints rules. Only a registered auditor can sign a statutory audit report.

Every accountancy practice must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises a firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled.

## LLP Accountant Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a special accountant for an LLP?

Not necessarily, but an accountant who deals with LLPs regularly will be familiar with the partnership return, members' Self Assessment and the salaried member rules. It is worth asking how often they work with LLPs.

### What is the salaried member rules test?

The salaried member rules can affect whether a member of an LLP is taxed as employed or self-employed. The tests look at the member's role and remuneration, and an accountant can explain how they apply to your members.

### What is LLP SORP?

LLP SORP is the Statement of Recommended Practice for limited liability partnerships, which sets out how their accounts should be prepared. If your LLP prepares accounts under it, your accountant should be able to explain what it involves.
