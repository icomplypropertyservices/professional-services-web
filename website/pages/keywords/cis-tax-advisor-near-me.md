## Checking tax advisers are qualified and regulated

"Tax adviser" is not a protected title in the UK. The Chartered Institute of Taxation (CTA qualification) and the Association of Taxation Technicians (ATT) are the main specialist bodies, and many accountants and solicitors also advise on tax. Members of these bodies follow Professional Conduct in Relation to Taxation, a shared code covering honesty, confidentiality and dealing with HMRC.

Tax advisers who provide services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes. HMRC has also been tightening standards for paid tax agents, so it is reasonable to ask about qualifications, supervision and professional indemnity insurance before you go ahead.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## FAQs: a CIS tax adviser

### Can a local tax adviser help me claim back CIS deductions?

Yes, a CIS tax adviser can look at the deductions taken from your payments and check whether a refund is due. They can also help you gather the records HMRC needs and deal with any queries that arise.

### What does gross payment status require?

Gross payment status generally needs a clean compliance record, including filing returns and paying tax on time. A CIS adviser can explain what HMRC looks at and help you prepare an application.

### What happens if my CIS return is late?

Late returns can attract penalties, so it is worth acting promptly. An adviser can explain the position, help you file what's outstanding and discuss how to avoid repeat issues.

### Do I need to check subcontractors before paying them?

Yes, it is sensible to verify subcontractors before paying them so you apply the correct treatment under the scheme. A CIS adviser can talk you through the verification process and what records to keep.

### I searched for a CIS tax advisor near me but I'm not sure whether I need an adviser at all. Can I check first?

Yes. A short initial conversation can help you see whether your situation needs professional help or whether you can sort it out yourself. There's no obligation to go ahead once you've been quoted.

### Do I need to live close to my CIS tax adviser?

Not necessarily. Many CIS matters can be handled remotely, but searching near you often means you'd prefer to meet face to face or have someone who knows your local area. It's worth saying which you'd prefer when you enquire.

### What records should I gather before speaking to a CIS tax adviser?

Gather your CIS payment and deduction statements, any returns you've filed, and letters or notices from HMRC. These let the adviser check your position and spot anything that needs putting right.

### Is tax planning legal?

Using reliefs and allowances as Parliament intended is normal and legal. Aggressive avoidance schemes are a different matter and can carry serious risks. A qualified adviser will explain the difference clearly.
