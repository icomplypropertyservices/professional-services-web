## Child Custody Solicitor Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a solicitor to apply for a child arrangements order?

You can apply to the court yourself, but a solicitor can explain the process, help complete forms and represent you at hearings. They can also advise on whether mediation might be a better first step.

### What is the difference between custody and child arrangements?

In England and Wales, the term custody has been replaced by child arrangements. A child arrangements order now deals with where a child lives and the contact they have with each parent.

### How do I check that a solicitor is regulated?

Solicitors in England and Wales are regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA). You can check an individual or firm on the SRA's register. In Scotland, check with the Law Society of Scotland, and in Northern Ireland with the Law Society of Northern Ireland.

### Can a solicitor help if I am worried about my child's safety?

Yes, a solicitor can advise on urgent protection options if a child is at risk. They can explain what steps you can take and whether you need to apply to court quickly.

### Is a solicitor the same as a lawyer?

"Lawyer" is a general word that covers solicitors, barristers, chartered legal executives and others. A solicitor is a specific regulated professional who can advise you, run your case and instruct a barrister if needed.
