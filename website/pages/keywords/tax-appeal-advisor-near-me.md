## The deadline and the reconsideration route

Appeals usually have a thirty-day deadline, so it is important to act promptly once you receive an HMRC decision. Missing it can limit your options, so gather your paperwork and seek advice as soon as you can.

A formal reconsideration can resolve some disputes without the need for a tribunal, which may save time and stress. If that does not resolve matters, the First-tier Tribunal is the next step, and your adviser will help you prepare evidence and arguments for either route.

## How to check you are in safe hands

"Tax adviser" is not a protected title in the UK. The Chartered Institute of Taxation (CTA qualification) and the Association of Taxation Technicians (ATT) are the main specialist bodies, and many accountants and solicitors also advise on tax. Members of these bodies follow Professional Conduct in Relation to Taxation, a shared code covering honesty, confidentiality and dealing with HMRC.

Tax advisers who provide services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes. HMRC has also been tightening standards for paid tax agents, so it is reasonable to ask about qualifications, supervision and professional indemnity insurance before you go ahead.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## FAQs about tax appeal advisor near me

### Can a tax appeal advisor help if I have missed the thirty-day deadline?

It may still be possible to appeal late in some circumstances, but you should seek advice quickly. An adviser can explain your options and whether you can apply for an extension.

### What is the difference between a formal reconsideration and the First-tier Tribunal?

A formal reconsideration is an internal HMRC reconsideration that can resolve some disputes without a tribunal hearing. If it does not resolve matters, you can usually take your case to the First-tier Tribunal.

### Do I need to meet my tax appeal advisor in person?

Not necessarily, but searching for someone near you can make it easier to discuss paperwork and evidence face to face. Many advisers also work remotely, so you can choose what suits you.

### How do I know a tax appeal advisor is properly qualified?

Tax adviser is not a protected title in the UK, so it is reasonable to ask about qualifications, membership of a specialist body, supervision and professional indemnity insurance before you go ahead.

### Is tax planning legal?

Using reliefs and allowances as Parliament intended is normal and legal. Aggressive avoidance schemes are a different matter and can carry serious risks. A qualified adviser will explain the difference clearly.
