## What happens when you first speak to an annuity adviser

A first conversation with an annuity adviser is mostly about your circumstances rather than products. They will want to know how your pension is held, roughly what it is worth, when you want income to start, and whether anyone else depends on that income. Expect questions about your health and lifestyle too, because these can shape whether an enhanced annuity is worth exploring.

From there the adviser can explain how a single life annuity differs from one that continues for a spouse or partner, and what inflation protection would mean for the income you start with. They may show you illustrations based on different choices so you can see the trade-offs rather than deciding in the abstract. Nothing should be rushed, and you are free to go away and think.

If you are over fifty and have a defined contribution pension, Pension Wise offers free, impartial guidance that can help you understand your options before you speak to an adviser. That guidance is not a personal recommendation, so it works well alongside regulated advice rather than instead of it.

## How to check you are in safe hands

Financial advice in the UK is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Check that any adviser or firm is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm, on the FCA register. If something goes wrong you can complain to the firm, then to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme may protect you if an authorised firm fails.

Some pension decisions carry special rules. Advice on transferring out of a defined benefit (final salary) scheme above a set value must come from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions, and safeguarded benefits deserve particular care. Free, impartial guidance is available from Pension Wise for people over 50 with defined contribution pensions.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## Questions to ask the professional

Asking a few direct questions early tells you a lot about how a practice works:

- Are you authorised by the FCA to advise on annuities, and can you show me your entry on the FCA register?
- Will you compare annuity quotes across the whole market rather than only my current provider?
- Could my health or lifestyle mean I qualify for an enhanced annuity, and how would you assess that?
- How would inflation protection affect the income I receive, and is it worth considering for my situation?
- What happens to the income if I die, and can it continue for my spouse or partner?
- What are your charges, and how will you be paid for the advice?

## FAQs about annuity advisor near me

### Can an adviser near me get me a better annuity rate than my pension provider?

Annuity rates differ between providers, so comparing quotes across the market is a normal step. An adviser can shop around on your behalf rather than you accepting the first offer from your existing provider.

### What is an enhanced annuity?

An enhanced annuity pays a higher income if you have certain health conditions or lifestyle factors that may shorten your life expectancy. An adviser can help you work out whether you might qualify.

### Do I have to buy an annuity with my whole pension pot?

No, you can usually use part of your pot to buy an annuity and keep the rest invested or take it another way. An adviser can explain the options for your particular pension.

### How do I check that an annuity adviser is properly authorised?

Look up the adviser or firm on the FCA register to confirm they are authorised or an appointed representative of an authorised firm. If something goes wrong you can complain to the firm, then to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

### Is an annuity adviser the same as a pension adviser?

Annuity advice is a specialism within pension advice, so many pension advisers also deal with annuities. When you enquire, say clearly that you are considering an annuity so we introduce you to someone who works in that area.

### Can I speak to an adviser before I have decided anything?

Yes, and it is often sensible to do so early. An adviser can set out how annuities compare with other ways of taking your pension, so you are deciding with a clear picture rather than after you have already committed.

### What if I only want a small annuity?

You can usually use part of your pension pot to buy an annuity and leave the rest as it is. Tell the adviser how much of your pot you want to use so the advice matches what you actually intend to do.

### Should I combine my pensions?

Sometimes it makes life simpler and cheaper, but some older pensions have valuable guarantees or low charges. An adviser checks before recommending a transfer.
