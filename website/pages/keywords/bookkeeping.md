## What to understand about bookkeeping in the UK

Bookkeeping is not a regulated title in the UK, but anyone providing bookkeeping services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by HMRC or by a professional body. Membership of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) or the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT) shows a bookkeeper has trained to a recognised standard and agreed to a code of conduct.

Ask whether a bookkeeper holds professional indemnity insurance and which body supervises them. Good bookkeepers are also clear about where their work stops and an accountant's starts, for example on year-end accounts and tax advice.

## Questions to ask a bookkeeper

Asking a few direct questions early tells you a lot about how a practice works:

- Which body supervises you for anti-money-laundering purposes?
- Do you hold professional indemnity insurance?
- Are you a member of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) or the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT)?
- How often would you do the bookkeeping, and does that suit my volume of transactions?
- Can you help me catch up if I am behind, and how would you approach that?

## FAQs: bookkeeping services

### Do I need a qualified bookkeeper?

Bookkeeping is not a regulated title, but anyone providing bookkeeping services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes.

### What is the difference between a bookkeeper and an accountant?

A bookkeeper keeps your financial records up to date, from bank reconciliations and invoices to VAT returns and management reports. An accountant typically handles year-end accounts and tax advice. Good bookkeepers are clear about where their work stops and an accountant's starts.

### Can a bookkeeper help if I am behind with my records?

Yes, many bookkeepers offer catch-up work to bring your records up to date. Ask about this when you enquire, and explain how far back the work needs to go.

### Should I use cloud software for my bookkeeping?

Cloud software lets you see the same numbers as your bookkeeper, which can make communication easier. Ask the bookkeeper whether they work with cloud software and whether they can help you set it up.
