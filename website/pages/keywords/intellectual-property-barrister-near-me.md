## FAQs: an intellectual property barrister

### Do I need a solicitor to instruct an IP barrister?

Not necessarily. Barristers registered under the Public Access scheme can be instructed directly by members of the public for suitable matters. However, some cases may still require a solicitor, and the barrister can advise you on this.

### How can I check if a barrister is regulated?

You can check the Barristers' Register to confirm their practising certificate and whether they are authorised for direct public access.

### What is IPEC and does my case qualify?

IPEC is the Intellectual Property Enterprise Court, which handles smaller IP claims. It is designed to be more accessible for individuals and smaller businesses, with simplified procedures and cost limits.

### How quickly should I act if my IP is being infringed?

It's important to act fast on infringement, as delays can affect your ability to enforce your rights. Seek advice as soon as possible to understand your options and any urgent steps you need to take.

### Can I go straight to a barrister without a solicitor?

Often yes, through the Public Access scheme, if the barrister is registered and the matter is suitable. Some cases still need a solicitor, and the barrister will tell you if so.
