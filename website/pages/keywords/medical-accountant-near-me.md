## Choosing between the medical accountants you find nearby

A search for a medical accountant near me often comes from someone who wants to sit down with their accountant in person, or at least know they are on the same patch. That is a reasonable instinct, but proximity alone tells you nothing about whether a firm actually handles NHS pensions, locum income and practice accounts. Use the location to build a shortlist, then judge each firm on what they do rather than where they are.

When you compare, ask the same questions of each firm and note how clearly they answer. A firm that works with doctors routinely should be able to talk you through how the NHS pension annual allowance is assessed, how locum income from several sources is pulled together, and how partnership accounts differ from a salaried post. If the answers sound vague, that is useful information too.

It also helps to think about how you like to work. Some people want to hand over a shoebox of paperwork and meet once a year; others want to be able to ring with a question mid-year. Ask how the firm prefers to communicate, whether you would deal with the same person each time, and what they need from you to get started. The right fit is about more than the postcode.

## What to understand about medical accountants

In the UK, the word "accountant" on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can call themselves one. Titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises a firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled. iComply is not regulated and does not give professional advice — we simply make the introduction.
