Barristers usually agree a fee for a defined piece of work, such as a written advice, a conference or attendance at a hearing, often negotiated through their clerk. Fees reflect seniority, specialism and the time needed. The chambers will quote you, POA.

## How criminal barristers are usually instructed

In the traditional route, your solicitor instructs the barrister for you, and the barrister works from chambers with clerks who manage diaries and fees. Legal aid often covers barrister fees in criminal matters, so it is worth asking about eligibility early. Private instructions are also possible, and some barristers registered under the Public Access scheme can be instructed directly by members of the public for suitable matters.

Barristers in England and Wales are regulated by the Bar Standards Board (BSB). You can check the Barristers' Register to see whether someone holds a current practising certificate and whether they are authorised to accept instructions directly from the public. In Scotland the equivalent professionals are advocates, regulated through the Faculty of Advocates. iComply is not regulated and does not give legal advice — we simply make the introduction.

## FAQs: a criminal barrister

### Do I need a solicitor to instruct a criminal barrister?

Usually your solicitor instructs the barrister on your behalf, and the two work together on your case. Some barristers registered under the Public Access scheme can be instructed directly by members of the public for suitable matters, so it is worth asking.

### Can legal aid cover a criminal barrister's fees?

Legal aid often covers barrister fees in criminal matters, and eligibility depends on your circumstances and the type of case. Ask the barrister or your solicitor about legal aid early so you understand where you stand.

### How do I check that a barrister is properly regulated?

Barristers in England and Wales are regulated by the Bar Standards Board (BSB). You can check the Barristers' Register to see whether someone holds a current practising certificate and whether they are authorised to accept instructions directly from the public.

### What does price on application mean for a criminal barrister?

It means the barrister will quote you directly once they understand your case, rather than advertising a fixed figure. Ask for the quote in writing and check what it covers before you agree.

### What is the difference between a solicitor and a barrister?

Solicitors usually manage a case from start to finish and deal with clients day to day. Barristers specialise in advocacy and specialist advice, and are typically brought in for hearings or difficult legal questions.
