## FAQs: a contractor mortgage

### What is a contractor mortgage and why might I need one?

A contractor mortgage is a mortgage where the lender assesses your income based on your day rate and contract rather than your limited company accounts. If you're a contractor, this can make it easier to borrow because your income isn't shown in the same way as a salaried employee's.

### How can I check if a mortgage broker is authorised?

You can check whether a broker or adviser is authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) on the FCA register. They should be authorised or an appointed representative of an authorised firm, and they must tell you how they are paid.

### Does IR35 affect my mortgage application?

IR35 status can affect how lenders assess your income. Some lenders may treat income from inside IR35 contracts differently, so it's worth discussing your situation with a broker who understands contractor mortgages.

### What if I have a short contract history or gaps between contracts?

Some lenders are more flexible than others when it comes to contract history and gaps. A broker who deals with contractors can explain which lenders are likely to consider your situation.

### Can I rely on a broker calling themselves the top contractor mortgage specialist?

There is no official body that ranks mortgage brokers as top or best, so treat such wording as a marketing claim rather than a fact. Instead, check that the broker is authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm, and ask how they assess contractor income.

### Do I have to choose a broker who is physically near me?

No. What matters most is that the broker understands contractor mortgages and can explain how lenders will view your day rate, contract history and IR35 position. If you would rather deal with someone local, tell us when you enquire and we will factor that in.
