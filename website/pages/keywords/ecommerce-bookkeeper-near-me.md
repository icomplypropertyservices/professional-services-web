## What people searching for an ecommerce bookkeeper usually need

Selling online brings a particular kind of bookkeeping muddle, and most people looking for help are trying to get on top of it before it grows.

- Your marketplace payouts arrive net of fees, so the sales figure in your records never quite matches what landed in your bank account.
- Refunds and chargebacks come through at odd times and need to be matched back to the original order to keep your figures honest.
- Stock levels across platforms and your own storage drift apart, and you want the bookkeeping to reflect what you actually hold and what it cost.
- You sell in more than one currency and are unsure how to record conversions and fees without creating confusion at the end of the year.
- You are handing over a growing pile of platform exports and processor statements and want someone to make sense of them going forward.

## Understanding how ecommerce bookkeeping differs

An ecommerce bookkeeper reconciles marketplace payouts, payment processor statements, refunds and stock across the platforms you sell on. That means matching gross sales, platform fees and the net amount received, rather than simply recording the figure that appears in your bank feed. Integration apps can automate much of this, so it is worth asking how a bookkeeper would connect to your platforms and what still needs checking by hand.

Bookkeeping is not a regulated title in the UK, but anyone providing bookkeeping services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by HMRC or by a professional body. Membership of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers or the Association of Accounting Technicians shows a bookkeeper has trained to a recognised standard and agreed to a code of conduct. It is also sensible to ask where their work stops and an accountant's begins, particularly on year-end accounts and tax advice.

## FAQs: an e-commerce bookkeeper

### Can a bookkeeper handle marketplace payouts and processor fees together?

Yes, that is central to ecommerce bookkeeping. A bookkeeper reconciles gross sales, platform fees, refunds and the net amount received, so your records reflect what actually happened rather than just the bank balance.

### Should I look for someone local when I search for an ecommerce bookkeeper near me?

Local can be convenient for meeting in person, but ecommerce bookkeeping is often done remotely because the work happens in platform exports and accounting software. It is worth asking how they prefer to work before deciding how close they need to be.

### What does it mean that bookkeeping is not a regulated title?

It means anyone can call themselves a bookkeeper, but anyone providing bookkeeping services for a fee must still be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes by HMRC or a professional body. Asking which body supervises them, and whether they belong to the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers or the Association of Accounting Technicians, helps you judge their standing.

### Can a bookkeeper help with stock as well as sales?

Many can, and stock reconciliation affects your profit, so it is worth raising early. Tell them how you track stock across platforms and storage so they can say whether that falls within their work.

### Can a bookkeeper submit my VAT return?

Many can, through Making Tax Digital compatible software, once you authorise them. Check this is included in the scope before you start.
