## How to check you are in safe hands

"Architect" is a protected title in the UK. Only people on the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance. Many practices are also RIBA Chartered Practices. Architectural designers and technologists can do excellent work too, but they are not registered architects, so it is worth knowing which you are hiring.

Before you appoint anyone, check the ARB register and ask for a written appointment that sets out services, fees and responsibilities. Building regulations approval, planning permission and party wall matters are separate processes that your architect can help you navigate.

## Listed Building Architect Near Me: FAQs

### Do I always need listed building consent for work on a listed building?

Not all work requires consent, but any alteration that affects the character of the building as a building of special architectural or historic interest will need it. It's always best to check with your local planning authority before starting work, as unauthorised works are a criminal offence.

### What is a heritage statement and why is it needed?

A heritage statement is a document that describes the significance of the listed building and assesses the impact of your proposed works. It is often required to support a listed building consent application, and an architect experienced with historic buildings can prepare it for you.

### Can I use modern materials on a listed building?

Traditional materials are often important for preserving the character and fabric of a listed building, and using modern materials can sometimes cause damage. Your architect can advise on the most appropriate materials for your specific project and help you understand what the local authority is likely to accept.

### How do I check if someone is a registered architect?

You can check the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register online to confirm that someone is a registered architect. Only those on the register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance.

### Do I need an architect for an extension?

Not legally, but a registered architect brings design skill, planning experience and accountability. For simple work, an architectural technologist or designer may be enough. We can match you to the right level of help.
