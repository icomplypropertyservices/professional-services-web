## What to understand about divorce financial advice

Financial advice in the UK is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Check that any adviser or firm is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm, on the FCA register. Advisers must tell you whether their advice is independent or restricted, and how they charge.

## Checking financial advisers are qualified and regulated

If something goes wrong you can complain to the firm, then to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme may protect you if an authorised firm fails.

Advisers must tell you whether their advice is independent (considering the whole market) or restricted (limited to certain products or providers), and how they charge. Chartered status from the Chartered Insurance Institute or the Personal Finance Society, and certifications such as CFP, show advanced qualifications.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## FAQs about divorce financial advisor near me

### Do I need a financial adviser if I already have a solicitor?

A solicitor handles the legal process, but a financial adviser can help with the detailed financial analysis, especially pensions and future income. They often work together to ensure the settlement is fair and workable.

### What is a pension sharing order and why does it need expert input?

A pension sharing order is a court order that splits a pension between divorcing couples. Pensions are often the largest asset, so expert input is needed to value them correctly and decide how to divide them fairly.

### Can a financial adviser help me budget for life after divorce?

Yes, a divorce financial adviser can help you plan your future income and budget based on the settlement you receive. They can also help you understand the tax implications and how to manage your finances going forward.

### How is a divorce financial adviser different from a solicitor?

A solicitor handles the legal aspects of divorce, such as filing papers and negotiating a settlement. A financial adviser focuses on the financial analysis, especially pensions and future income, and often works alongside your solicitor to ensure the settlement is fair and workable.

### Can a divorce financial adviser help if we are not yet divorced?

Yes, many people seek financial advice early in the separation process to understand their options. An adviser can help you prepare for financial disclosure and consider how assets might be divided, even before the divorce is finalised.
