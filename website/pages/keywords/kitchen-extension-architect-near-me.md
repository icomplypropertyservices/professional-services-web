## FAQs about kitchen extension architect near me

### Do I need an architect for a kitchen extension?

Not always, but a registered architect can help you get the most from the space, especially if the layout is complex or you need planning permission. Architectural designers and technologists can also do excellent work, so it is worth checking which type of professional you are hiring.

### How do I check if someone is a registered architect?

You can check the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register online. Only people on that register can legally call themselves architects in the UK.

### Will my kitchen extension need planning permission?

Some extensions can be built under permitted development, but others need planning permission. Your architect can advise you on this and help you prepare an application if needed.

### What should I have ready before I contact an architect?

It helps to have a clear idea of what you want, any measurements or photos of the existing space, and a rough budget in mind. The more detail you can give, the better the professional can respond.

### Should the architect also design the kitchen itself?

Some architects will plan the kitchen layout as part of the extension design, while others leave the cabinetry and appliances to a kitchen specialist. It is worth agreeing early who is responsible for the layout, service positions and finishes so nothing falls between the two.

### How local does a kitchen extension architect need to be?

There is no rule, but someone who can reach your home easily is better placed to visit during design and construction, and may already know your local planning authority's approach. Ask how many site visits their appointment includes and whether travel is charged.

### Can I stay in my home while the extension is built?

Many people do, but losing the kitchen for a stretch is the hardest part. Talk to your architect about a temporary cooking arrangement and a design that phases the work sensibly, so you can plan around the disruption.

### Will an architect guarantee planning permission?

No one can guarantee planning permission. A good architect will explain local policy, the risks and how to give your application the best chance.
