## Checking pension advisers are qualified and regulated

Financial advice in the UK is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Check that any adviser or firm is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm, on the FCA register. If something goes wrong you can complain to the firm, then to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme may protect you if an authorised firm fails.

Some pension decisions carry special rules. Advice on transferring out of a defined benefit (final salary) scheme above a set value must come from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions, and safeguarded benefits deserve particular care. Free, impartial guidance is available from Pension Wise for people over 50 with defined contribution pensions.

"Consultant" is a broad title that anyone can use. Check what qualifications and registrations sit behind it, and whether the work you need is regulated.

## Warning signs to watch for

Be cautious if you notice any of the following:

- Firms not on the FCA register
- Pressure to transfer quickly
- Offers to access a pension before age 55 (rising to 57 from 2028)
- Cold calls about your pension

## FAQs: a pension consultant

### Do I need a pension consultant for a defined benefit transfer?

Advice on transferring out of a defined benefit or final salary scheme above a set value must come from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions. Below that value the rules differ, so it is worth confirming your position with the scheme before you enquire.

### Can a pension consultant help an employer with a workplace scheme?

Yes, pension consultants advise employers as well as individuals on scheme choice, scheme assessments and benefits strategy. Employer schemes also carry governance duties, and good communication helps staff engage with their pension.

### Where can I get free guidance on my pension options?

Pension Wise offers free, impartial guidance for people over fifty with defined contribution pensions. It is guidance rather than regulated advice on your specific circumstances, so it can sit alongside a conversation with an adviser.

### How do I check that a pension adviser is properly authorised?

Use the FCA register to check that the adviser or firm is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

### What is Pension Wise?

A free, impartial government-backed service offering guidance to people over 50 about their options for defined contribution pensions. It is guidance, not personal advice.
