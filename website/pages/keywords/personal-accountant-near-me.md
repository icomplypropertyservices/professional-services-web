## How to check you are in safe hands

In the UK, the word "accountant" on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can use it. Titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises a firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled.

## Choosing between a local personal accountant and doing it yourself

Searching for a personal accountant near me often comes at a turning point: a first tax return after going self-employed, a rental property that has tipped your affairs past the point where a simple form feels safe, or a letter from HMRC you would rather someone else answered. Wanting someone near you is sensible, because personal tax work usually starts with a conversation about your circumstances, and being able to drop in with a folder of paperwork can make that easier.

Distance is only one measure, though. A nearby practice that cannot explain payments on account in plain language will serve you less well than one a little further away that can, so weigh convenience against how clearly they answer your questions and whether they will put their quote in writing. It is also worth asking whether they will act as your agent with HMRC, since that is often the point of hiring someone rather than filing yourself.

At a first appointment for personal tax work, expect to talk through your income sources, bring your employment income details, investment statements and rental figures, and be asked about anything unusual such as a property sale or pension drawdown. A good accountant will explain what you can claim, what you may owe and when, and set out how their fees work before you commit. If anything feels rushed or unclear, that is worth noting.

## FAQs about personal accountant near me

### Do I need a chartered accountant for personal tax matters?

Not necessarily, but a protected title such as Chartered Accountant or Chartered Certified Accountant shows the person is bound by a professional body's rules. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises any accountant you consider.

### What should I bring to a first meeting with a personal accountant?

Bring details of your employment income, investment statements and rental figures, as these are the documents most personal accountants will ask for. It also helps to have any correspondence from HMRC to hand.

### Can a personal accountant deal with HMRC on my behalf?

Many accountants can act as your agent and deal with HMRC for you, but you should ask directly whether they offer this service. It can save you time and worry when letters arrive.

### How do I judge if a personal accountant near me is suitable?

Ask about their professional body, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance and how they handle complaints. A clear quote and a willingness to explain things in plain language are also good signs.

### Is it worth paying a personal accountant for a straightforward tax return?

Only you can judge that, but it often comes down to time and confidence. If your affairs are simple and you are comfortable with the return, doing it yourself is possible; if you have rental income, share sales or HMRC correspondence you do not understand, an accountant can take that on and explain it. Ask for a quote in writing so you can weigh the cost against what you would otherwise spend.

### Can I change accountants if the local one I choose is not right?

Yes, you are free to move to another practice, and your new accountant will usually handle the handover by contacting your previous one for your records. It helps to ask at the start how they would manage that if it ever arose. Keeping your own copies of previous returns makes any change simpler.

### Does a personal accountant near me also handle Self Assessment for a landlord?

Many personal accountants deal with rental income as part of Self Assessment, including what you can claim against it. Ask directly whether they handle property income and whether they will deal with HMRC on your behalf. Bring your rental figures and any mortgage interest statements to the first meeting.

### Can I switch accountant part-way through the year?

Yes. Your new accountant will contact the old one for professional clearance and any information they need. Tell us your timing and we will match you with a practice that can take over smoothly.
