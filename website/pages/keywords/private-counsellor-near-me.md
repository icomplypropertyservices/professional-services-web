## Checking counsellors are qualified and regulated

"Counsellor" and "psychotherapist" are not protected titles in the UK. To protect yourself, choose a practitioner on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. Practitioner psychologists, who use titles such as clinical or counselling psychologist, must be registered with the HCPC.

Accredited registers require training, supervision, a code of ethics and a complaints procedure. A good therapist will explain their approach, confidentiality and its limits in the first session.

## Questions to ask the counsellor

Once you are introduced, these questions help you decide whether the counsellor is right for you:

- What is your approach to counselling, and how does it work in practice?
- Are you on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS?
- What are your fees, and how do you handle cancellations or missed sessions?
- How many sessions do you usually suggest, and how often would we meet?
- What are your arrangements for confidentiality, and in what circumstances would you share information?
- Do you offer sessions online or by telephone if I can't attend in person?

## FAQs: a private counsellor

### How do I find a private counsellor near me?

You can search online, ask your GP or look at accredited registers such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. iComply can also introduce you to a UK counsellor in your area, who will then quote you directly.

### What should I check before choosing a private counsellor?

Check that the counsellor is on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. Ask about their training, approach, fees and cancellation policy, and make sure you feel comfortable talking to them.

### Can I see a private counsellor for anxiety or depression?

Yes, private counsellors work with a wide range of issues including anxiety and depression. They will discuss your needs with you and explain how they can help, and you can decide together whether to proceed.

### Do I need a referral from my GP to see a private counsellor?

No, you can approach a private counsellor directly without a GP referral. However, if you have significant health concerns, it's still a good idea to keep your GP informed.

### Can I get therapy on the NHS?

Yes. In England you can refer yourself to NHS Talking Therapies. Waiting times vary by area, which is why some people choose private therapy.
