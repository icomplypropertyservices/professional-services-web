## Why agreed value and limited mileage matter for classic cars

Classic car insurance often works differently from standard car cover. Agreed value means you and the insurer settle on the car's value before the policy starts, so there's no dispute later. Limited mileage policies are common because many classics cover low annual mileage, and that can be reflected in the premium.

## FAQs about classic car insurance near me

### Do I need an agreed value policy for my classic car?

If your classic is worth more than a standard market value would reflect, agreed value avoids disputes. It sets the payout in advance, so you know where you stand.

### Will limited mileage save me money?

Many classic car policies offer lower premiums for limited mileage because the car is used less. You'll need to estimate your annual mileage accurately.

### Does club membership help with insurance?

Some brokers may offer discounts or specialist schemes for members of recognised classic car clubs. It's worth mentioning your membership when you enquire.

### Can I insure a classic car that's off the road for restoration?

Yes, specialist policies exist for cars under restoration, often covering fire and theft. Ask the broker about the level of cover available while work is in progress.

### What is non-disclosure?

Failing to tell an insurer something relevant. It can reduce or void a claim, so answer questions fully and ask your broker if unsure.
