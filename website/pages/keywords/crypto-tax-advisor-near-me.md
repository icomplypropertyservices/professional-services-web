## Why keeping complete records matters

HMRC receives data from crypto exchanges, so it's important that your records match what has been reported. Gaps or errors can lead to questions, so keeping a full transaction history is essential.

If you have past gains that weren't disclosed, making a voluntary disclosure can reduce penalties compared with waiting for HMRC to approach you. A crypto tax advisor can help you prepare that disclosure accurately.

## How to check you are in safe hands

"Tax adviser" is not a protected title in the UK. The Chartered Institute of Taxation (CTA qualification) and the Association of Taxation Technicians (ATT) are the main specialist bodies, and many accountants and solicitors also advise on tax. Members of these bodies follow Professional Conduct in Relation to Taxation, a shared code covering honesty, confidentiality and dealing with HMRC.

Tax advisers who provide services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes. HMRC has also been tightening standards for paid tax agents, so it is reasonable to ask about qualifications, supervision and professional indemnity insurance before you go ahead.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## FAQs: a crypto tax adviser

### Do I need a specialist crypto tax advisor, or can my regular accountant help?

Some accountants are familiar with crypto, but crypto tax can be complex. A specialist can help you apply HMRC's rules correctly, especially for staking, DeFi and NFTs.

### What should I do if I haven't kept good records of my crypto transactions?

Try to gather as much information as you can from exchange statements and wallets. A crypto tax advisor can help you reconstruct your transaction history and work out your gains.

### Is crypto taxed in the UK?

Yes, HMRC treats cryptoassets as property for tax purposes, and you may need to pay Capital Gains Tax or Income Tax depending on your activity. A tax advisor can explain how the rules apply to your situation.

### How can I find a reputable crypto tax advisor near me?

### Is tax planning legal?

Using reliefs and allowances as Parliament intended is normal and legal. Aggressive avoidance schemes are a different matter and can carry serious risks. A qualified adviser will explain the difference clearly.
