## Checking a clinic is licensed and what that means

Clinics that offer IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback, so you can compare local options before you decide.

Simple tests such as blood hormone checks may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing, but any treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre. It's worth asking about licensing early on, along with whether counselling is offered, as it should be.

## Costs and quotes for a fertility clinic

Fertility treatment is often quoted per cycle, but medication, tests, embryo storage and add-ons can be extra. The HFEA advises patients to ask for a full, personalised costed plan. The clinic quotes you, POA.

## Fertility Clinic Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a referral from my GP to go to a fertility clinic?

Not always. Some clinics accept self-referrals for initial consultations and tests, while others may ask for a GP referral. It's worth asking the clinic directly when you enquire.

### How do I check a fertility clinic is properly licensed?

The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool lists every licensed clinic along with its inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback. You can use it to check any clinic you're considering.

### What treatments count as fertility treatment that needs a licence?

Treatments involving eggs, sperm or embryos, such as IVF, ICSI, donor treatment and egg or embryo freezing, must be carried out at a HFEA-licensed clinic. Simple tests like blood hormone checks may not need a licence.

### Will counselling be available at the clinic?

Counselling should be offered as part of fertility treatment, and it's a good question to ask when you first speak to a clinic. It can help you talk through the emotional side of investigations and treatment.

### How do I compare clinics?

Use the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool, which gives ratings, outcomes and patient feedback for licensed clinics.
