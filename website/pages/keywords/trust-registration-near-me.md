## FAQs: trust registration

### Does every trust need to be registered?

No. Most UK express trusts must be registered on HMRC's Trust Registration Service, and some non-UK trusts must be too, but not every trust is caught. A tax adviser can tell you whether your particular trust needs registering.

### What happens if a trust is registered late?

Penalties can apply for late registration, so it is better to deal with it promptly. A tax adviser can explain the position for your trust and help you put things right.

### Do I need to update the register after it is set up?

Yes. The register must be kept up to date, so changes such as new trustees or beneficiaries need to be recorded. A tax adviser can handle this for you or show you how it works.

### Can you help with a non-UK trust?

Some non-UK trusts must be registered on HMRC's Trust Registration Service. Tell us about the trust when you enquire and we can introduce you to a UK tax adviser who deals with this.

### Should I reply to HMRC before speaking to an adviser?

Note the deadline on the letter, but avoid sending a detailed reply before you have advice if the matter is complex. An adviser can often ask HMRC for more time where it is reasonable.
