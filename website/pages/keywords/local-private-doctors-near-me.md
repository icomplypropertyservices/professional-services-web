## What to understand about private doctors

Every doctor practising in the UK must be registered and hold a licence to practise with the General Medical Council (GMC). GPs also appear on the GMC's GP register, and you can search the GMC's online register by name. Private clinics providing regulated healthcare in England must register with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which inspects and publishes ratings; in Wales the regulator is Healthcare Inspectorate Wales, in Scotland Healthcare Improvement Scotland, and in Northern Ireland the RQIA.

A private GP does not replace your NHS GP. Good private clinics will ask for your consent to share consultation notes with your NHS practice, so your records stay complete and medication is prescribed safely. When comparing local private doctors, ask what is included in the fee and whether they will keep your NHS GP informed.

## Local Private Doctors Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a referral to see a private doctor?

Not usually. Private GPs typically see patients without a referral, but some specialists may ask for a letter from your NHS GP. It's best to check when you book.

### Will using a private doctor affect my NHS care?

No, you can still use NHS services. A private GP does not replace your NHS GP, and good clinics will share notes with your NHS practice so your records stay complete.

### How do I check if a private doctor is properly registered?

You can search the General Medical Council's online register by the doctor's name to confirm they hold a licence to practise. For clinics in England, you can also check the Care Quality Commission's register.

### Can I see a private doctor if I have private medical insurance?

Yes, many private doctors work with insurers, but you should check with your insurer first to confirm cover and any excess. Some doctors may ask you to pay upfront and claim back later.

### Can I choose a private doctor near my workplace rather than my home?

Yes, many people do this so appointments fit around their working day. When you enquire, tell us both locations and how far you are willing to travel, and we can introduce you to a suitable professional who quotes you directly.

### What if I need tests after seeing a local private doctor?

Ask at the consultation whether the practice can arrange the tests it recommends and how results will be explained to you. It is worth checking what is included in the fee and what is charged separately before you go ahead.

### Should I tell my NHS GP I have seen a private doctor?

It is sensible for your records to stay joined up, and good private clinics will ask your consent to share consultation notes with your NHS practice. That helps your NHS GP keep track of your care and prescribe safely.

### Will a private GP share my notes with my NHS GP?

With your consent, most do, and it is safer for you. Joined-up records help avoid duplicate tests and medication clashes.
