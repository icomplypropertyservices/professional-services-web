## Checking accountants are qualified and regulated

In the UK, the word "accountant" on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can use it. Titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises a firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled.

## What to understand about crypto and HMRC

HMRC treats cryptoassets as property for tax purposes, and disposals such as selling, swapping one coin for another or spending crypto can give rise to capital gains. The calculations use pooling rules, which is why a full transaction history from every exchange and wallet matters so much. Staking rewards and airdrops can create income tax as well, so they need to be looked at separately from gains.

HMRC can obtain information from exchanges, so gaps in your records are worth addressing rather than leaving. ## FAQs: a crypto accountant

### Do I need to declare crypto on my UK tax return?

If you have disposed of cryptoassets, for example by selling, swapping or spending them, any gain may need to be reported depending on your circumstances. Staking rewards and airdrops can also count as income. An accountant can look at your transactions and tell you what applies to you.

### What if I've lost access to an old exchange?

It happens, and it's worth mentioning when you enquire. Your transaction history may still be recoverable through records you hold or the exchange itself, and an accountant can advise on how to handle gaps. HMRC can obtain information from exchanges, so it's better to address missing records openly.

### How do I choose a crypto accountant near me?

Ask which professional body supervises the practice, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance and how complaints are handled. Check whether they are comfortable with the specific activity you have, such as staking or swaps, and ask them to explain their approach before you commit. Quotes are on application, so compare what each professional offers.

### Can I just use the figures from my exchange?

Exchange summaries can be a helpful starting point, but they may not apply HMRC's pooling rules or separate income from gains. Exporting complete histories from every exchange and wallet gives a clearer picture. An accountant can then work through the figures properly.
