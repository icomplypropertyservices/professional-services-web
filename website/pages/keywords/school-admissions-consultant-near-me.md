## Checking education consultants are qualified and regulated

Education consultancy is not regulated by a single body. Look for consultants with relevant teaching, admissions or special educational needs experience, and membership of organisations such as the Society of Education Consultants. Ask for references and a clear written agreement.

For school admission appeals, independent appeal panels follow the School Admission Appeals Code in England. A consultant can help you prepare, but they cannot guarantee a result.

"Consultant" is a broad title that anyone can use. Check what qualifications and registrations sit behind it, and whether the work you need is regulated.

## FAQs: a school admissions consultant

### Can a consultant get my child a school place?

No one can promise a school place. A consultant can help you understand the criteria, prepare a strong application and put together an appeal, but the decision rests with the school or the independent appeal panel.

### How do I choose a school admissions consultant near me?

Look for someone with relevant teaching, admissions or special educational needs experience, and ask about membership of organisations such as the Society of Education Consultants. Ask for references and a clear written agreement before you go ahead.

### Can you help with an appeal against a refused place?

Yes, a consultant can help you prepare your case and gather evidence for an appeal. Independent appeal panels follow the School Admission Appeals Code in England, and the outcome is never certain.
