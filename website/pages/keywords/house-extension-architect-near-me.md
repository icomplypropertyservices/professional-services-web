## How to check you are in safe hands

"Architect" is a protected title in the UK. Only people on the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance. Many practices are also RIBA Chartered Practices. Architectural designers and technologists can do excellent work too, but they are not registered architects, so it is worth knowing which you are hiring.

Before you appoint anyone, check the ARB register and ask for a written appointment that sets out services, fees and responsibilities. Building regulations approval, planning permission and party wall matters are separate processes that your architect can help you navigate.

## House Extension Architect Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need planning permission for my house extension?

Some extensions are covered by permitted development rights, but there are limits on size, height and placement. Your local planning authority can confirm whether you need a full application, and an architect can advise you on the rules for your area.

### What is the difference between an architect and an architectural designer?

Only people on the Architects Registration Board register can call themselves architects. Architectural designers and technologists can also design extensions and may be qualified through other routes, so it's worth checking their credentials and experience.

### Will I need a structural engineer for my extension?

Often yes, especially if you're removing walls or creating large openings. A structural engineer ensures the design is safe and meets building regulations, and your architect can usually recommend one or coordinate their work.

### How much does a house extension architect cost?

Fees vary widely depending on the scope of work, the complexity of the project and your location. Architects quote on application, so you'll need to discuss your specific project with them to get an accurate figure.

### Will an architect guarantee planning permission?

No one can guarantee planning permission. A good architect will explain local policy, the risks and how to give your application the best chance.
