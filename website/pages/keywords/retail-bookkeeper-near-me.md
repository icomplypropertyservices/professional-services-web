## What to understand about retail bookkeeping

Bookkeeping is not a regulated title in the UK, but anyone providing bookkeeping services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by HMRC or by a professional body. Membership of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) or the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT) shows a bookkeeper has trained to a recognised standard and agreed to a code of conduct.

Good bookkeepers are clear about where their work stops and an accountant's starts, for example on year-end accounts and tax advice. Ask whether they hold professional indemnity insurance and which body supervises them.

## Retail Bookkeeper Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a bookkeeper who is local to my shop?

A local bookkeeper can visit your shop to see how your till and stock systems work, which can be helpful for retail. However, many bookkeepers work remotely and can still reconcile EPOS data and prepare VAT returns. It depends on how hands-on you want them to be.

### How do bookkeepers handle stock counts for a retail business?

Bookkeepers use stock counts to check that the value of stock in your accounts matches what is actually on the shelves. They may ask you to do regular counts or help you set up a simple system. This keeps your accounts accurate and can highlight shrinkage or ordering issues.

### Can a bookkeeper help with VAT returns for a shop?

Yes, preparing VAT returns is a common part of retail bookkeeping. They will need your sales records, including till and card payment data, and purchase invoices. If you are unsure about VAT rules, ask whether they can explain them or whether you need an accountant.

### What should I check before hiring a retail bookkeeper?

Ask which body supervises them for anti-money-laundering purposes, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how they work with EPOS and stock counts. Also clarify where their work ends and an accountant's begins. iComply can introduce you to a bookkeeper who can answer these questions.

### Can a bookkeeper submit my VAT return?

Many can, through Making Tax Digital compatible software, once you authorise them. Check this is included in the scope before you start.
