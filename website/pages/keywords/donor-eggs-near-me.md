## Qualifications and registration to look for

Clinics offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback.

Simple tests such as blood hormone checks may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing, but treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre.

## FAQs: donor eggs

### Do I need a referral to get donor eggs at a clinic?

Not usually. Most fertility clinics accept self-referrals, but some may ask for information from your GP. It is best to check with the clinic directly when you enquire.

### Is donor egg treatment regulated in the UK?

Yes. You can check a clinic's licence and inspection ratings using the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool.

### Will the child be legally mine if I use donor eggs?

Legal parenthood depends on your circumstances and the rules that apply. Implications counselling is recommended so you understand your position, and the clinic should explain what you need to do.

### How are success rates for donor eggs reported?

Success rates for donor egg treatment reflect the age of the donor rather than the recipient. Clinics should explain their figures clearly, and you can compare them using the HFEA's tool.

### How do I compare clinics?

Use the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool, which gives ratings, outcomes and patient feedback for licensed clinics.
