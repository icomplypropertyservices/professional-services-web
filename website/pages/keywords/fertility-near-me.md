## What to understand about fertility clinics near you

Clinics that offer IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback, so you can compare local options on facts rather than claims.

Simple tests such as blood hormone checks may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing, but any treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre. Both partners may need tests, and age can affect which options are suitable, so it's worth asking what investigations are relevant for you.

## Questions to ask the fertility clinic

Once you are introduced, these questions help you decide whether the fertility clinic is right for you:

- Are you licensed by the HFEA for the treatment I'm interested in?
- What tests would you recommend for me and my partner at this stage?
- How do you decide which treatment options are suitable for someone of my age?
- Can you explain the success rates for people in a similar situation to mine?
- What happens during the first consultation and what does it involve?
- How do I access your clinic's HFEA inspection ratings and patient feedback?

## Costs and quotes for fertility support

Fertility treatment is often quoted per cycle, but medication, tests, embryo storage and add-ons can be extra. The HFEA advises patients to ask for a full, personalised costed plan. The clinic quotes you, POA.

Be careful with add-ons. The HFEA rates many of them for evidence of benefit, and some have little or none.

## FAQs about fertility near me

### Do I need a referral to see a fertility clinic near me?

Not always. Some clinics accept self-referrals for initial consultations, while others may ask for a letter from your GP. It's best to ask the clinic directly when you enquire.

### How do I know if a local clinic is properly licensed?

Clinics offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the HFEA. You can check a clinic's licence and see its inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback using the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool.

### What can I expect at a first fertility appointment?

The clinic will usually ask about your medical history and may arrange tests for you and your partner. They should explain what the tests involve and discuss which options might be suitable for you.

### Can I get fertility tests without going to a licensed clinic?

Simple tests such as blood hormone checks may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing. However, any treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre.

### How do I compare clinics?

Use the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool, which gives ratings, outcomes and patient feedback for licensed clinics.

### How do I find fertility support near me?

### Do I have to accept a quote?
