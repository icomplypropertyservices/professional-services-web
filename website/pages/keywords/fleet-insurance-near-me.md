## Fleet cover versus insuring each vehicle separately

People searching for fleet insurance near me are often weighing up whether to move away from insuring each vehicle on its own policy. Separate policies can mean several renewal dates, several sets of paperwork and several excesses to keep track of, while a fleet arrangement brings the vehicles together under one policy. The right answer depends on how many vehicles you run and how they are used, which is exactly what a broker will want to discuss.

It is worth being clear that fleet cover is not simply a discount for having more than one vehicle. Insurers set their own thresholds for what they will treat as a fleet, and the terms can differ in how they handle drivers, excesses and claims. A broker can compare how your vehicles would sit under a fleet policy against keeping them separate, so you can judge which arrangement suits your business rather than assuming one is always better.

## FAQs: fleet insurance

### Do I need a minimum number of vehicles for fleet insurance?

Fleet size thresholds vary between insurers, so there is no single number that applies everywhere. A broker can tell you whether your vehicles would be accepted as a fleet or whether separate policies would suit you better.

### Can fleet insurance cover drivers who are not named on the policy?

Driver policies matter, and how unnamed or occasional drivers are treated depends on the insurer and the policy terms. Ask the broker to explain exactly who is covered and under what conditions before you commit.

### Would telematics help my business vehicles?

Telematics can help by giving insight into how vehicles are driven, and some insurers take it into account. Whether it benefits you depends on your fleet and how it is used, so raise it with the broker when you speak.

### How do I check that a broker is properly authorised?

You can also ask whether they belong to the British Insurance Brokers' Association or hold Chartered status from the Chartered Insurance Institute.

### Is fleet insurance always cheaper than separate policies?

Not necessarily. Whether a fleet policy works out better depends on your vehicles, drivers and claims history, and insurers price these differently. Ask the broker to explain the comparison for your circumstances rather than assuming one route is cheaper.

### What happens at a first conversation with a fleet insurance broker?

Expect questions about your vehicles, what they are used for, who drives them and any claims history, followed by an explanation of how fleet cover would work for you. The broker should set out key features, exclusions and how they are paid before you decide whether to proceed.

### Can I move my existing vehicles onto a fleet policy mid-term?

That depends on your current policies and their terms, so raise it with the broker before making any change. They can explain what cancelling or amending existing cover would involve and whether a fleet arrangement could start at a sensible point for you.

### What is non-disclosure?

Failing to tell an insurer something relevant. It can reduce or void a claim, so answer questions fully and ask your broker if unsure.
