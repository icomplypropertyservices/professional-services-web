## Child Arrangements Lawyers Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a solicitor to agree child arrangements?

Not necessarily. Many parents agree arrangements between themselves or through mediation. A solicitor can help you understand your options and put any agreement on a clear footing, and court involvement is usually a last resort.

### What is a mediation information meeting?

It is a session that usually comes before a court application about children, where a mediator explains what mediation involves and whether it might suit your situation. A solicitor can tell you what to expect and whether any exemptions might apply.

### How do the courts decide child arrangements?

The courts focus on the child's welfare when making decisions about where children live and how they spend time with each parent. A solicitor can explain how that principle applies to your circumstances.
