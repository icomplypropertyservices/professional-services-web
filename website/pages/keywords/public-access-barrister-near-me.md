## FAQs about public access barrister near me

### What is a public access barrister?

A public access barrister is a barrister registered with the Bar Standards Board to accept instructions directly from members of the public for suitable matters. This means you can instruct them without going through a solicitor.

### Can I instruct a barrister directly for any case?

Not always. The barrister will assess whether your matter is suitable for public access. Some cases may require a solicitor's involvement, and the barrister will advise you accordingly.

### How do I check if a barrister is registered for public access?

You can check the Barristers' Register to see whether a barrister holds a current practising certificate and is authorised to accept instructions directly from the public.

### What are the fees for a public access barrister?

Fees are usually fixed per task, but they vary depending on the barrister and the complexity of the work. You will receive a client care letter outlining the terms before any work begins.

### Can I go straight to a barrister without a solicitor?

Often yes, through the Public Access scheme, if the barrister is registered and the matter is suitable. Some cases still need a solicitor, and the barrister will tell you if so.
