## How to check you are in safe hands

Bookkeeping is not a regulated title in the UK, but anyone providing bookkeeping services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by HMRC or by a professional body. Membership of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) or the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT) shows a bookkeeper has trained to a recognised standard and agreed to a code of conduct.

Ask whether a bookkeeper holds professional indemnity insurance and which body supervises them. Good bookkeepers are also clear about where their work stops and an accountant's starts, for example on year-end accounts and tax advice.

## Sage Bookkeeper Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a bookkeeper who is a Sage specialist?

If you already use Sage, a bookkeeper who knows the software can work with your existing records without you having to change systems. That is especially useful for older desktop set-ups, where the way records are kept and backed up differs from cloud accounting.

### Can a bookkeeper help with payroll in Sage?

Yes, many bookkeepers handle payroll in Sage, but you should ask specifically about their experience with the payroll module. It is worth confirming they can take on payroll alongside your other bookkeeping needs.

### What should I check before sharing my Sage file with a bookkeeper?

Ask how they will access your file, what backups they will make and how access will be controlled. Good bookkeepers are clear about security and will explain their process for keeping your data safe.

### Is a Sage bookkeeper regulated?

Bookkeeping is not a regulated title in the UK, but anyone providing bookkeeping services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by HMRC or by a professional body. Membership of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) or the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT) shows a bookkeeper has trained to a recognised standard and agreed to a code of conduct.

### What is the difference between a bookkeeper and an accountant?

A bookkeeper records day-to-day transactions and keeps your books accurate. An accountant typically prepares year-end accounts, tax returns and gives tax advice. Many businesses use both, and they work best when they talk to each other.
