## Qualifications and registration to look for

Bookkeeping is not a regulated title in the UK, but anyone providing bookkeeping services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by HMRC or by a professional body. Membership of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) or the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT) shows a bookkeeper has trained to a recognised standard and agreed to a code of conduct.

Ask whether a bookkeeper holds professional indemnity insurance and which body supervises them. Good bookkeepers are also clear about where their work stops and an accountant's starts, for example on year-end accounts and tax advice.

## Comparing bookkeepers nearby without guessing

When you search for bookkeepers near me, you are usually weighing up several local people at once rather than looking for a single recommendation. That is a sensible way to go about it, because the right fit depends on your trade, how your records are kept and how much contact you want during the month. Rather than assuming anyone is the best or cheapest, ask each bookkeeper the same set of questions and compare the answers side by side.

A useful test is to ask how they would handle your particular mix of work, such as whether they would take on your VAT returns and payroll as well as the day-to-day recording, and where their work would stop and an accountant's would begin. Ask which body supervises them for anti-money-laundering purposes and whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, then note how clearly each one explains it. Clear answers to straightforward questions tell you more than any single figure quoted.

Bear in mind that quotes are given on application because the work varies so much from one business to the next. A bookkeeper who asks about your turnover, your software and how far behind your records are before quoting is taking the right approach. Compare the scope of what each quote covers, not just the headline figure, so you are weighing like with like.

It also helps to think about how you would actually work together month to month. If you would rather drop off a folder of receipts in person, someone nearby makes obvious sense, while a bookkeeper who works mainly online may suit you better if your paperwork is already digital. Say which you prefer when you enquire so the introduction is a useful one.

## Warning signs to watch for

Be cautious if you notice any of the following:

- No plan for handing records back if you part ways
- Claims to give tax advice they are not qualified to give
- Unclear access arrangements for your bank feeds and software
- No written agreement about scope and deadlines

## FAQs about bookkeepers near me

### Do I need a bookkeeper if I already use accounting software?

Software records what you enter, but someone still needs to check it, reconcile the bank account and keep everything consistent. A bookkeeper can take that on so your figures stay accurate through the year, which usually makes year end quicker and less costly.

### Can a bookkeeper help if my records are already behind?

Yes, catching up on overdue records is common work for bookkeepers. Tell them how far behind things are when you enquire so they can judge the time involved and quote accordingly.

### Should I choose a bookkeeper who is a member of a professional body?

It is not a legal requirement, but membership of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers or the Association of Accounting Technicians shows the person has trained to a recognised standard and agreed to a code of conduct. It is a reasonable question to ask, along with who supervises them for anti-money-laundering purposes.

### Will a bookkeeper also deal with my tax return?

Not necessarily. Bookkeepers record transactions, reconcile accounts and often prepare VAT returns and payroll, while tax advice and year-end accounts usually fall to an accountant. Ask upfront where their work stops so there are no surprises later.

### How do I compare several local bookkeepers fairly?

Ask each one the same questions about supervision, insurance, the work included and where their work stops and an accountant's begins. Then compare what each quote actually covers rather than the headline figure alone, since the scope of the work can differ a good deal.

### Why are bookkeeping quotes given on application?

The work depends on your circumstances, such as your turnover, whether you are VAT registered, whether you have employees and how far behind your records are. A bookkeeper needs those details before they can say what the job involves and quote for it.

### Does it matter whether a bookkeeper is local?

It is not a legal requirement, but being nearby can make it easier to drop off paperwork or sit down together, which many people find reassuring. If your records are already kept digitally, working with someone further away may suit you just as well.

### Can a bookkeeper submit my VAT return?

Many can, through Making Tax Digital compatible software, once you authorise them. Check this is included in the scope before you start.
