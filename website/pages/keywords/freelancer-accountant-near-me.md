## Checking accountants are qualified and regulated

In the UK, the word "accountant" on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can use it. Titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises a firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled.

iComply is not a regulator and does not provide the professional service itself. We introduce you to professionals who hold their own registrations, and we encourage you to check them on the public register.

## FAQs about freelancer accountant near me

### Do I need an accountant if I'm a freelancer with simple tax affairs?

Not necessarily, but an accountant can help you avoid common pitfalls, claim allowable expenses you might miss, and plan for VAT or incorporation. If your income is straightforward, you might only need one-off advice.

### What's the difference between a sole trader and a limited company for a freelancer?

As a sole trader you're personally liable for business debts and file a Self Assessment tax return; as a limited company you're a director and the company is separate, with different tax and reporting rules. An accountant can explain which suits your circumstances.

### How does Making Tax Digital affect freelancers?

Making Tax Digital for Income Tax will require many self-employed people and landlords to keep digital records and use compatible software to submit updates. An accountant can tell you when it applies to you and how to prepare.

### Can an accountant help if I work with overseas clients?

Yes, an accountant can advise on how foreign income is taxed in the UK, any double taxation relief, and how to record it correctly. They can also help with platform income and any reporting obligations.
