## Equity Release Advisor Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need to use an adviser near me?

Not necessarily, but a local adviser can meet you face to face and may have a better understanding of property values in your area. Many advisers also offer telephone or video appointments, so you can choose what suits you.

### What is the difference between a lifetime mortgage and a home reversion plan?

A lifetime mortgage is a loan secured against your home where you retain ownership, while a home reversion plan involves selling a share of your home for a lump sum or regular income. An adviser can explain the pros and cons of each.

### Will equity release affect my benefits or tax position?

Equity release can affect means-tested benefits, and the money released is generally tax-free but may affect your estate for inheritance tax. It's important to discuss your circumstances with the adviser and possibly a benefits adviser or tax specialist.

### Can I involve my family in the decision?

Yes, many advisers encourage you to involve family members, and you can bring them to appointments. It can be helpful to have a relative with you to ask questions and understand the long-term implications.

### Should I choose an adviser who is close by, or does distance matter less than I think?

Being nearby can make face-to-face meetings easier, especially if you'd like the adviser to visit you at home. But the quality of the advice matters more than the postcode, so it's fine to consider an adviser further away if they explain things in a way you understand and meet the same regulatory standards.

### What should I have ready for a first meeting with a local equity release adviser?

It helps to know the rough value of your home, any outstanding mortgage or secured borrowing, and what you'd like the released money to do. Bring details of your income and any benefits you receive, plus a note of your questions, and consider asking a family member to come with you.

### If an adviser visits me at home, does that change anything about the advice?

The setting shouldn't change the standards the advice has to meet, as equity release advice is regulated by the FCA wherever it takes place. What matters is that you understand the recommendation, have time to think, and still get your own solicitor to explain the legal documents before you sign.

### Will I still own my home?

With a lifetime mortgage, yes. With a home reversion plan, you sell part or all of your home but keep the right to live there.
