## FAQs: income protection insurance

### Do I need income protection insurance if I am self-employed?

Income protection can be particularly valuable for self-employed people, as you may not have employer sick pay or other support if you cannot work. A broker can explain how cover works for your situation.

### How is income protection insurance different from critical illness cover?

Income protection pays a regular income if you cannot work due to illness or injury, while critical illness cover pays a lump sum on diagnosis of a specified condition. A broker can help you understand the differences and decide what suits you.

### Can I get income protection insurance if I have a pre-existing medical condition?

Insurers may offer cover with exclusions or at a higher premium, depending on the condition. A broker can approach insurers to find out what is available for you.

### How do I check that a broker is authorised?

You can check on the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) register to see if a broker is authorised or an appointed representative. Many brokers also belong to the British Insurance Brokers' Association (BIBA).

### What is non-disclosure?

Failing to tell an insurer something relevant. It can reduce or void a claim, so answer questions fully and ask your broker if unsure.
