## The title 'accountant' is not protected on its own

In the UK, anyone can call themselves an accountant, so the letters after a name matter. Titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. ## Ecommerce Accountant Near Me: FAQs

### Why does an ecommerce seller need a specialist accountant rather than a general one?

Online selling brings marketplace fees, stock movements and multi-currency sales into the accounts in ways a general practice may not see often. An accountant who deals with these regularly is more likely to recognise the questions to ask and the records to keep.

### Can an accountant help with VAT on sales to the EU?

VAT on overseas sales can be complex, and the rules differ depending on where your customers are and how you hold stock. A professional can explain how the rules apply to your particular sales and what records you need to keep.

### What records should I bring to a first conversation?

It helps to have your platform statements, a summary of stock on hand, and any existing accounts or tax returns. The clearer the picture of your sales and costs, the more useful the first conversation will be.

### How do I judge whether an accountant is right for my business?

Ask about their experience with the platforms you sell on, how they handle stock and multi-currency sales, and which body supervises them. Compare how clearly they answer and whether their quote explains what is included, rather than relying on a single headline figure.

### Do I need a chartered accountant?

Not always. For simple Self Assessment a qualified, supervised accountant may be enough. For audits, complex group structures or specialist tax work, a chartered or certified firm is often the safer choice. We can match you to either.
