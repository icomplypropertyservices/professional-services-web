## Understanding the architect's role in a basement conversion

In the UK, "architect" is a protected title. Only people on the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance. Many practices are also RIBA Chartered Practices. Architectural designers and technologists can do excellent work too, but they are not registered architects, so it's worth knowing which you are hiring.

Before you appoint anyone, check the ARB register and ask for a written appointment that sets out services, fees and responsibilities. Building regulations approval, planning permission and party wall matters are separate processes that your architect can help you navigate. Waterproofing design is critical for basements, and some councils restrict basement conversions, so local knowledge matters.

## Basement Conversion Architect Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need planning permission for a basement conversion?

It depends on your project and local council rules. Some basement conversions fall under permitted development, while others need planning permission, and some councils have specific restrictions. Your architect can advise you on what applies to your situation.

### Why is waterproofing so important in a basement conversion?

Waterproofing is critical because basements are below ground and prone to damp. A well-designed waterproofing system protects your new space and prevents future problems. Your architect should incorporate this into the design from the outset.

### What is a party wall agreement and will I need one?

A party wall agreement is a legal agreement between you and your neighbours when work affects a shared wall or structure. Basement excavations often trigger this requirement. Your architect can help you navigate the process.

### How can I check if someone is a registered architect?

You can check the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register online. Only those on the register can call themselves architects. It's also worth asking about professional indemnity insurance and whether they are an RIBA Chartered Practice.

### Will an architect guarantee planning permission?

No one can guarantee planning permission. A good architect will explain local policy, the risks and how to give your application the best chance.
