## Qualifications and registration to look for

"Counsellor" and "psychotherapist" are not protected titles in the UK. To protect yourself, choose a practitioner on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. Practitioner psychologists, who use titles such as clinical or counselling psychologist, must be registered with the HCPC.

Accredited registers require training, supervision, a code of ethics and a complaints procedure. A good therapist will explain their approach, confidentiality and its limits in the first session.

## Anxiety Counselling Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a referral from my GP to see a counsellor for anxiety?

No, you do not usually need a referral to see a counsellor privately. You can contact a counsellor directly and arrange an initial session to talk about what you need.

### What is the difference between a counsellor and a psychologist for anxiety?

Counsellors and psychotherapists are not protected titles in the UK, while practitioner psychologists must be registered with the HCPC. Both may help with anxiety, but their training and the way they work can differ, so it is worth asking about their approach.

### How do I know if a counsellor is properly qualified to help with anxiety?

Look for a practitioner on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. Accredited registers require training, supervision, a code of ethics and a complaints procedure, which gives you some protection.

### Will counselling for anxiety involve talking about my physical symptoms?

Yes, physical symptoms such as a racing heart, tense muscles or feeling sick are common with anxiety, and a counsellor will usually want to understand how anxiety affects you physically as well as mentally. This helps them suggest coping strategies that address the whole picture.

### What is the difference between counselling and psychotherapy?

The terms overlap. Counselling often focuses on a specific problem over a shorter period, while psychotherapy may explore deeper patterns over a longer time. A good practitioner will explain their approach.
