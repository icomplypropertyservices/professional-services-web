## FAQs: a broker for private health insurance

### Will a broker near me cost me anything?

Brokers are paid in different ways, and they must tell you how they are paid. Some charge a fee, some receive commission from the insurer, and some do both. Ask the broker to explain their charges clearly before you proceed.

### Can a broker help if I have a pre-existing condition?

Yes, a broker can explain how insurers are likely to treat a pre-existing condition, which is usually excluded. They can also talk you through the difference between full medical underwriting and moratorium underwriting, so you understand what might be covered.

### How do I check that a broker is authorised?

You can check whether a broker is authorised by the FCA on the FCA register. Many brokers also belong to the British Insurance Brokers’ Association, and some hold Chartered status from the Chartered Insurance Institute, which you can ask about.

### What should I have ready before I speak to a broker?

It helps to have details of any medical conditions, the hospitals you would like to use, and a rough idea of your budget. Being open about your health and what you want from the policy will help the broker give you accurate information.

### Is a broker more expensive than buying direct?

Not necessarily. Brokers can access insurers and schemes not available directly, and help with claims. The right choice depends on how complex your needs are.
