## Qualifications and registration to look for

Solicitors in England and Wales are regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA), and you can check an individual or firm on the SRA's register. In Scotland solicitors are regulated by the Law Society of Scotland, and in Northern Ireland by the Law Society of Northern Ireland. Regulated firms must hold professional indemnity insurance and have a complaints procedure; if that does not resolve things, the Legal Ombudsman can look at service complaints in England and Wales.

The word "lawyer" is not protected on its own, so check that whoever handles your matter is a solicitor, a chartered legal executive (CILEx) or another authorised professional. Reserved activities such as conducting court litigation and certain probate and conveyancing work can only be carried out by authorised people.

## Understanding financial orders and mediation

Divorce itself does not end financial claims between you and your former partner; only a financial order can do that. A solicitor can help you apply for a financial order so that the settlement is legally binding and gives you both certainty.

Mediation is usually required before a court will consider certain applications, and it can help you reach agreement without going to court. Full financial disclosure is essential so that any settlement is based on an accurate picture of both parties' finances.

## FAQs about divorce solicitor near me

### Do I need a solicitor to get divorced?

You can apply for a divorce without a solicitor, but a solicitor can guide you through the process, help you avoid common pitfalls, and advise on financial and children matters. Many people find that professional advice saves time and reduces stress.

### What is a financial order and why do I need one?

A financial order is a legally binding court order that sets out how finances are divided. Without one, your former partner could potentially make a financial claim against you in the future, even after the divorce is finalised.

### Is mediation compulsory before I can go to court?

In many cases, you will be expected to attend a mediation information and assessment meeting before a court will hear certain applications. Mediation can help you reach agreement without the expense and stress of court.

### How do I check that a solicitor is properly regulated?

In Scotland, check with the Law Society of Scotland, and in Northern Ireland with the Law Society of Northern Ireland.

### How do I check if a divorce solicitor is properly regulated?

In England and Wales, solicitors are regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA), and you can check an individual or firm on the SRA's register.

### What should I bring to my first meeting with a divorce solicitor?

It helps to bring any relevant documents, such as your marriage certificate, details of your finances, and any correspondence from your former partner or the court. A brief timeline of key events can also be useful. The solicitor will guide you through what else they need.

### Can I get legal aid?

Legal aid is still available for some criminal, family (especially where there is domestic abuse), housing and immigration matters, subject to means and merits tests. A matched firm can tell you whether you are likely to qualify.
