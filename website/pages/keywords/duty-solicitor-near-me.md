Legal fees depend on the type of matter, its complexity and who does the work. Firms commonly charge hourly rates, fixed fees for defined stages, or (in some claims) conditional fee arrangements. ## Qualifications and registration to look for

Solicitors in England and Wales are regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA), and you can check an individual or firm on the SRA's register. In Scotland solicitors are regulated by the Law Society of Scotland, and in Northern Ireland by the Law Society of Northern Ireland. Regulated firms must hold professional indemnity insurance and have a complaints procedure; if that does not resolve things, the Legal Ombudsman can look at service complaints in England and Wales.

The word "lawyer" is not protected on its own, so check that whoever handles your matter is a solicitor, a chartered legal executive (CILEx) or another authorised professional. Reserved activities such as conducting court litigation and certain probate and conveyancing work can only be carried out by authorised people.

## FAQs: a duty solicitor

### Is duty solicitor advice really free at the police station?

Yes — police-station advice through the duty solicitor scheme is free regardless of your income. That applies whether you are arrested or asked to attend a voluntary interview.

### Can I ask for a different solicitor instead of the duty solicitor?

You can ask for your own solicitor instead of the duty solicitor. It is worth saying so clearly to the police so they can arrange it.

### What if I need advice outside normal office hours?

Police-station matters can arise at any time, and duty solicitor schemes are set up to cover them. Tell us when the matter is happening so we can introduce you to a solicitor who can respond.

### How do I check that the person advising me is a solicitor?

Solicitors in England and Wales are regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority, and you can check an individual or firm on the SRA's register. In Scotland it is the Law Society of Scotland, and in Northern Ireland the Law Society of Northern Ireland.

### Does it matter which police station or court is nearest to me?

It helps to know the location before you enquire, because a solicitor needs to cover that police station or court. Tell us the area and we can introduce you to someone who works there.

### Can I get legal aid?

Legal aid is still available for some criminal, family (especially where there is domestic abuse), housing and immigration matters, subject to means and merits tests. A matched firm can tell you whether you are likely to qualify.
