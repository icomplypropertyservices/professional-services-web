## How to check you are in safe hands

Conveyancing in England and Wales is a reserved legal activity. It can be carried out by solicitors regulated by the SRA or by licensed conveyancers regulated by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers (CLC). Both must hold professional indemnity insurance and follow strict rules on handling client money. In Scotland, property transactions are handled by solicitors under Scottish law.

Many lenders only work with conveyancers on their panel, and the Law Society's Conveyancing Quality Scheme (CQS) is a common accreditation. Check that your chosen firm is on your lender's panel before you instruct them.

## Best Accredited Conveyancer Near Me: FAQs

### What does 'accredited' mean for a conveyancer?

Accreditation usually means the firm or individual holds a recognised quality mark, such as the Law Society's Conveyancing Quality Scheme (CQS). It shows they meet certain standards, but you should still check their regulation and experience.

### How do I check if a conveyancer is regulated?

You can check the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA) register for solicitors or the Council for Licensed Conveyancers (CLC) register for licensed conveyancers. Both regulators maintain online lists of regulated professionals.

### Why does 'near me' matter for conveyancing?

Conveyancing can be done remotely, but some people prefer a local conveyancer for face-to-face meetings and easier document handling. Being nearby can also make it simpler to drop off or collect paperwork if needed.
