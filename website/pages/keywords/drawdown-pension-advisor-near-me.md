## How to check you are in safe hands

Financial advice in the UK is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Check that any adviser or firm is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm, on the FCA register. If something goes wrong you can complain to the firm, then to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme may protect you if an authorised firm fails.

Some pension decisions carry special rules. Advice on transferring out of a defined benefit (final salary) scheme above a set value must come from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions, and safeguarded benefits deserve particular care. Free, impartial guidance is available from Pension Wise for people over 50 with defined contribution pensions.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## What to understand about drawdown advice

Drawdown lets you take a flexible income from your pension while it remains invested, but the value can go down as well as up. The rate at which you withdraw affects how long your money lasts, and tax applies to withdrawals beyond your tax-free cash. A local adviser can help you set a sustainable withdrawal plan.

You can check that any adviser or firm is authorised on the FCA register. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme may protect you if an authorised firm fails. Free, impartial guidance is also available from Pension Wise for people with defined contribution pensions who are approaching retirement.

## Drawdown Pension Advisor Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a local adviser for drawdown?

Not necessarily, but meeting in person can help you build a relationship and discuss your options clearly. Many advisers also offer remote meetings, so you can choose what suits you.

### What is the difference between drawdown and an annuity?

Drawdown keeps your pension invested and lets you take a flexible income, while an annuity converts your pot into a fixed income for life. Each has different risks and benefits, and an adviser can help you compare them.

### Can I take tax-free cash and still use drawdown?

Yes, you can usually take up to a quarter of your pension as tax-free cash and put the rest into drawdown. The withdrawals you take beyond the tax-free cash are taxed as income.

### What if I have a final salary pension?

If you are considering transferring out of a defined benefit (final salary) scheme above a set value, you must take advice from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions. Safeguarded benefits also need particular care, so make sure your adviser has the right permissions.

### Should I combine my pensions?

Sometimes it makes life simpler and cheaper, but some older pensions have valuable guarantees or low charges. An adviser checks before recommending a transfer.
