## Checking assessment clinics are qualified and regulated

Diagnostic assessments for conditions such as ADHD and autism should be carried out by suitably qualified clinicians: psychiatrists registered with the GMC, practitioner psychologists registered with the HCPC, or multidisciplinary teams that follow NICE guidance. Clinics carrying out regulated healthcare in England register with the Care Quality Commission (CQC); Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland have their own regulators.

If you hope your NHS GP will prescribe medication after a private diagnosis, ask in advance whether they will agree to a shared care arrangement. Policies differ between areas and practices.

## Questions to ask the professional

Asking a few direct questions early tells you a lot about how a practice works:

- What qualifications and experience do you have in carrying out autism assessments?
- How long does the assessment process usually take, and what does it involve?
- Will the assessment be carried out by one clinician or a multidisciplinary team?
- What kind of report will I receive, and can it be used to request adjustments at work or in education?
- Do you offer any support or guidance after a diagnosis?
- If medication might be considered later, will you liaise with my NHS GP about shared care?

## FAQs about autism assessment near me

### What is an autism assessment?

An autism assessment is a process that uses developmental history and structured observation to consider whether someone is autistic. It is usually carried out by qualified clinicians and may involve one professional or a team.

### Can I get an autism assessment on the NHS?

Yes, you can ask your GP for a referral to an NHS autism assessment service. Waiting times can vary, and some people choose to explore private options as well.

### Will my GP accept a private autism diagnosis?

Policies vary between GP practices and areas. It is best to ask your GP in advance whether they will accept a private diagnosis and, if relevant, agree to a shared care arrangement for any medication.
