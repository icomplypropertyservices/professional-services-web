## FAQs about veneers dentist near me

### Will veneers ruin my natural teeth?

Veneers usually involve removing a small amount of tooth enamel, which is permanent, so it's important to discuss the trade-offs with your dentist. A careful dentist will explain exactly how much preparation is needed and whether a less invasive option might suit you.

### How do I know if a dentist is qualified to fit veneers?

You can check that your dentist is registered with the General Dental Council (GDC) by searching the online register. You can also ask about their experience with veneers and whether they use mock-ups and bite assessment as part of planning.

### Can I get veneers on the NHS?

Veneers are generally considered a cosmetic treatment and are not usually available on the NHS unless there is a clinical need, such as repairing damaged teeth. Most people seeking veneers for appearance pay privately, so it's worth asking your dentist about costs and what is included.

### What should I do if I'm unhappy with private dental treatment?

Start by raising your concern with the practice's own complaints process. If it isn't resolved, the Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints, and they can advise you on next steps.

### Can I see a private dentist and stay with my NHS dentist?

Yes, you can use both. Some patients see a private dentist for specific treatments and keep NHS care for routine check-ups, if their NHS practice agrees.
