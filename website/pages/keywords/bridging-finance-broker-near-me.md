## Qualifications and registration to look for

Mortgage advice is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Any broker or adviser you use should be authorised, or be an appointed representative of an authorised firm, and you can check this on the FCA register. Advisers typically hold a qualification such as CeMAP.

Brokers must tell you whether they search the whole of the market or a limited panel of lenders, and how they are paid, whether by lender commission, a fee, or both. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

A broker works on your behalf to find suitable products from providers. Ask how many providers they compare and how they are paid.

## FAQs about bridging finance broker near me

### How quickly can a bridging loan be arranged?

Bridging loans can complete much faster than a standard mortgage, sometimes within days if the paperwork is in order. However, timescales vary by lender and the complexity of your situation, so ask your broker for an honest estimate.

### What is the difference between a bridging finance broker and a mortgage broker?

A bridging finance broker specialises in short-term lending, while a mortgage broker may focus on standard residential or buy-to-let mortgages. Some brokers do both, so check that the broker you speak to regularly arranges bridging finance.

### Do I need a deposit for a bridging loan?

Most bridging lenders require some equity in the property, which acts like a deposit. The amount needed varies, so discuss your circumstances with the broker.

### Can I get bridging finance if I have bad credit?

Some lenders specialise in bridging finance for people with less-than-perfect credit, but terms and rates may be higher. A broker can tell you which lenders might consider your situation.

### Is a broker better than going to my bank?

A broker can compare many lenders and may find options your bank does not offer. Your bank can only offer its own products. Either can suit you; a broker gives you a wider view.
