Private endoscopy is usually carried out by a consultant gastroenterologist or surgeon at a private hospital or clinic. ## Checking private consultants are qualified and regulated

Hospital consultants should appear on the GMC's specialist register for their field, which you can check online. Private clinics providing regulated healthcare in England must register with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which inspects and publishes ratings. In Wales the regulator is Healthcare Inspectorate Wales, in Scotland Healthcare Improvement Scotland, and in Northern Ireland the RQIA. Private hospitals in England may also share outcomes data through the Private Healthcare Information Network (PHIN).

Many private specialists also work in the NHS. If you have private medical insurance, your insurer may require a GP referral and may limit you to certain consultants or hospitals, so check before you book.

## Endoscopy Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a referral from my GP to book a private endoscopy?

Some private consultants accept self-referrals, but others prefer a GP referral. If you have private medical insurance, your insurer may require a GP referral before you can book, so check with them first.

### Will I be asleep during the endoscopy?

Sedation may be offered to help you relax, but it is not a general anaesthetic, so you may still be aware of what is happening. If you have sedation, you will need someone to take you home afterwards and should not drive.

### How do I check the consultant and clinic are properly registered?

You can check a consultant's specialist registration on the General Medical Council (GMC) online register. In England, private clinics providing regulated healthcare must be registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which publishes inspection ratings.

### What if I have private medical insurance?

Your insurer may require a GP referral and may only cover certain consultants or hospitals, so check with them before you book. They can also confirm whether pre-authorisation is needed for the procedure.

### Do I need a GP referral to see a private consultant?

Self-paying patients can sometimes book directly, but many consultants prefer a referral, and insurers usually require one. A private GP can provide one quickly.
