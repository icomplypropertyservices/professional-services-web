## Cyber Insurance Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need cyber insurance if I already have business insurance?

Many standard business insurance policies do not include cyber cover, or they may only offer limited protection. It is worth checking your existing policy and discussing your needs with a broker to see if you need separate cyber insurance.

### What is typically covered by cyber insurance?

Cyber insurance can cover losses from data breaches, ransomware and cyber attacks, including incident response and business interruption. Policies vary, so it is important to check the details and exclusions with your broker.

### How can I check that a broker is authorised?

You can check whether a broker is authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) on the FCA register.

### What should I do if I have a complaint about a broker?

If you have a complaint that is not resolved, you can take it to the Financial Ombudsman Service. Brokers must tell you how they are paid and explain key policy features and exclusions.

### Does a local broker have access to different cyber insurers than a national one?

Brokers vary in which insurers they approach, and this is not determined by how near they are to you. It is worth asking each broker which insurers they would consider for your business and why, so you can judge the fit for yourself.

### What happens at a first meeting with a cyber insurance broker?

Expect questions about the data you hold, your security controls such as backups and staff training, and how you would handle an incident. The broker should then explain the cover available, the key exclusions, and how they are paid, before arranging a quote.

### Can I arrange cyber cover if my business is small?

Cyber insurance is available to businesses of different sizes, though insurers will still expect basic security controls to be in place. A broker can explain what is typically required and help you understand whether a policy suits your circumstances.

### What is non-disclosure?

Failing to tell an insurer something relevant. It can reduce or void a claim, so answer questions fully and ask your broker if unsure.
