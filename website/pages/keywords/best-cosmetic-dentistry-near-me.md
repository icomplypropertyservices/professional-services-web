## Checking private dentists are qualified and regulated

Dentists, dental hygienists, dental therapists and dental nurses must be registered with the General Dental Council (GDC), and you can check the GDC register online. Specialist titles such as orthodontist or endodontist can only be used by people on a GDC specialist list. Private clinics providing regulated healthcare in England must register with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which inspects and publishes ratings. In Wales the regulator is Healthcare Inspectorate Wales, in Scotland Healthcare Improvement Scotland, and in Northern Ireland the RQIA.

If something goes wrong with private dental treatment, start with the practice's complaints process. The Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints that are not resolved.

## FAQs about best cosmetic dentistry near me

### How do I judge which cosmetic dentist is best for me?

There's no single 'best' dentist for everyone. It's worth checking that the dentist is registered with the General Dental Council (GDC), asking about their experience with the treatments you're considering, and seeing whether they offer a digital smile preview so you can discuss likely results. A good consultation should leave you feeling informed, not pressured.

### What is a digital smile preview?

A digital smile preview is a way of showing you what your teeth might look like after cosmetic treatment, often using images or software. It can help you decide whether to go ahead and discuss options with your dentist, though it's a guide rather than a promise of the final result.

### Will cosmetic dentistry damage my teeth?

Cosmetic dentistry doesn't have to damage your teeth — a careful dentist will aim to preserve tooth structure and use minimal treatment where possible. You should discuss the risks and benefits of each option with your dentist, and ask how they plan to protect your natural teeth.

### What should I do if I'm unhappy with private dental treatment?

If that doesn't resolve the issue, the Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints.

### Does cosmetic dentistry near me mean I have to use the closest practice?

Not at all — 'near me' is a starting point, not a rule. It's sensible to weigh convenience against how well a dentist listens and explains your options, and how comfortable you feel discussing what you want to change.

### What if the nearest practice offers treatments I'm not sure about?

You don't have to agree to anything at a first appointment. A good consultation should leave you with a clearer understanding of the options, the risks and what maintenance would involve, so you can take time to decide.

### Can I get cosmetic dentistry through the NHS?

Cosmetic treatments are generally considered private work, which is why quotes are given on application. If you also have concerns about your oral health, it's worth asking the dentist to explain what is needed for health reasons and what is purely cosmetic.

### Do I need a check-up before treatment?

Almost always. A dentist needs to examine you, and often take X-rays, before recommending treatment.
