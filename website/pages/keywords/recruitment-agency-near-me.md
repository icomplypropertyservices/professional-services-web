## Questions to ask a recruitment agency

Once you are introduced, these questions help you decide whether the recruitment agency is right for you:

- Which industries and job types do you specialise in?
- How do you charge for your services, and who pays?
- What is your process for screening and shortlisting candidates?
- How often will you update me on progress?
- Do you handle temporary and contract roles as well as permanent ones?
- Are you a member of the Recruitment and Employment Confederation or APSCo?

## What to understand about using a recruitment agency

In the UK, they must follow the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations, which are enforced by the Employment Agency Standards Inspectorate. Agencies cannot usually charge work-seekers for finding them work, so be wary if one asks you to pay.

Agencies that supply temporary workers have specific obligations on pay, holiday and right-to-work checks. Clear terms of business for employers and clear contracts for workers are essential. Membership of the Recruitment and Employment Confederation (REC) or APSCo shows a commitment to an industry code, which you can ask about.

## FAQs: a recruitment agency

### Will a recruitment agency charge me to find work?

Agencies cannot usually charge work-seekers for finding them work under UK regulations. If an agency asks you to pay upfront, ask for clarification and consider walking away.

### How do I choose a good recruitment agency near me?

Look for agencies that specialise in your industry or role type and check whether they belong to a professional body like the REC or APSCo. Ask about their terms and how they communicate before you commit.

### What should I have ready before contacting an agency?

Have an up-to-date CV and a clear idea of the type of work you want, your availability and your salary expectations. For employers, prepare a job description and details of the role.

### Can I use more than one agency at a time?

Yes, you can register with several agencies, but be clear with each about your other applications to avoid duplicate submissions. For employers, check terms to avoid conflicts if two agencies put forward the same candidate.

### What is a rebate period?

An agreed period after a permanent hire during which the agency refunds part of its fee or finds a replacement if the person leaves.
