## Qualifications and registration to look for

"Architect" is a protected title in the UK. Only people on the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance. Many practices are also RIBA Chartered Practices. Architectural designers and technologists can do excellent work too, but they are not registered architects, so it is worth knowing which you are hiring.

Before you appoint anyone, check the ARB register and ask for a written appointment that sets out services, fees and responsibilities. Building regulations approval, planning permission and party wall matters are separate processes that your architect can help you navigate.

## Choosing between local architects for a garage conversion

A garage conversion is a small project with a lot riding on it, so the local architect you choose should be able to show you how they have handled similar conversions before, particularly ones in your type of property. Ask each practice what they would check first, such as the condition of the garage foundations, how the new room will be insulated and ventilated, and whether the garage sits close enough to a boundary to raise party wall matters. Two practices may take quite different approaches, and comparing those early conversations is often more useful than comparing anything else.

Because quotes are on application, it helps to ask what each fee covers before you decide. Some practices quote for design and drawings only, while others include help with a planning application and building regulations submissions, so the scope behind a quote matters as much as the figure. Ask whether the appointment will be in writing and what it sets out, and check the Architects Registration Board register for anyone using the title architect, since some garage conversions are handled by architectural designers or technologists who are not registered.

The wording of your search suggests you want someone who can visit the site rather than work only from photographs, which is sensible for a garage because so much depends on what is actually there. At a first appointment, expect questions about how the garage is built, whether it is attached or detached, what you want the new room for and whether you need to keep parking on the plot. It is also worth raising any covenants on the title and any planning conditions you know about, so the professional can tell you what needs checking with the local authority.

## FAQs: a garage conversion architect

### Does a garage conversion always need planning permission?

Not always. Some garage conversions fall under permitted development, but others need planning permission, for example where there are conditions on the property or where the work affects a conservation area. Your architect can help you check with the local authority.

### Do building regulations apply to a garage conversion?

Yes, building regulations always apply when you convert a garage into living space. They cover matters such as insulation, structural stability, fire safety and ventilation, and your architect can help you navigate the approval process.

### What should I check before converting my garage?

It is worth checking whether there are any covenants on your property that restrict how the garage can be used or altered. You should also confirm parking arrangements and any planning conditions that might affect the conversion.

### How do I know if someone is a registered architect?

"Architect" is a protected title, so you can check the Architects Registration Board register to confirm whether someone is registered. Architectural designers and technologists can also do excellent work, but they are not registered architects, so it is worth knowing which you are hiring.

### Is a garage conversion cheaper than an extension?

It can be, because the walls and roof are often already in place, but the cost depends on the condition of the garage, the foundations and how much insulation and structural work is needed. Quotes are on application, so ask each professional what their fee covers and what building work is likely to involve.

### Can I use an architectural designer instead of a registered architect for a garage conversion?

Yes, architectural designers and technologists can do excellent work on a garage conversion, but they are not registered architects and cannot use that title. If you want a registered architect, check the Architects Registration Board register, and in either case ask for a written appointment setting out services, fees and responsibilities.

### Will converting my garage affect my council tax or parking?

Converting a garage into living space can change how your property is banded for council tax, and some properties have planning conditions requiring a parking space to be kept. Your architect can help you check the conditions attached to your property, and your local authority or Citizens Advice can explain council tax questions.

### Do I need an architect for an extension?

Not legally, but a registered architect brings design skill, planning experience and accountability. For simple work, an architectural technologist or designer may be enough. We can match you to the right level of help.
