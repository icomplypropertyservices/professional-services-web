## Qualifications and registration to look for

Clinics offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback.

Simple tests such as blood hormone checks may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing, but treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre.

## Semen Analysis Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a referral from my GP for a semen analysis?

Not always. Some clinics accept self-referrals for semen analysis, while others may ask for a GP referral. It is best to check with the clinic when you enquire.

### How long does it take to get semen analysis results?

Results are usually available within a few days, but this can vary by clinic. The clinic should tell you when to expect them and how they will be shared.

### Can I have a semen analysis at a clinic that is not HFEA licensed?

Yes, simple tests such as semen analysis may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing. However, any treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with an HFEA licensed centre.

### What should I do if my semen analysis result is abnormal?

An abnormal result does not necessarily mean you cannot conceive, and one test may need repeating. The clinic should explain what the results mean and discuss next steps with you.

### Am I eligible for NHS fertility treatment?

Eligibility depends on local commissioning rules, including age, how long you have been trying and previous children. Ask your GP; a private clinic can also explain options.
