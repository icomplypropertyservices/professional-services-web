## What to understand about contemporary house architects

In the UK, the title "architect" is protected by law. Only people on the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance. Many practices are also RIBA Chartered Practices, which is a further sign of professional standards.

Architectural designers and technologists can also work on contemporary homes and may offer excellent service, but they are not registered architects. Before you appoint anyone, check the ARB register and ask for a written appointment that sets out services, fees and responsibilities. Your architect can help you navigate planning permission, building regulations approval and party wall matters, which are separate processes.

## Contemporary House Architect Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a registered architect for a contemporary house?

Only a registered architect can call themselves an architect and must be on the ARB register. However, you can use an architectural designer or technologist, but they are not registered architects. It is worth checking the ARB register and asking about qualifications before you appoint anyone.

### Can an architect help with planning permission for a modern house?

Yes, architects can prepare and submit planning applications on your behalf and advise on design to meet local planning policies. They can also help with building regulations approval and party wall matters, which are separate processes.

### How do I choose an architect for a contemporary home near me?

Look for architects with experience in contemporary design and local projects. Ask to see examples of their work, check their ARB registration and RIBA Chartered Practice status if applicable, and request a written appointment setting out services and fees.

### What should I budget for a contemporary house architect?

Architect fees vary depending on the project scope and complexity. It is best to discuss your budget openly and ask for a detailed fee proposal. Remember to budget for quality materials and energy-efficient features, as these are key to contemporary design.

### Will an architect guarantee planning permission?

No one can guarantee planning permission. A good architect will explain local policy, the risks and how to give your application the best chance.
