## FAQs: a broker for construction insurance

### Do I need a broker rather than going direct to an insurer?

You can buy construction cover directly, but a broker can compare insurers and explain exclusions across policies. That can help when your work spans several types of cover, such as public liability, contract works and hired plant.

### What is contract works cover?

Contract works cover protects the project itself while it's being built, including materials and work in progress. It's often required alongside public liability on construction contracts, so it's worth checking what your contract asks for.

### How do I check a construction insurance broker is properly authorised?

You can check them on the FCA register before you go ahead.

### What if I'm unhappy with the broker's service?

Brokers must explain key policy features and exclusions and tell you how they are paid. If a complaint isn't resolved, it can be escalated to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

### Does my construction insurance broker need to be local?

Not necessarily. What matters more is whether the broker understands construction risks and can arrange the cover your contracts require. Many brokers work remotely and can still provide a personal service.

### How can I compare construction insurance brokers?

Ask each broker about their experience with your type of construction work and how they explain exclusions. Also check they are authorised by the FCA and ask how they are paid. You can then judge which broker suits you best.

### What is non-disclosure?

Failing to tell an insurer something relevant. It can reduce or void a claim, so answer questions fully and ask your broker if unsure.
