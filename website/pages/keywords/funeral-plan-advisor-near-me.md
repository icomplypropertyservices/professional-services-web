## Qualifications and registration to look for

Funeral directors are not licensed in England, but many belong to the National Association of Funeral Directors (NAFD) or the National Society of Allied and Independent Funeral Directors (SAIF), which have codes of practice. Scotland has a statutory code of practice for funeral directors. Since 2022, providers of pre-paid funeral plans must be authorised by the FCA.

The Competition and Markets Authority requires funeral directors to display standardised price lists, so you can compare costs clearly.

## FAQs about funeral plan advisor near me

### Do I need a funeral plan advisor to buy a pre-paid funeral plan?

No, you can buy directly from a FCA-authorised provider. But an advisor can help you compare plans and understand what is covered, which many people find useful.

### How do I check that a funeral plan provider is authorised?

You can check the Financial Conduct Authority's register. All providers of pre-paid funeral plans must be authorised by the FCA.

### What should I look for in a pre-paid funeral plan?

Look at what the plan covers, whether the funeral director is local, the cancellation terms, and how the funds are protected. It is also worth checking the provider's FCA authorisation.

### Can I change my mind after buying a funeral plan?

Yes, you usually have a cooling-off period, but the terms vary. Always check the cancellation policy before you commit.

### What is direct cremation?

A cremation without a funeral service. Families can then hold a memorial separately.
