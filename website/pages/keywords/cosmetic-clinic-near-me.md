## What to understand about regulation when choosing a cosmetic clinic

In England, clinics that carry out surgical procedures must be registered with the CQC. For non-surgical treatments, the government has set out plans for a licensing scheme, but until it is in force you need to choose carefully.

Anti-wrinkle injections use a prescription-only medicine, so a prescriber such as a doctor, nurse prescriber, dentist or pharmacist prescriber must assess you, normally face to face. Dermal fillers are devices rather than medicines, and the rules are tightening. Checking whether a practitioner is on a register such as Save Face or the JCCP, or is a doctor or nurse registered with the GMC or NMC, can offer extra reassurance.

## Qualifications and registration to look for

Dermal fillers are devices rather than medicines, and the rules are tightening: the government has set out plans for a licensing scheme for non-surgical cosmetic procedures in England.

Until licensing is in force, choose a clinic carefully. Practitioners on registers such as Save Face or the JCCP, and doctors and nurses registered with the GMC or NMC, offer extra reassurance. Clinics run by regulated healthcare providers may also be registered with the CQC.

Clinics vary from single-practitioner rooms to multi-specialist centres. Ask who will see you, their registration and what facilities are on site.

## FAQs about cosmetic clinic near me

### Do I need a referral to visit a cosmetic clinic?

No, you can usually book directly with a cosmetic clinic. However, for treatments involving prescription-only medicines, you will need an assessment by a prescriber, which is often done at the clinic.

### Is a cosmetic clinic the same as a beauty salon?

Not necessarily. Cosmetic clinics provide treatments such as anti-wrinkle injections and dermal fillers, which may be carried out by healthcare professionals, whereas beauty salons typically offer non-medical treatments. It is important to check who will be treating you and their qualifications.

### How can I check if a practitioner is properly qualified?

You can ask the clinic about the practitioner's professional registration. Doctors are registered with the GMC, nurses with the NMC, and some practitioners are on registers such as Save Face or the JCCP. You can also check the registers directly.

### What does CQC registration mean for a cosmetic clinic?

In England, clinics that provide surgical procedures must be registered with the CQC, which means they meet certain standards of safety and quality. For non-surgical treatments, registration is not always required, but it can offer extra reassurance.

### Are under-18s allowed to have these treatments?

In England it is illegal to give Botox-type injections or dermal fillers for cosmetic purposes to under-18s.
