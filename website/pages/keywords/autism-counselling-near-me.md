## Qualifications and registration to look for

"Counsellor" and "psychotherapist" are not protected titles in the UK. To protect yourself, choose a practitioner on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. Practitioner psychologists, who use titles such as clinical or counselling psychologist, must be registered with the HCPC.

Accredited registers require training, supervision, a code of ethics and a complaints procedure. A good therapist will explain their approach, confidentiality and its limits in the first session.

## FAQs about autism counselling near me

### Do I need a formal diagnosis of autism to see a counsellor?

No, a diagnosis is not required to seek counselling for autism-related concerns. Many counsellors will work with you based on your self-understanding and needs, and they can adapt their approach accordingly.

### How can I check that a counsellor is properly qualified?

Look for a counsellor on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. These registers require training, supervision, a code of ethics and a complaints procedure. Practitioner psychologists must be registered with the HCPC.

### Can counselling sessions be adapted for sensory sensitivities?

Yes, counsellors experienced with autism can adapt sessions to communication and sensory needs. This might include adjusting lighting, offering online sessions, or allowing breaks. It is best to discuss your specific needs with the counsellor beforehand.

### What does neurodiversity-affirming practice mean?

Neurodiversity-affirming practice recognises autism as a natural variation of human experience rather than a deficit. It focuses on understanding and accommodating individual needs, and it respects autistic identity. You can ask a counsellor how they put this into practice.

### What is the difference between counselling and psychotherapy?

The terms overlap. Counselling often focuses on a specific problem over a shorter period, while psychotherapy may explore deeper patterns over a longer time. A good practitioner will explain their approach.
