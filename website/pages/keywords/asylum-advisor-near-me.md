## Understanding regulation and legal aid for asylum advice

In the UK, it is a criminal offence to give immigration advice unless the adviser is qualified and regulated. Asylum advisers must be registered with the Immigration Advice Authority (IAA, formerly the OISC) at the right level for your matter, or be a solicitor, barrister or chartered legal executive authorised by their own regulator. Before you share documents or pay, check the IAA's adviser finder or the SRA register.

Legal aid is often available for asylum cases, so it is worth asking whether the adviser offers legal aid and what level of IAA registration they hold. IAA advisers are registered at levels that limit what they can do, from basic applications to appeals and representation, so ask if their level covers your type of case.
