## Questions to ask the fertility clinic

Asking a few direct questions early tells you a lot about how a practice works:

- What does your fertility assessment include, and will it cover hormone blood tests, an ultrasound scan and semen analysis if needed?
- Are you licensed by the HFEA for treatments such as IVF, donor treatment or egg and embryo freezing?
- How and when will you explain my results to me, and what support do you offer to help me understand them?
- Can you tell me the price of the assessment and any follow-up costs before I book?
- How soon can I be seen for an assessment, and are there any preparation steps I need to take?
- What are the next steps if the assessment shows something that needs treatment or further investigation?

## Checking fertility clinics are qualified and regulated

Clinics offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback.

Simple tests such as blood hormone checks may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing, but treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre.

## Fertility Assessment Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a referral from my GP for a fertility assessment?

Not always. Many clinics accept self-referrals for a fertility assessment, but it's worth asking the clinic when you enquire. Your GP can also advise on NHS options.

### What happens during a fertility assessment?

The clinic should explain each step and what the results mean.

### Is a fertility assessment the same as fertility treatment?

No. An assessment is about gathering information, while treatment such as IVF or donor treatment is a separate step. Clinics offering most fertility treatments must be licensed by the HFEA, so check before you book.

### How do I choose a clinic near me?

Look for a clinic that is convenient for you and, if treatment might be needed, check its HFEA licence and inspection ratings using the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool. Ask how results will be explained and what the costs are.

### Am I eligible for NHS fertility treatment?

Eligibility depends on local commissioning rules, including age, how long you have been trying and previous children. Ask your GP; a private clinic can also explain options.
