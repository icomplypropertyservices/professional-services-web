## One key thing to understand about CIS and accountants

In the UK, the word 'accountant' on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can call themselves an accountant. However, titles such as Chartered Accountant, Chartered Certified Accountant and Chartered Management Accountant are protected, and members are bound by their professional body's ethics, training and complaints rules. It's reasonable to ask which professional body, if any, an accountant belongs to.

Every accountancy practice offering services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. You can ask which body supervises the firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled. This helps you make an informed choice.

## CIS Accountant Near Me: FAQs

### What is the Construction Industry Scheme (CIS)?

The Construction Industry Scheme is a UK tax scheme for contractors and subcontractors in the construction industry. Contractors deduct tax from subcontractors' payments and pass it to HMRC, and subcontractors can reclaim overpaid tax.

### Do I need a CIS accountant if I'm a subcontractor?

If you're a subcontractor, you may be able to reclaim tax deducted under CIS, especially if you operate through a limited company. An accountant can help you claim back what you're owed and keep your records straight.

### What happens if I file my CIS returns late?

Late CIS returns can attract penalties from HMRC. An accountant can help you get up to date and avoid further penalties.

### Can I reclaim CIS deductions if I'm a limited company?

Yes, limited company subcontractors can offset CIS deductions against their tax bill. An accountant can guide you through the process.

### Does my accountant need to be local to my building site?

Not necessarily, but many people searching for a CIS accountant near me want someone they can visit or call easily when paperwork builds up. It is worth asking how a practice handles documents and queries before you decide whether distance matters for you.

### Can a CIS accountant help if I am both a contractor and a subcontractor?

Some people in construction work both ways, taking on subcontractors for some jobs and being paid as a subcontractor on others. Explain this when you enquire so the accountant can tell you how they would handle both sides of the scheme for you.

### What should I bring to a first meeting about CIS?

Bring your payment and deduction statements, details of your business structure and any correspondence from HMRC, including penalty notices. Having these to hand lets the accountant give you a clearer picture of what help you need and what they would charge.
