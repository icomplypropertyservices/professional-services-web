## Questions to ask the consultant

Asking a few direct questions early tells you a lot about how a practice works:

- What experience do you have with our specific regulator and the type of work we do?
- Can you explain the limits of your role and when we would need to involve a solicitor or other regulated adviser?
- Which professional memberships do you hold, and can you share case studies or references from similar clients?
- How will you work alongside our accountable people without taking over their responsibilities?
- What records will you keep of the decisions we make together, and how will they be shared with us?
- What information do you need from us before you can give a clear quote for the work?

## Qualifications and registration to look for

Most consultancy work is not regulated by a single body, so it pays to check credentials yourself. Relevant memberships include the Chartered Management Institute (CMI), the CIPD for HR professionals, BCS for IT professionals, and industry-specific bodies for compliance specialists. Ask for case studies and references from similar clients.

## Regulatory Compliance Consultant Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a consultant who is near me, or can the work be done remotely?

Much compliance work can be done remotely, but a local consultant can be useful if you want face-to-face meetings or someone familiar with your regional regulator contacts. Tell us your preference and we will introduce you to a suitable professional.

### Will a consultant take over responsibility for compliance from our team?

No. Consultants do not replace your accountable people, who remain responsible for meeting your obligations. The consultant's role is to support, advise and help you keep proper records of the decisions you make.

### How do I check a consultant's credentials if the work is not regulated by a single body?

Ask about relevant memberships such as the Chartered Management Institute, the CIPD for HR professionals, BCS for IT professionals, or industry-specific bodies, and ask for case studies and references from similar clients.

### What should I have ready before I speak to a consultant?

It helps to have your regulator's correspondence, a note of the compliance area you are worried about and any internal records of decisions already made. Having these ready means the consultant can give you a clearer view of what help you need and quote you directly.

### Can I hire a consultant for a short project?

Yes. Many consultants work on short, defined pieces of work such as a review, a policy update or a workshop.
