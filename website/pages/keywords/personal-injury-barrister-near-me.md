## Qualifications and registration to look for

Barristers in England and Wales are regulated by the Bar Standards Board (BSB). You can check the Barristers' Register to see whether someone holds a current practising certificate and whether they are authorised to accept instructions directly from the public. In Scotland the equivalent professionals are advocates, regulated through the Faculty of Advocates.

Barristers usually work from chambers, with clerks who manage diaries and fees. Many barristers can be instructed through a solicitor in the traditional way, and those registered under the Public Access scheme can be instructed directly by members of the public for suitable matters.

## FAQs about personal injury barrister near me

### Do I need a solicitor to instruct a personal injury barrister?

Not always. Barristers registered under the Public Access scheme can be instructed directly by members of the public for suitable matters. However, many people still choose to instruct a barrister through their solicitor, especially if their case is complex or already underway.

### How do I check if a personal injury barrister is regulated?

Barristers in England and Wales are regulated by the Bar Standards Board (BSB). You can check the Barristers' Register to see whether someone holds a current practising certificate and whether they are authorised to accept instructions directly from the public.

### What does a personal injury barrister do?

They often advise on settlement value, and expert evidence is crucial in these cases. Joint settlement meetings resolve many cases.

### Can a personal injury barrister help if my case is already with a solicitor?

Yes. Barristers are often instructed by solicitors to provide specialist advice or representation at hearings. If your case is already with a solicitor, they can instruct a barrister on your behalf, or you may be able to instruct one directly if appropriate.

### Can I instruct a personal injury barrister near me directly?

You can instruct a barrister directly if they are registered under the Public Access scheme and your matter is suitable. Otherwise, you would need a solicitor to instruct them on your behalf. It is worth asking the barrister about this when you first make contact.

### How do I find a personal injury barrister near me?

You can search the Barristers' Register or use a service like iComply to be introduced to a suitable barrister. When choosing, consider their experience with your type of injury, their location, and whether they are authorised for Public Access. Always check their regulatory status before instructing them.

### What is the difference between a solicitor and a barrister?

Solicitors usually manage a case from start to finish and deal with clients day to day. Barristers specialise in advocacy and specialist advice, and are typically brought in for hearings or difficult legal questions.
