## How to check you are in safe hands

In the UK, the word "accountant" on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can use it. Titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises a firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled.

iComply is not a regulator and does not provide the professional service itself. We introduce you to professionals who hold their own registrations, and we encourage you to check them on the public register.

## One key thing to understand about expat tax

The Statutory Residence Test determines whether you're treated as a UK resident for tax purposes, which affects how your income is taxed. Double tax treaties between the UK and other countries can prevent you from being taxed twice on the same income, but the rules are specific to each treaty.

If you're a non-resident selling UK property, you must report the gain to HMRC, usually within a set period after completion. An expat accountant can help you navigate these rules and ensure you meet your obligations.

## Expat Accountant Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need an expat accountant if I only have rental income from a UK property?

Yes, if you're a non-resident landlord, you must declare the rental income to HMRC and may need to file a tax return. An expat accountant can help you understand your obligations and any reliefs available.

### What is the Statutory Residence Test and why does it matter?

The Statutory Residence Test is a set of rules that determines whether you're a UK resident for tax purposes. It matters because it affects how your worldwide income is taxed in the UK.

### Can an expat accountant help me avoid double taxation?

Yes, they can advise on how double tax treaties between the UK and your country of residence apply to your income, helping you claim relief where available.

### I'm selling a UK property while living abroad. What should I do?

You must report the gain to HMRC, usually within a set period after completion. An expat accountant can help you calculate the gain and file the necessary return.
