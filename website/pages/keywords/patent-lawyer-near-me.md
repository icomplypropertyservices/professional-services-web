## Checking solicitors are qualified and regulated

Solicitors in England and Wales are regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority (SRA), and you can check an individual or firm on the SRA's register. In Scotland solicitors are regulated by the Law Society of Scotland, and in Northern Ireland by the Law Society of Northern Ireland. Regulated firms must hold professional indemnity insurance and have a complaints procedure; if that does not resolve things, the Legal Ombudsman can look at service complaints in England and Wales.

The word "lawyer" is not protected on its own, so check that whoever handles your matter is a solicitor, a chartered legal executive (CILEx) or another authorised professional. Reserved activities such as conducting court litigation and certain probate and conveyancing work can only be carried out by authorised people.

"Lawyer" is a general term. In England and Wales the person handling your matter will usually be a solicitor, chartered legal executive or barrister, each regulated by their own body.

## FAQs: a patent lawyer

### Should I speak to a patent attorney or an IP solicitor?

Patent attorneys specialise in drafting and prosecuting patent applications, while IP solicitors often handle disputes and enforcement. Many matters need both skills, so it helps to explain your situation and let the professional advise on the right fit. iComply can introduce you to a suitable UK professional who quotes you directly.

### Can I talk about my invention before filing?

It is generally wise not to disclose an invention publicly before filing, as this can affect novelty. A professional can advise on what counts as disclosure and whether any confidentiality agreement is needed before you speak to manufacturers or investors.

### How do I check that someone is properly regulated?

Patent attorneys are regulated by IPReg, and solicitors in England and Wales can be checked on the Solicitors Regulation Authority's register. In Scotland and Northern Ireland the relevant law societies regulate solicitors. Confirming this before you share confidential details is a sensible step.

### What does price on application mean here?

It means the professional will quote you directly once they understand your matter, rather than a fixed price being shown in advance. The cost can vary with the complexity of the invention and the work involved. You can ask for the quote and scope in writing before anything begins.

### Is a solicitor the same as a lawyer?

"Lawyer" is a general word that covers solicitors, barristers, chartered legal executives and others. A solicitor is a specific regulated professional who can advise you, run your case and instruct a barrister if needed.
