## Choosing between the cosmetic dentists close to you

Because cosmetic dentistry covers several different treatments, two practices near you may offer quite different things. One may focus on whitening and bonding, another on veneers or clear aligners, so it helps to list the treatments you are actually considering and check which local practices discuss them openly.

Distance matters more with cosmetic work than with a one-off check-up, because you may need more than one visit, including a review afterwards. Think about how easily you could get back to the practice if you had a question or needed a follow-up, and whether the appointment times suit you.

It is also worth noticing how each practice responds when you first get in touch. A cosmetic dentist who asks about your general dental health, explains the limits of what each treatment can achieve and puts the quote in writing is giving you more to judge by than a practice that only talks about appearance.

## Best Cosmetic Dentist Near Me: FAQs

### Is cosmetic dentistry available on the NHS?

Cosmetic treatments are generally private, because the NHS provides dental care for health needs rather than appearance. Some work may be available on the NHS if it is needed for your dental health, so it is worth asking the practice what applies to your situation.

### Can I whiten my teeth at home with a kit?

Whitening must be done by a dental professional, who can assess your teeth and gums first. A dentist can provide whitening safely and explain what to expect.

### How do I check a cosmetic dentist is properly registered?

Dentists, dental hygienists, dental therapists and dental nurses must be registered with the General Dental Council, and you can check the register online. Specialist titles such as orthodontist can only be used by people on a GDC specialist list.

### What if I am unhappy with private dental treatment?

Start with the practice's complaints process. If it is not resolved, the Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints.

### Should I see a cosmetic dentist if I haven't had a check-up recently?

It is sensible to have your teeth and gums looked at before any cosmetic work, because healthy teeth and gums come first. A cosmetic dentist can carry out that examination and tell you whether treatment is appropriate now or whether something else needs attention first.

### Can I compare cosmetic dentists by asking for a quote?

Yes, and it helps to ask each one to quote for the same treatment so the comparison is fair. Prices are on application, and a written quote should set out what is included, such as any review appointments after the work.

### What if I only want whitening?

Whitening still needs a dental professional, who can check your teeth and gums and provide the treatment safely. You can say at the outset that whitening is your main interest, so the cosmetic dentist can quote for that rather than a wider treatment plan.

### Can I see a private dentist and stay with my NHS dentist?

Yes, you can use both. Some patients see a private dentist for specific treatments and keep NHS care for routine check-ups, if their NHS practice agrees.
