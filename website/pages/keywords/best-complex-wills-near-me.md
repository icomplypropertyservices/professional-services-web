## How to check you are in safe hands

Writing a will is not a reserved legal activity in England and Wales, so some will writers are not regulated. Solicitors (SRA) and chartered legal executives (CILEx) are regulated, insured and covered by the Legal Ombudsman. Many specialists are also members of STEP (the Society of Trust and Estate Practitioners) or Solicitors for the Elderly. Applying for a grant of probate on someone else's behalf for a fee is a reserved activity.

When choosing a provider, ask whether they are regulated, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance and where your original will is stored. Scotland has its own rules on succession and confirmation, so make sure your adviser practises in the right jurisdiction.

## Best Complex Wills Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a solicitor for a complex will?

Not necessarily, as will writing is not a reserved legal activity in England and Wales. However, for complex estates, a regulated specialist such as a solicitor or chartered legal executive can offer greater protection and expertise.

### What makes a will complex?

A will is often complex if it involves blended families, business assets, overseas property, trusts or tax planning. These situations require careful drafting to ensure your wishes are carried out.

### Can I have a will that covers overseas property?

A UK will may not cover overseas property, and you may need a separate will in that country. A specialist can advise on how to coordinate your estate across jurisdictions.

### How can I judge who is the best specialist for my needs?

Ask about their experience with estates like yours, check their regulatory status and insurance, and compare quotes. Personal recommendations and professional memberships can also help you decide.

### How do I compare complex wills specialists near me?

Ask each specialist about their experience with estates like yours, their regulatory status and whether they hold professional indemnity insurance. Compare what their quote covers and how they will charge, so you can judge which one suits your needs.

### Will a complex will help avoid disputes after I die?

A carefully drafted complex will can reduce the risk of disputes by making your wishes clear, especially in blended families or where trusts are involved. A specialist can explain how to structure your will to minimise uncertainty.

### Do I need a solicitor for probate?

Not always. Executors can apply for probate themselves for simpler estates. Many choose professional help where there is property, inheritance tax, overseas assets or family tension.
