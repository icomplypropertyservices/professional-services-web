## Costs and quotes for a conveyancer

Conveyancing is often quoted as a fixed legal fee plus disbursements, such as search fees, Land Registry fees and Stamp Duty Land Tax. Leasehold, new-build and auction purchases usually involve more work. Regulated firms in England and Wales must publish pricing information for residential conveyancing. iComply does not set fees; the firm will quote you, POA.

Compare written quotes line by line rather than on the headline figure, and ask whether there is a fee if the deal falls through. Very low quotes sometimes mean higher add-on charges later.

## Comparing quotes side by side

When you gather quotes from several conveyancers, put them next to each other in the same format so the differences stand out. Ask each firm to break their quote into legal fees and disbursements, since searches, Land Registry fees and bank transfer charges are often listed separately and can make one quote look lower than another when it is not.

Read what each quote actually covers for your transaction. A purchase involves title checks, searches and enquiries, while a sale centres on replying to the buyer's solicitor and preparing the contract pack, and a remortgage is narrower again, so compare like with like rather than a purchase quote against a remortgage one.

Bear in mind that conveyancers quote on application rather than publishing fixed prices, so the figure you receive depends on your property and circumstances. Weigh the clarity of the quote, how promptly the firm responded and how willing they were to explain each line, not just the total at the bottom.

## Qualifications and registration to look for

Conveyancing in England and Wales is a reserved legal activity. It can be carried out by solicitors regulated by the SRA or by licensed conveyancers regulated by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers (CLC). Both must hold professional indemnity insurance and follow strict rules on handling client money. In Scotland, property transactions are handled by solicitors under Scottish law.

Many lenders only work with conveyancers on their panel, and the Law Society's Conveyancing Quality Scheme (CQS) is a common accreditation. Check that your chosen firm is on your lender's panel before you instruct them.

## FAQs about best comparison conveyancer near me

### How can I compare conveyancers if there is no official ranking?

Start by checking each firm's regulator (SRA or CLC) and whether they are on your lender's panel. Then compare how they communicate, their local knowledge and how clearly they explain fees and process. Personal recommendations and online research can help, but always verify details directly with the firm.

### What does 'near me' mean for conveyancing?

Many conveyancers work remotely, but a local firm may offer face-to-face meetings and knowledge of the local property market. Consider whether you value proximity or are happy with remote communication. iComply can introduce you to a conveyancer serving your area.

### Is the cheapest conveyancer always the best choice?

No, the cheapest quote may not include all disbursements or offer the level of service you need. Compare the full fee structure and what is included, such as searches and enquiries. Also consider the firm's experience and communication style.

### What should I check before sending money to a conveyancer?

Always verify bank details by phone using a number you have independently confirmed, such as from the firm's official correspondence or a previous letter. Never rely solely on email instructions, as fraudsters can intercept emails. Ask the firm about their client money handling procedures.

### Why do conveyancers quote on application rather than showing a fixed price?

The work depends on your property, whether you are buying, selling or remortgaging, and which searches your lender requires. A conveyancer needs those details before giving you an accurate figure, so quotes are prepared for your situation rather than advertised as a flat rate.

### Should I choose the conveyancer with the lowest quote?

Not automatically. A lower quote may exclude disbursements or offer less explanation of the process, so compare what each fee covers and how clearly it is set out. The right choice balances a clear quote with a firm that answers your questions and suits how you want to communicate.

### How does a comparison search differ from looking for one named firm?

A comparison search suggests you are still weighing up options and want to see how local conveyancers differ before committing. That makes it worth gathering several quotes in the same format and checking each firm's regulator and lender panel status before you decide.

### What is the difference between a solicitor and a licensed conveyancer?

Both are regulated and insured. Licensed conveyancers specialise in property transactions, while solicitors can also advise on wider legal issues. Either can handle a standard purchase or sale.
