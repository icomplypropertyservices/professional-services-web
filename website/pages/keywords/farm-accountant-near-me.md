## Checking accountants are qualified and regulated

In the UK, the word "accountant" on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can use it. Titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises a firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled.

## FAQs: a farm accountant

### Do I need a specialist farm accountant rather than a general accountant?

A farm accountant understands agricultural property relief, subsidies and payments, herd basis, averaging of profits and diversification. If your finances involve these areas, it is worth asking whether a general accountant has the relevant experience. You can ask about their familiarity with farming matters before you appoint them.

### What is averaging of profits and could it help my farm?

Averaging allows farmers to spread fluctuating profits over two years for tax purposes, which can reduce the impact of a bad year. It is a specific relief for farming and market gardening businesses. A farm accountant can tell you whether you are eligible and how to claim it.

### How does diversification affect my farm's tax position?

Diversification can change the tax treatment of your business, for example by creating a separate trade or affecting reliefs. The rules depend on what you do and how it is structured. A farm accountant can explain the implications for your situation.

### Should I plan for succession early?

Succession planning is often best started well in advance so that there is time to consider all the options and avoid rushed decisions. It can involve agricultural property relief and the transfer of assets. A farm accountant can work alongside you to think through the financial aspects.
