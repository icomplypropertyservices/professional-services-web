## How to check you are in safe hands

"Tax adviser" is not a protected title in the UK. The Chartered Institute of Taxation (CTA qualification) and the Association of Taxation Technicians (ATT) are the main specialist bodies, and many accountants and solicitors also advise on tax. Members of these bodies follow Professional Conduct in Relation to Taxation, a shared code covering honesty, confidentiality and dealing with HMRC.

Tax advisers who provide services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes. HMRC has also been tightening standards for paid tax agents, so it is reasonable to ask about qualifications, supervision and professional indemnity insurance before you go ahead.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## Best CSOP Advisor Near Me: FAQs

### What is a CSOP?

A Company Share Option Plan is a tax-advantaged way to offer share options to staff. CSOP advisers design and implement the plan, agree the valuation with HMRC and make sure the plan rules are followed.

### Should we choose a CSOP or EMI options?

It depends on your company, your staff and what you want the options to achieve. A tax adviser can compare the two with you, and it's worth asking that question directly when you speak to one.

### Why does the share valuation matter?

The valuation of the shares is agreed with HMRC as part of setting up the plan. Getting it right at the start helps the plan work as intended, so ask how the adviser will handle it.

### How do I judge who is the best adviser for us?

There's no official ranking, so look at qualifications and membership of bodies such as the Chartered Institute of Taxation or the Association of Taxation Technicians, ask about supervision for anti-money-laundering purposes and professional indemnity insurance, and check they have experience with CSOPs specifically.

### Do I need a local adviser for a CSOP?

Not necessarily. Many tax advisers work with clients remotely, so you can choose someone based on their CSOP experience rather than their location. If you prefer face-to-face meetings, say so when you enquire, and we can introduce you to an adviser who suits your preference.

### What should I bring to a first meeting about a CSOP?

It helps to have an outline of your company's structure, the number of employees you want to include and any existing share schemes. Also think about what you want the plan to achieve, such as rewarding key staff or giving all employees a stake, so the adviser can tailor their advice.

### Can a CSOP adviser also help with EMI options?

Many tax advisers who work with CSOPs also advise on EMI options, so they can compare the two for you. It's worth asking directly whether they have experience with both, as the right choice depends on your company's size and goals.

### Is tax planning legal?

Using reliefs and allowances as Parliament intended is normal and legal. Aggressive avoidance schemes are a different matter and can carry serious risks. A qualified adviser will explain the difference clearly.
