## How long things usually take

Design and planning usually take several months before building starts. A householder planning application normally has a statutory determination period of around eight weeks once validated, though decisions can take longer. Listed building consent and complex sites take more time.

## Qualifications and registration to look for

"Architect" is a protected title in the UK. Only people on the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance. Many practices are also RIBA Chartered Practices. Architectural designers and technologists can do excellent work too, but they are not registered architects, so it is worth knowing which you are hiring.

Before you appoint anyone, check the ARB register and ask for a written appointment that sets out services, fees and responsibilities. Building regulations approval, planning permission and party wall matters are separate processes that your architect can help you navigate.

## Architect Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need an architect for my project?

Not every project legally requires an architect, but for many building projects an architect can add real value by designing a scheme that meets your needs and guiding you through planning and building regulations. If your project is complex or you want professional design input, an architect is a wise choice.

### What is the difference between an architect and an architectural designer?

An architect is a protected title in the UK, meaning only those on the Architects Registration Board register can use it. Architectural designers can also design buildings and prepare drawings, but they are not regulated in the same way, so it is important to check which you are hiring.

### How do I check if someone is a registered architect?

You can check the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register online or by contacting the ARB directly. It is a quick and free way to confirm that the person you are considering is a registered architect.

### Will my architect handle planning permission for me?

Many architects offer planning application services as part of their work, but you should confirm this in your written appointment. Planning permission is a separate process, and your architect can prepare and submit the application on your behalf.

### Does an architect near me need to visit the property before quoting?

Many architects will want to see the site or at least discuss it in detail before giving a firm quote, because the condition of the building and the plot affect the work involved. A first visit is also your chance to judge whether you would work well together.

### Can I use an architect who is not based in my town?

Yes. What matters most is that they can visit your property and are familiar with your local planning authority, which may be some distance from their office. Convenience for site visits is worth weighing, but relevant experience counts too.

### Do I need an architect for an extension?

Not legally, but a registered architect brings design skill, planning experience and accountability. For simple work, an architectural technologist or designer may be enough. We can match you to the right level of help.
