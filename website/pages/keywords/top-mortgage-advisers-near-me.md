One common misunderstanding is that a nearby adviser will automatically know your local housing market better than a distant one. In practice, what matters is whether the adviser understands your circumstances and can recommend a mortgage that is suitable for you, and advice must be suitable for your situation whichever firm you choose. Distance is mainly about how you prefer to meet, whether face to face, by phone or by video call.

## FAQs about top mortgage advisers near me

### How can I tell if a mortgage adviser is any good if there are no official rankings?

There is no official ranking of mortgage advisers, so 'top' is about your own judgement. Check that the adviser is authorised by the FCA, ask about their qualifications and how they search the market, and see whether they explain things clearly and answer your questions fully.

### Do I need to use a mortgage adviser near me, or can I use one further away?

You can use an adviser wherever they are based, as long as they are authorised by the FCA. Many people prefer a local adviser for face-to-face meetings, but phone and video appointments are common, so distance need not be a barrier.

### What will a mortgage adviser need from me to recommend a suitable mortgage?

They will need details of your income, outgoings, deposit or equity, and the property you want to buy or remortgage. They may also ask about your credit history and future plans, because advice must be suitable for your circumstances.

### Will a mortgage adviser also talk to me about protection?

Many advisers will discuss protection such as life insurance or income protection alongside your mortgage, as it can affect your overall financial position. It is worth asking whether this is part of their service.

### If I approach several mortgage advisers, will each one search the same lenders?

Not necessarily. Brokers must tell you whether they search the whole of the market or a limited panel, so different advisers may look at different lenders. Ask each one the same question so you can compare their reach fairly.

### Should I choose a local adviser or one who works for a lender?

It depends on what you want. A broker may compare products across lenders, while an adviser working for a lender can only offer that lender's mortgages. Either way, check that they are authorised by the FCA and ask how they are paid before you go ahead.

### Will speaking to a broker affect my credit score?

An initial conversation does not. Formal applications usually involve a credit search, and the broker should tell you before that happens.
