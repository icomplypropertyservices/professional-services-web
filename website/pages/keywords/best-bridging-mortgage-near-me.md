## How to check you are in safe hands

Mortgage advice is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Any broker or adviser you use should be authorised, or be an appointed representative of an authorised firm, and you can check this on the FCA register. Advisers typically hold a qualification such as CeMAP.

Brokers must tell you whether they search the whole of the market or a limited panel of lenders, and how they are paid, whether by lender commission, a fee, or both. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

### What is a bridging loan and when might I need one?

A bridging loan is short-term secured finance used for chain breaks, auctions, refurbishment or quick purchases. You might need one if you need to complete quickly or cover a gap between buying and selling.

### What is an exit strategy and why does it matter?

An exit strategy is your plan to repay the bridging loan, usually through sale of the property or refinancing. Lenders will want to see a credible exit before agreeing to lend.

### Is a bridging loan regulated?

If the loan is secured against your own home, it is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority. For other properties, it may not be regulated, but the broker should still be authorised.
