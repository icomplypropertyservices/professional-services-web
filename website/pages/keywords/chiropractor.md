## Checking chiropractors are qualified and regulated

"Chiropractor" is a protected title in the UK. Every chiropractor must be registered with the General Chiropractic Council (GCC), which you can search online. Many are also members of the Royal College of Chiropractors or the British Chiropractic Association.

Chiropractors focus on the spine, joints and muscles, using manual adjustment and mobilisation, plus exercise and lifestyle advice. Evidence is strongest for back and neck pain, and a careful chiropractor will be honest about what they can and cannot help with.

## FAQs: a chiropractor

### Do I need a referral from my GP to see a chiropractor?

No, you can contact a chiropractor directly without a referral. However, it's a good idea to tell your GP if you're having treatment, especially if you have ongoing health conditions.

### Is chiropractic treatment safe?

Chiropractic is generally considered safe when performed by a registered professional. Like any treatment, there are some risks, and a chiropractor should discuss these with you. You should always check that your chiropractor is registered with the GCC.

### What happens during a chiropractic appointment?

The chiropractor will ask about your symptoms and medical history, and may examine your spine and joints. Treatment usually involves manual techniques such as spinal manipulation, and you may be given exercises to do at home.

### How do I choose a chiropractor?

Check that the chiropractor is registered with the GCC, and ask about their experience and approach. It's also worth asking about costs and whether they are a member of a professional body. You should feel comfortable asking questions and be given clear answers.

### Do I need a referral?

No, you can usually book directly. Health insurers may need a GP referral before they pay.
