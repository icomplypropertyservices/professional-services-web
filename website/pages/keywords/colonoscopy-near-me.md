## FAQs: a colonoscopy

### Do I need a referral from my GP to book a private colonoscopy?

Not always, but many private hospitals and insurers do ask for a GP referral. It is worth checking with the consultant's secretary and your insurer before you book.

### How do I check that a private clinic is properly regulated?

In England, private clinics providing regulated healthcare must register with the Care Quality Commission, which publishes inspection ratings. In Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, the regulators are Healthcare Inspectorate Wales, Healthcare Improvement Scotland and the RQIA respectively.

### Can polyps be removed during the colonoscopy?

Yes, polyps can often be removed during the test. Your consultant will explain what they find and whether any further follow-up is needed.

### What should I do if I have bleeding while waiting for my appointment?

You should report any bleeding to a doctor promptly, as it may need to be looked at sooner. If you are worried, contact your GP or call NHS 111 for advice.

### Can I switch back to the NHS after a private consultation?

Yes. You can move between private and NHS care, though you may need to rejoin an NHS waiting list for treatment. Ask your GP how this works locally.
