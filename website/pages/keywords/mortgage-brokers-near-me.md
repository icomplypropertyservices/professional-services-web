## Checking a broker is authorised and how they're paid

Mortgage advice is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Any broker or adviser you use should be authorised, or be an appointed representative of an authorised firm, and you can check this on the FCA register. Advisers typically hold a qualification such as CeMAP.

Brokers must tell you whether they search the whole of the market or a limited panel of lenders, and how they are paid, whether by lender commission, a fee, or both. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

## Mortgage Brokers Near Me: FAQs

### How do I check a mortgage broker is authorised?

You can check on the FCA register whether a broker or adviser is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm. It's worth doing this before you share personal details or pay anything.

### Will a broker search the whole market for me?

Not necessarily. Brokers must tell you whether they search the whole of the market or a limited panel of lenders. Ask this early so you understand what you're comparing.

### How are mortgage brokers paid?

Brokers can be paid by lender commission, a fee from you, or both, and they must explain this to you. Make sure you understand any fee before you start.

### What is a decision in principle?

A decision in principle is an early indication from a lender of how much they might lend you. Getting one early can help you know your budget and show sellers you're serious, though it isn't a firm offer of a mortgage.

### Does a broker near me mean I have to meet them in person?

Not necessarily. Many brokers work by phone, email or video as well as in person, so being nearby is mainly about convenience and ease of contact. Ask each broker how they prefer to work before you decide.

### Why do several brokers near me give different answers?

Brokers can differ in whether they search the whole market or a limited panel of lenders, and in the lenders they can access. That is why it helps to ask the same questions of each and compare how they are paid and what they charge.

### Should I speak to more than one broker near me?

It can help to compare a few before committing, since fees, lender access and how they communicate may vary. Make sure you understand any fee and check FCA authorisation before you share personal details or pay anything.

### Is a broker better than going to my bank?

A broker can compare many lenders and may find options your bank does not offer. Your bank can only offer its own products. Either can suit you; a broker gives you a wider view.
