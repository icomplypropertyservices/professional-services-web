## What to understand about commercial accountants

In the UK, the word "accountant" on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can use it. Titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises a firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled.

## FAQs about commercial accountant near me

### What does a commercial accountant do that a regular accountant doesn't?

A commercial accountant focuses on improving your business's performance through budgeting, pricing, margin analysis and forecasting. They use financial information to help you make better business decisions, rather than just keeping records and filing returns.

### Can a commercial accountant help if my business is growing quickly?

Yes, they are particularly useful when growth is outpacing your financial controls. They can help you put better systems in place, understand your margins and plan for the future with confidence.

### Do I need a commercial accountant who is part-time finance director?

Some commercial accountants work part-time as a finance director, which can be helpful if you need strategic financial leadership without a full-time hire. You can discuss your needs with the accountant and see if this arrangement suits you.

### How do I check a commercial accountant's credentials?

You can ask whether they hold a protected title such as Chartered Accountant, Chartered Certified Accountant or Chartered Management Accountant, and which body supervises them for anti-money-laundering purposes.
