## Understanding HFEA licensing for IVF clinics

In the UK, any clinic offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing or most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA inspects clinics and publishes inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback on its Choose a Fertility Clinic tool, so you can compare licensed clinics near you.

Some simple tests, such as blood hormone checks, may be offered outside HFEA licensing, but any treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always take place at a licensed centre. Checking a clinic's licence is a sensible first step when you're searching for IVF near you.

You can use the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool to see which clinics are licensed and to view their inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback. Any clinic offering IVF or treatments involving eggs, sperm or embryos must be HFEA-licensed.
