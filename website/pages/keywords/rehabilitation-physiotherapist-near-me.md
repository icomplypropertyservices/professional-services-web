## How to check you are in safe hands

"Physiotherapist" and "physical therapist" are protected titles in the UK. Anyone using them must be registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC), and you can check the HCPC register online. Most physiotherapists are also members of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP), shown by the letters MCSP.

Sports therapists, massage therapists and personal trainers can be helpful for some needs, but they are not physiotherapists. For an injury, post-surgery rehabilitation or a neurological condition, check that you are booking a HCPC-registered physiotherapist.

## Getting to a clinic regularly when you need rehabilitation close to home

Rehabilitation is rarely a one-off appointment, so the near me in your search matters more than it might for other kinds of care. Choosing a clinic you can reach without a long journey, and at a time of day that fits around work or caring responsibilities, makes it easier to keep attending when progress feels slow. It is worth thinking about parking, public transport and whether someone can help you get there in the early stages.

Before you commit, ask how the clinic handles the practical side of rehabilitation: whether appointments are one to one, how they share exercises for you to do between sessions, and what happens if you have a setback. A good plan should be specific to your goals and adjusted as you improve, rather than a fixed set of exercises handed over at the first visit. It also helps to know who you would see if your usual physiotherapist is away.

If travelling is difficult, say so when you enquire. Some people need home visits at first and then move to clinic-based sessions as they get stronger, and others prefer to start in a clinic and continue with a plan at home. Explaining your situation means the introduction we make can reflect what is actually workable for you week to week.

## Warning signs to watch for

Most professionals are honest and competent, but it is worth knowing the warning signs:

- A long block of prepaid sessions before any assessment
- No referral back when problems do not improve
- Treatments promised to cure everything
- No HCPC registration
