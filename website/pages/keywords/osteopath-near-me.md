## Qualifications and registration to look for

"Osteopath" is a protected title in the UK. Every osteopath must be registered with the General Osteopathic Council (GOsC), which sets standards and investigates concerns. You can check the GOsC register online before you book.

Osteopaths use hands-on techniques such as massage, stretching and joint mobilisation, along with advice on exercise and posture. They assess your medical history first and should refer you to your GP if your symptoms need medical investigation.

## Osteopath Near Me: FAQs

### How do I check that an osteopath is properly registered?

You can check the General Osteopathic Council (GOsC) register online. Every osteopath must be registered with the GOsC to use the title "osteopath" in the UK.

### What should I expect at my first osteopathy appointment?

The osteopath will take a full case history, including your medical background and current symptoms. They will then assess you and discuss whether hands-on treatment is appropriate, and they may give you exercises or advice to follow at home.

### Will an osteopath refer me to my GP if needed?

Osteopaths are trained to recognise when symptoms need medical investigation. If your symptoms are not suitable for osteopathic treatment, they should refer you to your GP.

### Can I see an osteopath near me without a referral from my GP?

Yes, you can usually book an appointment with an osteopath directly without a referral. It is still a good idea to mention any relevant medical history when you book.

### Is it better to choose the osteopath closest to me?

Convenience helps, especially if you need several appointments, but it should not be the only consideration. Check GOsC registration and ask about their approach to your problem before deciding, even if that means travelling a little further.

### Can I contact more than one local osteopath before booking?

Yes. It is reasonable to ring a few osteopaths near you, ask what a first appointment involves and how they handle your kind of symptoms, then choose the one you feel most comfortable with.

### What if there is no osteopath in my immediate area?

Widen your search gradually and weigh up the extra travel against how often you may need to attend. Ask about appointment times and whether advice on exercises can support you between visits.

### Is treatment painful?

Some techniques can feel uncomfortable, and you may feel sore for a day or two. Your practitioner should explain what to expect and adapt treatment to your comfort.
