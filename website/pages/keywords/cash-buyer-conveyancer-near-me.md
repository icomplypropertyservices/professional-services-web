## Qualifications and registration to look for

Conveyancing in England and Wales is a reserved legal activity. It can be carried out by solicitors regulated by the SRA or by licensed conveyancers regulated by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers (CLC). Both must hold professional indemnity insurance and follow strict rules on handling client money. In Scotland, property transactions are handled by solicitors under Scottish law.

Many lenders only work with conveyancers on their panel, and the Law Society's Conveyancing Quality Scheme (CQS) is a common accreditation. Check that your chosen firm is on your lender's panel before you instruct them.

## Why searches and source-of-funds checks still matter for cash buyers

Even without a lender, your conveyancer will still carry out searches such as local authority and environmental checks. These protect you from hidden issues like planning problems or flood risk, which could affect the property's value or your enjoyment of it.

Your conveyancer is also legally required to verify the source of your funds to prevent money laundering. This means you'll need to provide evidence of where the cash came from, such as savings or a property sale. Being prepared for this can help the purchase move smoothly.

## FAQs: a conveyancer for cash buyers

### Do I really need a conveyancer if I'm paying cash?

Yes, conveyancing is a reserved legal activity in England and Wales, so it must be carried out by a qualified solicitor or licensed conveyancer. They will handle the legal transfer, searches, and source-of-funds checks on your behalf.

### Can a cash purchase move faster than a mortgage purchase?

It can, because there's no lender to satisfy and no mortgage offer to wait for. However, the legal process still involves searches and checks, so timescales vary depending on the property and the parties involved.

### What is a source-of-funds check and why is it needed?

It's a legal requirement for conveyancers to verify where your money comes from to prevent money laundering. You'll need to provide documents such as bank statements or sale contracts to show the funds are legitimate.

### How do I choose a conveyancer near me?

You can ask for recommendations, check that the firm is regulated by the SRA or CLC, and compare quotes. It's also worth considering their location for ease of communication and whether they have experience with cash purchases.

### When should I instruct a conveyancer?

As soon as your offer is accepted, or before you list a property for sale. Instructing early helps you avoid delays.
