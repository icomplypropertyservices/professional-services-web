## What to understand about burnout counselling

Burnout counselling is not about quick fixes. Recovery usually takes time, and a counsellor can help you understand your patterns and make gradual changes to how you work and rest. It can also be useful to talk to your GP about time off if your health is affected, and your employer may offer support such as an employee assistance programme.

In the UK, "counsellor" and "psychotherapist" are not protected titles, so it is worth checking that the person you see is on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. Practitioner psychologists, who use titles like clinical or counselling psychologist, must be registered with the HCPC. A good therapist will explain their approach, confidentiality and its limits in the first session.

## FAQs: burnout counselling

### Do I need a referral from my GP to see a burnout counsellor?

No, you can contact a counsellor directly without a referral. However, if your burnout is affecting your health, it is still worth talking to your GP about time off and support.

### How do I know if a counsellor is properly qualified?

Check whether they are on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. These registers require training, supervision and a code of ethics.

### Can counselling help if my burnout is caused by caring responsibilities rather than work?

Yes, burnout can come from chronic caring stress as well as work. A counsellor can help you recover and set boundaries in either situation.

### Will my employer find out I am having counselling?

Counselling is confidential, and a counsellor will not contact your employer without your consent. They will explain the limits of confidentiality in your first session.

### What is the difference between counselling and psychotherapy?

The terms overlap. Counselling often focuses on a specific problem over a shorter period, while psychotherapy may explore deeper patterns over a longer time. A good practitioner will explain their approach.
