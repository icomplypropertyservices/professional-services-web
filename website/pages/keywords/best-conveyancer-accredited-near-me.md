## What 'accredited' really means for a conveyancer

When you search for an accredited conveyancer, you're probably looking for reassurance that the person handling your property transaction meets a recognised standard. In England and Wales, conveyancing is a reserved legal activity, so it must be carried out by a solicitor regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority or a licensed conveyancer regulated by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers. Both must hold professional indemnity insurance and follow strict rules on handling client money, which gives you important protections.

Accreditation can also mean additional schemes, such as the Law Society's Conveyancing Quality Scheme, which many lenders recognise. However, not all accreditations are the same, and some firms may hold several while others hold none beyond their basic regulation.
