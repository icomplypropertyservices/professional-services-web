## Qualifications and registration to look for

"Counsellor" and "psychotherapist" are not protected titles in the UK. To protect yourself, choose a practitioner on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. Practitioner psychologists, who use titles such as clinical or counselling psychologist, must be registered with the HCPC.

Accredited registers require training, supervision, a code of ethics and a complaints procedure. A good therapist will explain their approach, confidentiality and its limits in the first session.

"Therapist" covers many approaches. Ask about training, which register they are on and the type of therapy they offer.

## EMDR Therapist Near Me: FAQs

### What is EMDR and how does it work?

EMDR stands for eye movement desensitisation and reprocessing. It is a therapy that helps people process traumatic memories by guiding them through eye movements or other forms of bilateral stimulation while recalling the memory. NICE recommends EMDR for PTSD.

### Do I need a referral to see an EMDR therapist?

You do not need a referral to see an EMDR therapist privately. You can contact a therapist directly. However, if you are seeking therapy through the NHS, a referral from your GP or another professional is usually needed.

### How do I check if an EMDR therapist is properly qualified?

Check whether the therapist is accredited by the EMDR Association UK, which sets standards for EMDR training and practice. Also check that they are on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS, or if they are a practitioner psychologist, that they are registered with the HCPC.

### Is EMDR suitable for everyone?

EMDR is generally considered suitable for adults and children with PTSD, but it may not be right for everyone. A therapist will assess your situation and may recommend preparation sessions first. If you are unsure, you can discuss it with a GP or mental health professional.

### What is the difference between counselling and psychotherapy?

The terms overlap. Counselling often focuses on a specific problem over a shorter period, while psychotherapy may explore deeper patterns over a longer time. A good practitioner will explain their approach.
