## How to check you are in safe hands

Insurance brokers and intermediaries in the UK must be authorised by the FCA, or be appointed representatives of an authorised firm. Check them on the FCA register. Many brokers belong to the British Insurance Brokers' Association (BIBA), and some hold Chartered status from the Chartered Insurance Institute.

Brokers must understand your demands and needs, explain key policy features and exclusions, and tell you how they are paid. Complaints that are not resolved can go to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

## Construction Insurance Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need employers' liability insurance for construction work?

Most employers in the UK are required by law to have employers' liability insurance. If you employ anyone, even family members, you should check whether you need it. A broker can confirm the rules for your situation.

### What is contract works insurance?

Contract works insurance covers the building project itself while it is under construction, including materials and work in progress. It can protect against damage or loss during the project. A broker can explain how it fits with your other cover.

### Can I get cover for hired plant and equipment?

Yes, hired plant can be covered under a construction insurance policy. You should tell the broker what plant you hire and how it will be used. They will explain any conditions or exclusions.

### How do I check that an insurance broker is authorised?

You can check whether a broker is authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) on the FCA register. Many brokers also belong to the British Insurance Brokers' Association (BIBA).

### How do I choose a construction insurance broker near me?

Look for a broker who is authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) and has experience with construction risks. Ask how they will assess your needs and explain your options, and check that they can meet the requirements of your contract.

### Can a local broker help with contract works and plant cover?

Yes, a local broker can arrange contract works cover to protect your project and plant cover for hired equipment. They will explain any conditions, such as where plant can be used, and help you meet your contractual obligations.

### What is non-disclosure?

Failing to tell an insurer something relevant. It can reduce or void a claim, so answer questions fully and ask your broker if unsure.
