## Qualifications and registration to look for

"Physiotherapist" and "physical therapist" are protected titles in the UK. Anyone using them must be registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC), and you can check the HCPC register online. Most physiotherapists are also members of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP), shown by the letters MCSP.

Sports therapists, massage therapists and personal trainers can be helpful for some needs, but they are not physiotherapists. For an injury, post-surgery rehabilitation or a neurological condition, check that you are booking a HCPC-registered physiotherapist.

## What to understand about post operative physiotherapy

Post operative physiotherapy is not a one-size-fits-all approach. Your surgeon will have a specific protocol for your operation, which sets out what you can and cannot do at each stage. A physiotherapist will follow that protocol to help you restore movement and strength safely. It is important to bring the protocol with you to your first appointment.

The title 'physiotherapist' is protected in the UK, so anyone using it must be registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC). You can check the HCPC register online to confirm someone's registration.

## When to think twice

Most professionals are honest and competent, but it is worth knowing the warning signs:

- No HCPC registration
- Treatments promised to cure everything
- No home exercise plan or progress review
- A long block of prepaid sessions before any assessment

## Questions to ask the physiotherapist

- What experience do you have in treating patients after my specific operation?
- How will you help me manage swelling and pain during rehabilitation?

## FAQs about post operative physiotherapy near me

### When can I start physiotherapy after my operation?

Your surgeon will advise you on when it is safe to start. It is important to follow their guidance and bring any written instructions to your first physiotherapy appointment.

### Do I need a referral from my surgeon to see a physiotherapist?

Not always, but it helps to have your surgeon's protocol or a letter outlining your operation. Some physiotherapists may ask for this before your first appointment.

### What should I bring to my first physiotherapy appointment?

Bring your surgeon's protocol or any written instructions, a list of your medications, and wear comfortable clothing that allows the physiotherapist to assess the affected area.

### How do I check if a physiotherapist is properly qualified?

You can check the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC) register online to confirm that the physiotherapist is registered. Membership of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP) is shown by the letters MCSP after their name.

### Do I need a GP referral for private physio?

No, you can usually book directly as a self-paying patient. Your insurer may require a referral, so check your policy.
