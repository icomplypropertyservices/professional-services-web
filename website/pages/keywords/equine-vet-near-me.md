## How fees usually work (POA)

Veterinary fees vary a great deal between practices, regions and ownership groups, and the CMA has looked closely at transparency in this market. ## Knowing who you're dealing with

Every vet practising in the UK must be registered with the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons (RCVS), and you can search the RCVS register by name before you book. Many practices also join the voluntary RCVS Practice Standards Scheme, which inspects premises, equipment and clinical processes, so it's worth asking whether the practice you're considering is part of it.

Some equine vets hold further qualifications such as an RCVS certificate, advanced practitioner status or specialist status. If your horse has a complex or unusual condition, it's reasonable to ask whether the vet treating it holds one of these, or whether they would refer you to someone who does.

## Equine Vet Near Me: FAQs

### Can I check an equine vet's registration before booking?

Yes. Every vet practising in the UK must be registered with the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons, and you can search the RCVS register by name. Veterinary nurses have their own RCVS register too, so you can check them as well.

### What happens at a pre-purchase examination?

Pre-purchase vettings follow a standard format, and it's worth agreeing with the vet which stages you need for the horse you're buying. The vet will examine the horse and report their findings so you can make an informed decision.

### Should I ask about out-of-hours cover for colic?

Yes, it's sensible to ask how emergency colic calls are covered at night before you need them. Colic can become serious quickly, so knowing who to call and where your horse might be seen matters.

### What if my horse needs imaging or surgery?

It's worth checking whether the practice has a clinic for imaging or surgery, or whether they would refer you to another practice for those. Asking this in advance helps you plan if your horse needs more than a yard visit.

### Can you help in an animal emergency?
