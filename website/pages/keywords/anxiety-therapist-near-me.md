## What to know about finding an anxiety therapist near you

In the UK, 'counsellor' and 'psychotherapist' are not protected titles, so anyone can call themselves one. To protect yourself, look for a practitioner on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. Practitioner psychologists, who use titles like clinical or counselling psychologist, must be registered with the HCPC.

Accredited registers require training, supervision, a code of ethics and a complaints procedure. A good therapist will explain their approach, confidentiality and its limits in the first session. Anxiety therapy often involves approaches such as CBT and exposure work, and you can ask about their experience with your type of anxiety.

## Anxiety Therapist Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a referral from my GP to see an anxiety therapist?

No, you can contact a private anxiety therapist directly without a referral. However, if your anxiety is severe or you have other health concerns, it's a good idea to speak to your GP as well.

### How do I know if a therapist is qualified to help with anxiety?

Check whether they are on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. You can also ask about their training and experience with your specific type of anxiety.

### What happens in the first session with an anxiety therapist?

The therapist will usually ask about your anxiety, what you hope to achieve, and explain their approach. They should also cover confidentiality and its limits, and you can ask any questions you have.

### Can anxiety therapy be done online or do I need to see someone in person?

Many therapists offer online or telephone sessions as well as in-person appointments. When you enquire, let us know your preference so we can introduce you to a professional who suits your needs.

### I get anxious about leaving the house. Can I still see a therapist near me?

Yes, and it's worth telling us this when you enquire so we can introduce you to a counsellor who understands it. Some therapists offer online or telephone sessions as a first step, and you can discuss with them what feels manageable.

### How do I compare anxiety therapists near me without reviews or ratings?

Ask each therapist about their training, their experience with your type of anxiety, and the approaches they use, such as CBT or exposure work. Check whether they are on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS, and see how they explain confidentiality and its limits.

### Is seeing an anxiety therapist the same as getting NHS talking therapy?

No. NHS talking therapies are accessed through your GP or self-referral to an NHS service, while a private anxiety therapist is someone you contact and pay directly. If you're unsure which route suits you, your GP can advise, and NHS 111 can help if you need urgent advice.

### What is the difference between counselling and psychotherapy?

The terms overlap. Counselling often focuses on a specific problem over a shorter period, while psychotherapy may explore deeper patterns over a longer time. A good practitioner will explain their approach.
