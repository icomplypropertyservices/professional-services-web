## Qualifications and registration to look for

Mortgage advice is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Any broker or adviser you use should be authorised, or be an appointed representative of an authorised firm, and you can check this on the FCA register. Advisers typically hold a qualification such as CeMAP.

Brokers must tell you whether they search the whole of the market or a limited panel of lenders, and how they are paid, whether by lender commission, a fee, or both. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## FAQs about top mortgage advisor near me

### How do I check that a mortgage adviser is properly authorised?

Mortgage advice is regulated by the FCA, so any adviser or broker should be authorised, or be an appointed representative of an authorised firm. You can check this yourself on the FCA register before you share any personal details.

### Does a top mortgage advisor near me mean the cheapest one?

Not necessarily, and it's worth being wary of anyone who claims to be the best or cheapest. Ask how they are paid, whether by lender commission, a fee, or both, and compare that with what they offer before deciding.

### Will the adviser look at the whole market?

Brokers must tell you whether they search the whole of the market or a limited panel of lenders. Ask this early so you understand the range of options they can consider for you.

### Can I get advice on protection as well as the mortgage?

It's sensible to ask about protection such as life cover at the same time, as it can be arranged alongside your mortgage. Mention this when you enquire so the adviser knows to cover it.

### Should I bring documents to a first meeting with a mortgage adviser?

It helps to bring proof of income, details of your deposit and information about any debts or existing mortgage. Having these to hand means the adviser can give you a clearer picture of what may be possible rather than a general chat.

### Is a local adviser better than one I only speak to by phone?

Not necessarily. Some people find meeting in person easier when discussing their finances, while others prefer the flexibility of phone or remote advice. What matters most is that the adviser is authorised, explains how they are paid and finds a mortgage suitable for you.

### What if I'm not ready to apply for a mortgage yet?

You can still have an initial conversation to understand your options and what you might need to do before applying. Just be clear about your timing when you enquire so the adviser can tailor their advice to where you are.

### Is a broker better than going to my bank?

A broker can compare many lenders and may find options your bank does not offer. Your bank can only offer its own products. Either can suit you; a broker gives you a wider view.
