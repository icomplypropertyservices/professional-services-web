## FAQs about life cover advisor near me

### Do I need life cover if I already have cover through my job?

It depends on what your employer provides. Death-in-service benefits are often a multiple of your salary, but they may not be enough to cover a mortgage or long-term family costs. An advisor can help you compare what you have with what you might need.

### Will my health affect what I pay for life cover?

Yes, your health and lifestyle can affect your premiums. Advisors will ask about your medical history and may need to approach insurers who specialise in certain conditions. Being honest is important so your policy pays out when needed.

### What's the difference between a joint policy and single policies?

A joint policy covers two people and usually pays out once, on the first death. Single policies are separate, so each person has their own cover, which can be more flexible but may cost more. An advisor can explain which suits your situation.

### Should I put my life cover in trust?

Putting a policy in trust can help ensure the payout goes to the people you choose without waiting for probate, which can speed things up. It can also have inheritance tax benefits. An advisor can tell you whether it's appropriate for you.

### Is a broker more expensive than buying direct?

Not necessarily. Brokers can access insurers and schemes not available directly, and help with claims. The right choice depends on how complex your needs are.
