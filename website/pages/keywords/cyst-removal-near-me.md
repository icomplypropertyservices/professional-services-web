## Cyst Removal Near Me: FAQs

### Does a cyst always need removing?

No. Some cysts cause no trouble and can be left alone, while others become sore, inflamed or repeatedly troublesome. A proper assessment is the way to decide whether removal is worthwhile for you.

### Will removing a cyst leave a scar?

Removal involves a cut, so a scar is likely, though its size and position depend on the lump and the technique used. Ask the professional where the scar would sit and how it is likely to heal.

### Why might the tissue be sent for histology?

Examining the removed tissue under a microscope helps confirm exactly what the lump was. It is a routine step for many removals, and you can ask how and when you would receive the result.

### How do I check a private clinic or consultant?

Private clinics providing regulated healthcare in England must register with the Care Quality Commission, with different regulators in Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland.

### Do I need a GP referral to see a private consultant?

Self-paying patients can sometimes book directly, but many consultants prefer a referral, and insurers usually require one. A private GP can provide one quickly.
