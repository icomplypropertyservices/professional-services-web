## What to understand about dentures and regulation

Dentures are removable plates, full or partial, made to fit your mouth and replace missing teeth. A dentist will take impressions and assess your bite to ensure the denture is comfortable and functional. Adjustments are often needed in the first few weeks as your mouth gets used to the denture.

Dentists and dental care professionals must be registered with the General Dental Council (GDC), and you can check the register online. Private clinics providing regulated healthcare in England must register with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which inspects and publishes ratings; in Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland different regulators apply. If something goes wrong with private treatment, the Dental Complaints Service can help if the practice's own complaints process does not resolve it.

## How to check you are in safe hands

Dentists, dental hygienists, dental therapists and dental nurses must be registered with the General Dental Council (GDC), and you can check the GDC register online. Specialist titles such as orthodontist or endodontist can only be used by people on a GDC specialist list. Private clinics providing regulated healthcare in England must register with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which inspects and publishes ratings. In Wales the regulator is Healthcare Inspectorate Wales, in Scotland Healthcare Improvement Scotland, and in Northern Ireland the RQIA.

If something goes wrong with private dental treatment, start with the practice's complaints process. The Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints that are not resolved.

## FAQs: dentures

### How do I find a dentist for dentures near me?

You can search for local dental practices that offer denture services, check that the dentist is registered with the General Dental Council, and read about the practice's services. iComply can introduce you to a private dentist who will assess your needs and provide a quote.

### Are dentures always removable?

Most dentures are removable, but implant-retained dentures are anchored to implants in the jaw and are more stable. Your dentist can advise which type is suitable for you.

### Will my denture need adjustments?

Yes, adjustments are normal at first as your mouth gets used to the denture. Your dentist can make small changes to improve comfort and fit.

### How should I clean my dentures?

You should clean your dentures daily using a soft brush and denture cleaner, following your dentist's advice. Regular cleaning helps prevent stains and keeps your mouth healthy.

### Can I see a private dentist and stay with my NHS dentist?

Yes, you can use both. Some patients see a private dentist for specific treatments and keep NHS care for routine check-ups, if their NHS practice agrees.
