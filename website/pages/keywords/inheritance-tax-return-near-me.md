## Why the valuation matters so much

The inheritance tax return rests on the values you put on everything in the estate, so a house, a share portfolio or a business interest needs a defensible figure rather than a guess. If a value is later found to be too low, HMRC can revisit it, and interest runs on tax that is paid late.

Not every estate has to file a full account: some qualify as excepted estates, which means a shorter route. Whether yours does depends on the size and makeup of the estate, so it is worth having that checked before you assume either way. A tax adviser can tell you which route applies and prepare the figures.

## Checking tax advisers are qualified and regulated

"Tax adviser" is not a protected title in the UK. The Chartered Institute of Taxation (CTA qualification) and the Association of Taxation Technicians (ATT) are the main specialist bodies, and many accountants and solicitors also advise on tax. Members of these bodies follow Professional Conduct in Relation to Taxation, a shared code covering honesty, confidentiality and dealing with HMRC.

Tax advisers who provide services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes. HMRC has also been tightening standards for paid tax agents, so it is reasonable to ask about qualifications, supervision and professional indemnity insurance before you go ahead.

## Inheritance Tax Return Near Me: FAQs

### Do I always have to file an inheritance tax return when someone dies?

No. Some estates qualify as excepted estates and take a shorter route, while others need a full inheritance tax account. Whether yours does depends on the size and nature of the estate, so it is worth having that checked rather than assumed.

### When does inheritance tax have to be paid?

Tax is generally due before probate is granted, and interest runs on anything paid late. The exact timing depends on the estate and the date of death, so ask the adviser to set out the dates that apply to you.

### Can a local tax adviser deal with HMRC for me?

Yes, a tax adviser can act as your agent and correspond with HMRC on the estate's behalf. Ask them to confirm this at the outset so you know who is handling what.

### How do I judge whether an adviser is right for this work?

"Tax adviser" is not a protected title in the UK, so ask about qualifications such as CTA or ATT membership, and about supervision and professional indemnity insurance. Comparing fees and what is included helps you judge for yourself rather than relying on labels.

### Should I reply to HMRC before speaking to an adviser?

Note the deadline on the letter, but avoid sending a detailed reply before you have advice if the matter is complex. An adviser can often ask HMRC for more time where it is reasonable.
