## What to understand about pension advice and regulation

Pension advice in the UK is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). You can check that any adviser or firm is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm, on the FCA register. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme may protect you if an authorised firm fails.

Some pension decisions carry special rules. Advice on transferring out of a defined benefit (final salary) scheme above a set value must come from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions, and safeguarded benefits deserve particular care. Free, impartial guidance is available from Pension Wise for people who have reached the age when they can access their defined contribution pension.

## Comparing pension advisers close to home

Searching for pension advisers near me, in the plural, usually means you are weighing up more than one option rather than simply wanting the first name that comes up. That is a sensible approach, because the right adviser for you depends on the kind of pension you hold and the decision in front of you, not just on who is closest. Take a little time to compare before you commit to anything.

Start by checking each firm on the FCA register and confirming the permissions match what you need, particularly pension transfer specialist permissions if a final salary scheme is involved. Then ask each one how they charge, whether a written quote is provided before work begins, and whether they will meet locally or prefer to speak remotely. Comparing these answers side by side tells you far more than a postcode alone.

It also helps to think about how you would like the relationship to work. Some people want an adviser they can visit in person with paperwork in hand, while others are happy with phone and video appointments. Neither is better than the other; what matters is that you feel able to ask questions and understand the answers.

## Red flags when choosing a pension adviser

Most professionals are honest and competent, but it is worth knowing the warning signs:

- Offers to access a pension before age 55 (rising to 57 from 2028)
- Overseas investments or unusual schemes
- Pressure to transfer quickly
- Firms not on the FCA register

## Pension Advisors Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a pension adviser who is local to me?

Not necessarily. Many pension advisers work remotely, but meeting in person can help if you prefer to discuss paperwork face to face. When you search for pension advisers near me, we can introduce you to a professional who suits your preference.

### What is the difference between a pension adviser and Pension Wise?

Pension Wise offers free, impartial guidance for people with defined contribution pensions who have reached the age when they can access them, but it does not give personal recommendations. A pension adviser can give regulated advice tailored to your circumstances and must be authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority.

### Can I transfer my defined benefit pension without advice?

For transfers above a set value, you must take advice from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions. This rule is in place to protect you, as defined benefit schemes often carry valuable safeguards.

### How do I check that a pension adviser is regulated?

You can check the Financial Conduct Authority register to confirm that the adviser or firm is authorised or is an appointed representative. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

### How many pension advisers should I compare before choosing one?

There is no set number, and it is fine to speak to a few before deciding. What matters more is that each one is authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority for the advice you need and that you understand their charges before any work starts.

### Is a local pension adviser better than one further away?

Not automatically. Proximity can make face-to-face meetings easier, but what really counts is the adviser's permissions, experience with your type of pension, and how clearly they explain things. Many people are well served by an adviser they speak to remotely.

### Should I combine my pensions?

Sometimes it makes life simpler and cheaper, but some older pensions have valuable guarantees or low charges. An adviser checks before recommending a transfer.
