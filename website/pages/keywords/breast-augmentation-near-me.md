## What to understand about breast augmentation and finding a local surgeon

In the UK, cosmetic surgery should be carried out by a surgeon on the GMC's specialist register, usually in plastic surgery or a related specialty, in a hospital or clinic registered with the CQC (or the equivalent regulator outside England). Many surgeons are members of BAAPS or BAPRAS, and the Royal College of Surgeons offers a cosmetic surgery certification.

The GMC requires doctors offering cosmetic interventions to give patients time to reflect before agreeing to surgery, and not to use high-pressure sales. Your consultation should be with the surgeon who will operate, so you can ask questions and make an informed choice.

## FAQs about breast augmentation near me

### How do I check that a cosmetic surgeon is properly qualified for breast augmentation?

You can check that the surgeon is on the GMC's specialist register, usually in plastic surgery or a related specialty. It is also worth confirming that the hospital or clinic is registered with the CQC or the equivalent regulator in your nation.

### Will my implant details be recorded somewhere?

Yes, implant details go on the Breast and Cosmetic Implant Registry, which helps trace implants if needed. You can ask your surgeon to confirm this.

### Do I need to choose the implant size at the first consultation?

No, you should discuss size realistically and take time to reflect before deciding. The GMC requires doctors to give you time to reflect and not to use high-pressure sales.

### What should I expect at a consultation for breast augmentation?

Your consultation should be with the surgeon who will operate, and you should have the opportunity to ask questions and discuss your goals. You should not feel pressured to book surgery on the day.

### Is surgery abroad cheaper and safe?

Costs can be lower, but follow-up care and complications can be harder to manage. If you are considering it, ask how aftercare and complications will be handled once you are home.
