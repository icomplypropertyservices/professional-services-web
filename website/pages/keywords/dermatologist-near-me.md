## What to understand when choosing a dermatologist near you

A dermatologist is a hospital consultant who diagnoses and treats skin, hair and nail conditions. In the UK, hospital consultants should appear on the General Medical Council's specialist register for their field, and you can check this online. Private clinics providing regulated healthcare in England must register with the Care Quality Commission, which inspects and publishes ratings; in Wales the regulator is Healthcare Inspectorate Wales, in Scotland Healthcare Improvement Scotland, and in Northern Ireland the RQIA.

Many private dermatologists also work in the NHS. If you have private medical insurance, your insurer may require a GP referral and may limit you to certain consultants or hospitals, so check before you book. Photos of flare-ups can help the consultant understand your condition, and changing moles should be seen promptly.

### How do I check if a dermatologist is properly qualified?

You can check the General Medical Council's specialist register online to confirm a doctor is registered as a specialist in dermatology. This is a public register and free to search.

Bring any relevant medical records, a list of medications you take, and photos of flare-ups if the condition comes and goes. This helps the consultant understand your situation.
