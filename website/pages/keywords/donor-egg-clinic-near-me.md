## Checking fertility clinics are qualified and regulated

Clinics offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback.

Simple tests such as blood hormone checks may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing, but treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre.

Clinics vary from single-practitioner rooms to multi-specialist centres. Ask who will see you, their registration and what facilities are on site.

## Licensing and what donor treatment in the UK involves

The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback, which is a sound way to judge a clinic for yourself. Treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre.

Donor treatment in the UK has particular features worth understanding before you choose a clinic. ## When to think twice

Be cautious if you notice any of the following:

- Clinics not licensed by the HFEA for the treatment
- No offer of counselling
- Pressure to buy add-ons
- Success rates that are not comparable to HFEA data

## FAQs about donor egg clinic near me

### Do donor egg clinics near me have to be licensed?

Yes. Clinics offering donor treatment, IVF and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority. You can check a clinic's licensed status, inspection ratings and patient feedback using the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool.

### Will the donor be anonymous?

In the UK, donors can be identified by children at eighteen, so anonymity is not the same as it once was. A clinic can explain what this means for you and for any child born from donated eggs.

### Why is counselling mentioned so often in donor treatment?

Counselling is an important part of donor egg treatment and gives you space to think through the implications before you go ahead. Ask each clinic how counselling is arranged and when it takes place in their pathway.

### How long might I wait for donor eggs?

Waiting times vary between clinics, so there is no single answer. It is worth asking each clinic you consider about its current wait and how it keeps you updated.

### How do I compare clinics?

Use the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool, which gives ratings, outcomes and patient feedback for licensed clinics.
