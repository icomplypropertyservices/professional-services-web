## Qualifications and registration to look for

Financial advice in the UK is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Check that any adviser or firm is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm, on the FCA register. If something goes wrong you can complain to the firm, then to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme may protect you if an authorised firm fails.

Advisers must tell you whether their advice is independent (considering the whole market) or restricted (limited to certain products or providers), and how they charge. Chartered status from the Chartered Insurance Institute or the Personal Finance Society, and certifications such as CFP, show advanced qualifications.

## FAQs: a financial planner

### What is the difference between a financial planner and a financial adviser?

The terms are often used interchangeably in the UK, but financial planning tends to take a whole-life view, linking your goals with investments, pensions, tax and protection rather than focusing on a single product. A financial adviser may offer planning as part of their service, so it is worth asking how they work.

### How do I check that a financial planner near me is properly authorised?

Financial advice in the UK is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, so you can check the adviser or firm on the FCA register. Confirm whether they are authorised directly or are an appointed representative of an authorised firm before you engage them.

### What should I have ready before my first meeting?

It helps to gather details of your pensions, savings, investments and any protection policies, along with a sense of your goals and concerns. Having this to hand means the planner can give you a more useful first conversation about how they might help.

### What can I do if I am unhappy with the advice I receive?

You can complain to the firm first, and if that does not resolve things you can take your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service. The Financial Services Compensation Scheme may also protect you if an authorised firm fails.

### Should I contact several financial planners near me before choosing one?

It is reasonable to speak to more than one, since the first conversation usually costs nothing and helps you judge how each planner works. Ask the same questions of each so you can compare their answers fairly, and remember that being local is only one factor among several.

### Does a nearby financial planner have to meet me in person?

No. Many planners work remotely or offer a mix of video, phone and in-person meetings, so a firm slightly further away may still suit you. If meeting face to face locally matters to you, say so early on so the planner can tell you what they can offer.

### Is the first meeting free?

Many firms offer a free initial consultation to explain their service and charges. Formal advice is then charged as agreed in writing.
