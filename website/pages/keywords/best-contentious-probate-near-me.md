## Checking wills and probate specialists are qualified and regulated

Writing a will is not a reserved legal activity in England and Wales, so some will writers are not regulated. Solicitors (SRA) and chartered legal executives (CILEx) are regulated, insured and covered by the Legal Ombudsman. Many specialists are also members of STEP (the Society of Trust and Estate Practitioners) or Solicitors for the Elderly. Applying for a grant of probate on someone else's behalf for a fee is a reserved activity.

When choosing a provider, ask whether they are regulated, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance and where your original will is stored. Scotland has its own rules on succession and confirmation, so make sure your adviser practises in the right jurisdiction.

## FAQs about best contentious probate near me

### What is contentious probate?

Contentious probate covers disputes about wills and estates, such as challenges to the validity of a will, claims under the Inheritance Act and disagreements with executors. It involves legal proceedings to resolve these issues.

### How do I choose the best contentious probate specialist near me?

Look for a professional who is regulated, insured and experienced in handling disputes like yours. Ask about their membership of specialist bodies and whether they can meet you locally if that is important to you.

### Are there time limits for Inheritance Act claims?

Yes, Inheritance Act claims have a six-month time limit from the grant of probate. It is important to seek advice quickly if you are considering such a claim.

### Can a caveat stop probate?

A caveat can stop a grant of probate temporarily while a dispute is investigated. It is a formal notice that prevents the grant from being issued without your knowledge.

### How do I judge which contentious probate specialist is best for me?

There is no official ranking, so look for a professional who is regulated, insured and experienced in the type of dispute you have. Ask about their approach, their membership of specialist bodies and whether they can meet locally if that matters to you.

### Can I use a local professional for a dispute involving an estate in another part of the UK?

Yes, but make sure they practise in the right jurisdiction, as Scotland has its own rules on succession and confirmation. If the estate is in England or Wales, a local professional can often handle matters remotely.

### What should I bring to a first meeting about contentious probate?

Bring any relevant documents, such as the will, correspondence with other parties and details of the estate. Also note any key dates, such as the date of the grant of probate, as time limits may apply.

### Do I need a solicitor for probate?

Not always. Executors can apply for probate themselves for simpler estates. Many choose professional help where there is property, inheritance tax, overseas assets or family tension.
