## Contents Insurance Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need contents insurance if I rent?

Your landlord's building insurance covers the structure, not your belongings. Contents insurance protects your possessions, and some policies also cover accidental damage to the landlord's fixtures and fittings.

### How do I value my contents for insurance?

Go room by room and estimate the cost of replacing each item with a new equivalent. Don't include items that are permanently fitted, as these are usually part of the building. A broker can help you work out a realistic sum.

### Is my jewellery covered away from home?

Many policies offer optional cover for valuables away from home, but there may be limits per item. Tell your broker about any high-value pieces so they can advise on the right cover.

### What is new-for-old cover?

New-for-old means that if an item is stolen or damaged beyond repair, the insurer replaces it with a new one of similar quality, rather than deducting for wear and tear. It's common in contents policies, but always check the terms.

### Is a broker more expensive than buying direct?

Not necessarily. Brokers can access insurers and schemes not available directly, and help with claims. The right choice depends on how complex your needs are.
