## What the letters ACCA actually mean

In the UK, the word accountant on its own isn't a protected title, so anyone can use it. ACCA members are different: they're chartered certified accountants who have passed a demanding qualification, follow ACCA's ethics code and complete continuing professional development. You can check whether someone is a member through the ACCA register.

If a firm offers public practice services, it also needs an ACCA practising certificate, and every accountancy practice must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes by a professional body or HMRC. It's reasonable to ask which body supervises the firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports.

## FAQs: an ACCA chartered certified accountant

### How can I check that an accountant is really ACCA-qualified?

You can search the ACCA register online or ask the accountant for their membership number. A genuine member will be happy to point you to the register. If a firm offers public practice services, you can also ask to see their ACCA practising certificate.

### Does an ACCA accountant have to be local to me?

Not necessarily, but many people prefer someone nearby for face-to-face meetings. If you search for an ACCA accountant near me, you're probably looking for that convenience. It's fine to ask how they usually work with clients and whether they visit your area.

### What's the difference between an ACCA accountant and a chartered accountant?

Both are protected titles with demanding qualifications and professional ethics codes. ACCA members are chartered certified accountants, while chartered accountants may belong to ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland. The right choice depends on your needs, so ask about their experience with clients like you.

### Will an ACCA accountant handle my tax return or payroll?

Many ACCA practices offer a wide range of services, from tax returns to payroll and bookkeeping. ACCA members can work with sole traders through to large companies. It's best to ask directly whether they cover the specific work you need.

### Do I need a chartered accountant?

Not always. For simple Self Assessment a qualified, supervised accountant may be enough. For audits, complex group structures or specialist tax work, a chartered or certified firm is often the safer choice. We can match you to either.
