## Qualifications and registration to look for

"Tax adviser" is not a protected title in the UK. The Chartered Institute of Taxation (CTA qualification) and the Association of Taxation Technicians (ATT) are the main specialist bodies, and many accountants and solicitors also advise on tax. Members of these bodies follow Professional Conduct in Relation to Taxation, a shared code covering honesty, confidentiality and dealing with HMRC.

Tax advisers who provide services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes. HMRC has also been tightening standards for paid tax agents, so it is reasonable to ask about qualifications, supervision and professional indemnity insurance before you go ahead.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## Best EIS Adviser Near Me: FAQs

### What does an EIS adviser do?

An EIS adviser helps companies apply for advance assurance from HMRC and comply with Enterprise Investment Scheme rules, and helps investors understand the reliefs and risks. They can guide you through the strict qualifying conditions and the process.

### How do I judge who is the best EIS adviser for me?

There is no official ranking, so you should check qualifications, experience with EIS specifically, and whether they are supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes. Ask about their approach, fees and whether they carry professional indemnity insurance.

### Is 'tax adviser' a protected title?

No, 'tax adviser' is not a protected title in the UK, so it is important to check credentials. The Chartered Institute of Taxation and the Association of Taxation Technicians are the main specialist bodies, and their members follow a shared code of conduct.

### What are the risks of EIS investments?

Investors in EIS face higher risks because early-stage companies can fail, and the tax reliefs do not remove that risk. It is important to understand the qualifying conditions and the possibility of losing your investment.

### Does my EIS adviser need to be local to me?

Not necessarily. Much EIS work is about documents, HMRC correspondence and conversations that can happen by phone or video. If you would rather meet in person, say so when you enquire and we will take your location into account.

### Can an EIS adviser promise my company will qualify?

No. Qualifying depends on the strict EIS rules and on HMRC's view, and an adviser can help you prepare and apply for advance assurance but cannot promise the outcome. Be wary of anyone who says otherwise.

### What should I have ready for a first conversation about EIS?

For a company, have your business plan and a clear description of your trade to hand. For an investor, think about the company you are considering, the amount involved and your own tax position, so the adviser can explain the reliefs and risks in context.

### Should I reply to HMRC before speaking to an adviser?

Note the deadline on the letter, but avoid sending a detailed reply before you have advice if the matter is complex. An adviser can often ask HMRC for more time where it is reasonable.
