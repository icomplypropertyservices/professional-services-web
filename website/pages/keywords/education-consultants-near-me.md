## Understanding regulation and local knowledge

Education consultancy is not regulated by a single body, so it is worth looking for a consultant with relevant teaching, admissions or special educational needs experience. Membership of organisations such as the Society of Education Consultants is one sign to look for, along with references and a clear written agreement setting out what they will do.

If your situation involves a school admission appeal, independent appeal panels in England follow the School Admission Appeals Code. A consultant can help you prepare, but the decision rests with the panel, so treat any firm promise of a particular outcome with caution.
