## Checking private dentists are qualified and regulated

Dentists, dental hygienists, dental therapists and dental nurses must be registered with the General Dental Council (GDC), and you can check the GDC register online. Specialist titles such as orthodontist or endodontist can only be used by people on a GDC specialist list. Private clinics providing regulated healthcare in England must register with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which inspects and publishes ratings. In Wales the regulator is Healthcare Inspectorate Wales, in Scotland Healthcare Improvement Scotland, and in Northern Ireland the RQIA.

If something goes wrong with private dental treatment, start with the practice's complaints process. The Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints that are not resolved.

## FAQs: private dentures

### Can a clinical dental technician make my dentures instead of a dentist?

Yes, clinical dental technicians can make and fit dentures directly to the public. They must be registered with the General Dental Council, so you can check the register online. Some people see a clinical dental technician for dentures, while others see a private dentist.

### How do I check if a private dentist or clinical dental technician is properly registered?

Dentists and clinical dental technicians must be registered with the General Dental Council (GDC). You can search the GDC register online to confirm their registration. If the practice provides regulated healthcare in England, it should also be registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC).

### What should I do if I have a problem with private dentures?

Start by speaking to the practice where you had the treatment, as they should have a complaints process. If the issue is not resolved, the Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints. They offer advice and can look into your complaint independently.

### Will private dentures feel different from NHS dentures?

Private dentures often use premium materials and involve more appointments to perfect fit and appearance. That can mean a more comfortable fit and a more natural look, but it varies from person to person. It's worth discussing the differences with the professional you see.

### Can I see a private dentist and stay with my NHS dentist?

Yes, you can use both. Some patients see a private dentist for specific treatments and keep NHS care for routine check-ups, if their NHS practice agrees.
