## Understanding offset mortgages

An offset mortgage lets you link your savings to your mortgage, so you pay interest only on the difference between your mortgage balance and your savings. This can reduce the interest you pay, but the rate may be slightly higher than on a standard mortgage. The main benefit is flexibility, as you can usually access your savings when needed.

Offset mortgages tend to suit people with substantial savings who want to reduce their interest without giving up access to their money. It's important to compare the overall cost, including any fees, and to check whether the offset facility is right for your circumstances. A mortgage broker can help you weigh up the options.

## FAQs: an offset mortgage

### Is an offset mortgage suitable for everyone?

No, offset mortgages usually suit people with substantial savings who want flexibility. If you have limited savings, a standard mortgage might be more cost-effective.

### Can I get an offset mortgage if I'm remortgaging?

Yes, offset mortgages are available for remortgaging as well as for new purchases. A broker can help you compare options.

### How do I find the best offset mortgage near me?

The best option depends on your circumstances, so it's wise to speak to a broker who can compare deals and explain the features. They can help you judge which offset mortgage fits your needs.

### What should I check before choosing an offset mortgage?

Check the interest rate, any fees, and how the offset facility works, including whether you can access your savings. Also confirm that the broker is authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority.

### How do I compare offset mortgage options if I have savings?

Look at the offset interest rate alongside the standard rate, any fees, and whether you can access your savings freely. A broker can show you how the interest would be calculated on your own savings and mortgage balance, so you can see the real difference rather than just the headline rate.

### Does 'near me' mean I have to meet a broker in person?

Not necessarily. Many brokers offer phone or online appointments as well as face-to-face meetings, so you can choose what suits you. It's worth asking how they usually work and whether there is any charge for the first conversation.

### Can a broker near me promise the best offset mortgage?

No broker can promise the best deal, because that depends on your circumstances and the lenders available to them. What a good broker can do is explain whether they search the whole market or a limited panel, and set out the costs clearly so you can decide.

### Is a broker better than going to my bank?

A broker can compare many lenders and may find options your bank does not offer. Your bank can only offer its own products. Either can suit you; a broker gives you a wider view.
