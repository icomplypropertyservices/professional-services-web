Bookkeeping is usually priced on volume and frequency: how many transactions, how many bank accounts and how often you want the books brought up to date. Catch-up work is often quoted separately. The bookkeeper you are matched with will give you a POA quote based on your records.

## What to understand about bookkeeping for small businesses

Bookkeeping is not a regulated title in the UK, but anyone providing bookkeeping services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by HMRC or by a professional body. Membership of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) or the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT) shows a bookkeeper has trained to a recognised standard and agreed to a code of conduct.

A good bookkeeper will be clear about where their work stops and your accountant's starts, particularly on year-end accounts and tax advice. Ask whether they hold professional indemnity insurance and which body supervises them, so you know you're dealing with someone accountable.

## FAQs: a small business bookkeeper

### Do I need a bookkeeper if I already have an accountant?

Many small businesses use a bookkeeper for the day-to-day records and an accountant for year-end accounts and tax advice. A bookkeeper can keep things tidy so your accountant's time is spent on the higher-level work. It's worth asking each to be clear about where their work stops and the other's starts.

### What does a small business bookkeeper near me actually do?

Typically they record sales and purchase invoices, reconcile bank statements, keep receipts in order and may prepare VAT returns. They can also chase unpaid invoices and give you a short monthly report. The exact scope depends on what you agree with them.

### How do I judge if a bookkeeper is right for my small business?

Ask about their training and which body supervises them, such as the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) or the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT). Check they hold professional indemnity insurance and are clear about where their work stops and an accountant's begins. A good bookkeeper will also explain things in plain language and answer your questions openly.

### Can a bookkeeper help me get on top of VAT?

Yes, many small business bookkeepers handle VAT records and prepare VAT returns as part of their service. They should be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes and will work within the scope you agree. If you're unsure about VAT rules, ask them to explain what they can and can't do.

### Can a bookkeeper submit my VAT return?

Many can, through Making Tax Digital compatible software, once you authorise them. Check this is included in the scope before you start.
