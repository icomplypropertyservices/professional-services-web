## Qualifications and registration to look for

Insurance brokers and intermediaries in the UK must be authorised by the FCA, or be appointed representatives of an authorised firm. Check them on the FCA register. Many brokers belong to the British Insurance Brokers' Association (BIBA), and some hold Chartered status from the Chartered Insurance Institute.

Brokers must understand your demands and needs, explain key policy features and exclusions, and tell you how they are paid. Complaints that are not resolved can go to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

A broker works on your behalf to find suitable products from providers. Ask how many providers they compare and how they are paid.

## FAQs: a broker for cyber insurance

### Do I need cyber insurance if I already have business insurance?

Cyber cover is often separate from a standard business policy, and the two may not overlap in the way you expect. A broker can explain what your existing policy does and does not include so you can judge whether additional cover is worthwhile.

### Will the insurer expect me to have security measures in place?

Insurers generally expect basic security controls before they will offer cyber cover. A broker can tell you what is typically looked for and how to describe what you already do.

### What is social engineering cover and do I need it?

Social engineering cover relates to losses where someone is tricked into transferring money or giving away information. It is worth asking whether it is included, as it is often an area people overlook until they need it.

### How do I check that a broker is properly authorised?

You can check them on the FCA register, and many brokers also belong to BIBA or hold Chartered status from the Chartered Insurance Institute.

### Is it better to use a broker near me than one further away?

For cyber cover, much can be handled by phone or email, so distance matters less than whether the broker understands your type of business. A local broker may suit you if you prefer meeting in person to talk through sensitive details about your systems and data. Either way, check they are authorised by the FCA before you proceed.

### What should I bring to a first meeting with a cyber insurance broker?

Bring details of what your business does, the data you hold, and any security measures already in place, such as backups or staff training. If you have an existing policy, bring it so the broker can see what is already covered. It also helps to know what a client or contract has asked you for, if that is what prompted your search.

### Is a broker more expensive than buying direct?

Not necessarily. Brokers can access insurers and schemes not available directly, and help with claims. The right choice depends on how complex your needs are.
