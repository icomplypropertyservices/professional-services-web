## FAQs about cardiologist near me

### Do I need a GP referral to see a cardiologist privately?

Not always, but some consultants and most insurers prefer one. If you have private medical insurance, check with your insurer first, as it may require a GP referral and may limit you to certain consultants or hospitals.

### What tests might a cardiologist arrange?

Common tests include an ECG, an echocardiogram and a heart monitor worn over a period. The consultant will discuss which tests are appropriate for your symptoms at your appointment.

### Should I book if I have chest pain right now?

No — chest pain that is happening now needs 999, not an appointment. A private consultation is for symptoms that have settled or concerns you can discuss calmly.

### How can I check that a cardiologist is properly registered?

You can also check whether the clinic is registered with the relevant regulator for its nation.

### Do I need a GP referral to see a private consultant?

Self-paying patients can sometimes book directly, but many consultants prefer a referral, and insurers usually require one. A private GP can provide one quickly.
