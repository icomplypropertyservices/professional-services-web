## FAQs about ADHD assessment near me

### Do I need a referral from my GP to get an ADHD assessment?

Not always. Some private clinicians accept self-referrals, while others may ask for a letter from your GP. It is worth asking the professional when you enquire.

### What is shared care and why does it matter?

Shared care is an arrangement where your GP prescribes medication recommended by a specialist. Policies differ between practices, so ask in advance whether your GP will agree to this if you receive a private diagnosis.

### Can I use Right to Choose for an ADHD assessment?

Right to Choose may apply in England, allowing you to ask your GP to refer you to a different provider for NHS-funded care. Availability can vary, so it is best to discuss this with your GP.

### How do I know the clinician is qualified to assess ADHD?

ADHD assessments should be carried out by suitably qualified clinicians, such as a psychiatrist registered with the GMC, a practitioner psychologist registered with the HCPC, or a multidisciplinary team following NICE guidance. You can ask about their qualifications and registration.

### Will a private diagnosis be recognised?

A thorough assessment by a properly qualified clinician should be. Shared care for medication depends on your GP's agreement, so ask them before booking.
