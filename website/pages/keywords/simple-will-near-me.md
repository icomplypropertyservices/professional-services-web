## How to check you are in safe hands

Writing a will is not a reserved legal activity in England and Wales, so some will writers are not regulated. Solicitors (SRA) and chartered legal executives (CILEx) are regulated, insured and covered by the Legal Ombudsman. Many specialists are also members of STEP (the Society of Trust and Estate Practitioners) or Solicitors for the Elderly. Applying for a grant of probate on someone else's behalf for a fee is a reserved activity.

When choosing a provider, ask whether they are regulated, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance and where your original will is stored. Scotland has its own rules on succession and confirmation, so make sure your adviser practises in the right jurisdiction.

## FAQs about simple will near me

### Do I need a solicitor to make a simple will?

Not necessarily, because writing a will is not a reserved legal activity in England and Wales. However, solicitors regulated by the SRA and chartered legal executives regulated by CILEx are insured and covered by the Legal Ombudsman, which gives you extra protection.

### What makes a will simple?

A simple will usually suits a straightforward estate where you leave assets to a partner, children or chosen people without complex trusts. If your affairs are more complicated, the specialist can tell you what else might be needed.

### Why should I name substitute beneficiaries?

If someone you have chosen to benefit dies before you, a substitute beneficiary can step in so that your wishes are still followed. This is a common part of a simple will.

### Where should I keep my original will?

It is important to keep the original safe, and many people ask their solicitor or will writer to store it. Always check where it will be held and how you or your executors can get it when needed.

### Is a simple will cheaper than a complex one?

A simple will usually involves less work than one with trusts or business assets, so it is often quoted differently. Because quotes are price on application, ask the specialist to explain what their quote covers before you decide.

### Can I make a simple will if I have never made one before?

Yes, and it is a common starting point. Tell the specialist whether you have an existing will or none at all, and they can explain what information they need from you to prepare a simple will.

### Does a simple will still need to be signed in a particular way?

Yes, a will must be signed and witnessed correctly to be valid. Ask the specialist to explain the signing and witnessing requirements so your will is not put at risk.

### Do I need a solicitor for probate?

Not always. Executors can apply for probate themselves for simpler estates. Many choose professional help where there is property, inheritance tax, overseas assets or family tension.
