## What to understand about QuickBooks bookkeepers near you

QuickBooks ProAdvisor status shows that a bookkeeper has completed training with Intuit, but it isn't a sign of quality on its own. More important is whether they hold professional indemnity insurance and which body supervises them for anti-money-laundering purposes, either HMRC or a professional body. Membership of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) or the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT) shows they have trained to a recognised standard and agreed to a code of conduct.

Bookkeeping is not a regulated title in the UK, so anyone can call themselves a bookkeeper. That's why it's worth asking directly about supervision and insurance before you share your QuickBooks data. A good bookkeeper will also be clear about where their work stops and an accountant's starts, especially for year-end accounts and tax advice.

## FAQs: a QuickBooks bookkeeper

### Do I need a bookkeeper who is a QuickBooks ProAdvisor?

ProAdvisor status shows the bookkeeper has completed training with Intuit, but it isn't essential. What matters more is that they understand your version of QuickBooks and can work with your existing file.

### Can a bookkeeper help me move from QuickBooks Desktop to QuickBooks Online?

Yes, many bookkeepers handle migrations between desktop and online versions. Ask about their experience with migrations and how they will check that your data transfers correctly.

### Will a bookkeeper near me also handle VAT returns in QuickBooks?

Many bookkeepers prepare and submit VAT returns through QuickBooks, but confirm this when you speak to them. They should also explain how they keep records and meet HMRC's Making Tax Digital requirements.

### How do I know a bookkeeper is properly supervised?

Ask which body supervises them and whether they hold professional indemnity insurance.

### Can a bookkeeper submit my VAT return?

Many can, through Making Tax Digital compatible software, once you authorise them. Check this is included in the scope before you start.
