## Checking architects are qualified and regulated

"Architect" is a protected title in the UK. Only people on the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance. Many practices are also RIBA Chartered Practices. Architectural designers and technologists can do excellent work too, but they are not registered architects, so it is worth knowing which you are hiring.

Before you appoint anyone, check the ARB register and ask for a written appointment that sets out services, fees and responsibilities. Building regulations approval, planning permission and party wall matters are separate processes that your architect can help you navigate.

## FAQs: a chartered architect

### What is the difference between a chartered architect and an architectural designer?

A chartered architect is registered with the Architects Registration Board (ARB) and may also be a member of the Royal Institute of British Architects (RIBA). Architectural designers are not registered architects, although they can offer design services. It's important to check your professional's credentials before appointing them.

### Do I need a chartered architect for a small home extension?

Not always, but a chartered architect can help with design, planning permission and building regulations, especially for complex projects. For simple extensions, you might use an architectural technologist, but a chartered architect offers additional assurance of professional standards and insurance.

### How do I check if an architect is properly qualified?

You can check the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register online to confirm someone is a registered architect. If they claim to be chartered, you can also ask for their RIBA Chartered Practice listing.

### What should be included in a written appointment with an architect?

A written appointment should set out the services the architect will provide, their fees, and the responsibilities of both parties. It's also wise to confirm that they hold professional indemnity insurance and are registered with the ARB.

### Do I need an architect for an extension?

Not legally, but a registered architect brings design skill, planning experience and accountability. For simple work, an architectural technologist or designer may be enough. We can match you to the right level of help.
