## Wisdom Tooth Removal Near Me: FAQs

### Do I always need my wisdom tooth removed?

No, not always. Removal is considered when the tooth causes infection, decay, pain or damage to neighbouring teeth, and a X-ray helps guide that decision.

### Will the dentist take a X-ray first?

Yes, a X-ray is usually taken to see the position of the tooth and to plan the treatment. It helps the dentist decide whether removal is straightforward or needs an oral surgeon.

### How long is recovery after wisdom tooth removal?

Recovery varies from person to person, but it is sensible to plan a few days of rest and soft food. Your dentist will give you specific aftercare advice.

### What if I have a complaint about private treatment?

Start with the practice's complaints process. If it is not resolved, the Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints.

### Do I need a check-up before treatment?

Almost always. A dentist needs to examine you, and often take X-rays, before recommending treatment.
