## Mole Check Near Me: FAQs

### What happens during a mole check?

The consultant will examine your skin, often using a dermatoscope to look closely at moles. They will assess any mole that concerns you and may recommend removal or monitoring. Suspicious moles need urgent attention.

### Should I see a GP first?

You can see a private consultant directly, but if you have private medical insurance, your insurer may require a GP referral and may limit you to certain consultants or hospitals. Check with your insurer before you book.

### How do I check if a consultant is qualified?

You can also check whether the clinic is registered with the relevant regulator, such as the CQC in England.

### What changes in a mole should prompt a check?

Look for changes in shape, colour or size. A mole that is new, itching, bleeding or not healing should be seen urgently. If you are unsure, it is safer to have it checked.

### Do I need a GP referral to see a private consultant?

Self-paying patients can sometimes book directly, but many consultants prefer a referral, and insurers usually require one. A private GP can provide one quickly.
