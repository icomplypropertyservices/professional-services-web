## How to check you are in safe hands

In the UK, the word "accountant" on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can use it. Titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises a firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled.

## Affordable Accountant Near Me: FAQs

### How can I tell if an accountant is affordable?

Affordable means the fee fits your budget and covers what you need. Ask for a clear quote that lists which returns and filings are included, and check whether advice and support are part of the fee. Comparing a few quotes will help you judge value, not just price.

### Do I need a local accountant, or can I work with one remotely?

Many accountants work remotely using phone, email and online tools, so you can often choose someone outside your area. If you prefer face-to-face meetings, look for a local practice. Either way, make sure you're comfortable with how they communicate.

### What should I have ready before I speak to an accountant?

It helps to have your records organised, such as income and expenses, bank statements, and any previous tax returns. Also think about what you want to achieve, like filing a return or planning for tax. The more you can tell them upfront, the more accurate their quote will be.

### Is the word accountant protected in the UK?

No, the word "accountant" on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can call themselves an accountant. However, titles like Chartered Accountant and Chartered Certified Accountant are protected, and members are bound by their professional body's rules. It's wise to ask about qualifications and supervision.

### Will a local accountant be more expensive than one further away?

Not necessarily. Fees depend on the practice and the work involved, not just location. Some local practices are very competitive, and some remote ones charge more for the same service. It's always worth getting a few quotes and comparing what's included.

### How can I compare quotes from different accountants?

Ask each accountant to list exactly which services are covered, such as accounts preparation, tax returns, and advice, and whether there are extra charges for calls or emails. Then you can see which quote offers the best value for what you actually need. Don't be afraid to ask for clarification on anything that isn't clear.
