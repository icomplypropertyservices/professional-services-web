## What to understand about private fertility clinics

Clinics that offer treatments involving eggs, sperm or embryos, such as IVF, donor treatment and egg or embryo freezing, must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback, so you can compare clinics on a like-for-like basis.

Some simple tests, such as blood hormone checks, may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing, but any treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre. When comparing clinics, ask about multi-cycle packages carefully and check what medication costs are included, as these can vary significantly.

## Private Fertility Clinic Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a referral from my GP to attend a private fertility clinic?

Most private fertility clinics accept self-referrals, so you can usually book an appointment directly without seeing your GP first. However, it is still a good idea to keep your GP informed, as they can share relevant medical history and test results.

### How can I check if a clinic is properly licensed?

You can check the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool, which lists all licensed clinics along with their inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback. Any treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should only be carried out at a licensed centre.

### What should I consider when comparing the cost of private fertility treatment?

Ask for a detailed breakdown of what is included in the quoted price, and check whether medication, scans, blood tests and follow-up appointments are extra. Multi-cycle packages can offer savings but may have conditions, so read the terms carefully.

### Can I get NHS funding for fertility treatment if I go private?

NHS funding for fertility treatment depends on where you live and your individual circumstances, and having private treatment does not usually affect your eligibility. It is best to check with your GP or local integrated care board for the criteria in your area.

### Am I eligible for NHS fertility treatment?

Eligibility depends on local commissioning rules, including age, how long you have been trying and previous children. Ask your GP; a private clinic can also explain options.
