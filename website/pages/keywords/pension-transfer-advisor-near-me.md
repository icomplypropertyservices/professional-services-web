## Why pension transfers need specialist advice

Pension transfers are regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), so you can check that any adviser or firm is authorised on the FCA register. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme may protect you if an authorised firm fails.

If you are transferring out of a defined benefit (final salary) scheme above a set value, the advice must come from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions. Safeguarded benefits deserve particular care, and free, impartial guidance is available from Pension Wise for people over a certain age with defined contribution pensions.

## Qualifications and registration to look for

Financial advice in the UK is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Check that any adviser or firm is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm, on the FCA register. If something goes wrong you can complain to the firm, then to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme may protect you if an authorised firm fails.

Some pension decisions carry special rules. Advice on transferring out of a defined benefit (final salary) scheme above a set value must come from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions, and safeguarded benefits deserve particular care. Free, impartial guidance is available from Pension Wise for people over 50 with defined contribution pensions.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## FAQs about pension transfer advisor near me

### Do I need a specialist adviser to transfer a defined benefit pension?

Yes, if the transfer value is above a set level, the advice must come from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions. This is a requirement set by the FCA, and it is there to make sure you get the right kind of expert input.

### How can I check that a pension transfer adviser is properly authorised?

You can check the FCA register to see whether an adviser or firm is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm. This is a quick and free way to confirm that the advice you receive is regulated.

### What is Pension Wise and could it help me?

Pension Wise is a free and impartial guidance service for people over a certain age with defined contribution pensions. It can help you understand your options before you pay for advice, though it does not give a personal recommendation.

### How can I protect myself from pension transfer scams?

Be wary of any unexpected contact about your pension, especially if someone pressures you to transfer quickly. Always check that the adviser or firm is on the FCA register, and remember that scams often target transfers.

### Can I get pension transfer advice without meeting anyone in person?

Many advisers work by phone or video as well as face to face, so searching near me does not limit you to firms on your doorstep. What matters most is that the adviser is authorised by the FCA and holds the permissions your scheme requires.

### Does the adviser have to be local to my pension scheme?

No. Your scheme can be based anywhere in the UK, and the adviser's job is to assess whether transferring suits you, not where the scheme sits. Choosing someone who covers your area is about convenience and being able to talk things through easily.

### What if I have more than one pension to consider?

Tell iComply about each pension when you enquire, including whether any are defined benefit or have safeguarded benefits. An adviser can then look at them together and explain whether a single transfer or a wider review makes sense for you.

### What is Pension Wise?

A free, impartial government-backed service offering guidance to people over 50 about their options for defined contribution pensions. It is guidance, not personal advice.
