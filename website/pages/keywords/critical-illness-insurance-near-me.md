## How critical illness cover differs from other protection you may be offered

People searching for critical illness insurance near me often end up being shown several types of protection at once, so it helps to know where critical illness sits. Critical illness cover pays a lump sum if you are diagnosed with a specified serious illness, while income protection generally replaces part of your earnings if you cannot work because of illness or injury. Life insurance pays out on death or, in some policies, on diagnosis of a terminal illness, so the three do different jobs and are not interchangeable.

A common misunderstanding is that critical illness cover works like private medical insurance. It does not pay for treatment or consultations; it pays a lump sum when a diagnosis meets the policy's definition, and you decide how to use the money. That is why the definitions and exclusions matter so much, and why an insurance broker will talk through your demands and needs rather than simply naming a policy.

## FAQs: critical illness cover

### What does critical illness cover actually pay for?

It pays a lump sum if you are diagnosed with a specified serious illness, and you can generally use the money however you need. Which illnesses are specified, and how they are defined, varies by insurer, so the policy wording is the place to check.

### Why do definitions vary between insurers?

Insurers set their own wording for what counts as a specified serious illness and the severity required. That means a diagnosis covered by one policy may not meet another policy's definition, so it is worth comparing the detail rather than just the policy name.

### Should I mention health conditions when I enquire?

Yes, disclose your health fully. Brokers must understand your demands and needs, and incomplete information can affect a policy later, so it is better to be open from the start.

### How do I check that a broker is properly authorised?

Many brokers also belong to BIBA, and some hold Chartered status from the Chartered Insurance Institute.

### Is critical illness cover the same as income protection?

No. Critical illness cover pays a lump sum on diagnosis of a specified serious illness, whereas income protection generally replaces part of your income if you cannot work. They can be arranged alongside each other, and a broker can explain how they would fit your situation.

### Will critical illness cover pay for my treatment?

It does not pay for treatment directly. It pays a lump sum if you are diagnosed with a specified serious illness, and you choose how to use the money, whether that is covering costs while you are away from work or adapting your home.

### What should I have ready for a first conversation with a local broker?

Be ready to explain who the cover is for, your health and family history, and what you want the lump sum to protect. Disclose your health fully, because incomplete information can affect a policy later, and ask how the broker is paid.

### What is non-disclosure?

Failing to tell an insurer something relevant. It can reduce or void a claim, so answer questions fully and ask your broker if unsure.
