## Understanding how a contents insurance broker works

Insurance brokers and intermediaries in the UK must be authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), or be appointed representatives of an authorised firm. You can check any broker on the FCA register. Many brokers also belong to the British Insurance Brokers' Association (BIBA), and some hold Chartered status from the Chartered Insurance Institute.

A broker must understand your demands and needs, explain the key features and exclusions of any policy, and tell you how they are paid. If you have a complaint that isn't resolved, you can take it to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

## FAQs about contents insurance broker near me

### Do I need contents insurance if I rent my home?

Your landlord's building insurance won't cover your personal belongings, so it's usually up to you to insure your contents. A broker can help you find a policy that fits your needs as a tenant.

### How do I value my contents for insurance?

Go through your home room by room and add up what it would cost to replace everything at today's prices. A broker can guide you on typical sums and remind you to include items you might overlook.

### Can a broker help if I have a high-value item like an engagement ring?

Yes, brokers can arrange cover for high-value items, either within your contents policy or as a separate item. They can explain single-item limits and how to make sure the ring is fully protected.

### What should I do if my claim is refused?

Ask the broker to explain the reasons in writing, as the policy wording may have exclusions you weren't aware of. If you're still unhappy, you can complain to the firm and, if unresolved, take it to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

### Does a broker need to be local to help with my contents claim?

Not strictly, but a broker near you may find it easier to visit your home to assess damage or talk through paperwork in person. What matters most is that they understand your policy and will deal with the insurer on your behalf.

### What should I have ready before my first appointment with a local broker?

It helps to have a room-by-room list of your belongings with rough replacement values, details of any high-value items, and any past claims or refused insurance. This lets the broker match you to policies that suit your situation rather than guessing.

### Will a local broker always be cheaper than going direct?

Not necessarily, and no one can promise that. A broker's value lies in comparing policies, explaining exclusions and supporting you at claim time, so weigh the cover and service alongside the premium when you decide.

### What is non-disclosure?

Failing to tell an insurer something relevant. It can reduce or void a claim, so answer questions fully and ask your broker if unsure.
