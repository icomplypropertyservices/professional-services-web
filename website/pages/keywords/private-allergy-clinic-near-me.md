## Questions to ask the private professional

Once you are introduced, these questions help you decide whether the private GP is right for you:

- Who will I actually see, and are they a specialist allergist or a private GP with an interest in allergy?
- Which tests are you proposing, and are they validated tests rather than unvalidated ones?
- Will you share consultation notes with my NHS GP, with my consent, so my records stay complete?
- Is immunotherapy something you'd consider for my case, and what would that involve?
- What does the quote cover, and are there likely to be separate charges for tests or follow-up?
- How would you handle a food challenge, especially if the patient is a child?

## FAQs: a private allergy clinic

### Is a private allergy clinic near me the same as seeing an NHS allergist?

No. A private clinic is paid for directly and usually offers faster access, while NHS allergy services are accessed through your GP referral. A private GP does not replace your NHS GP, so keep your NHS practice informed.

### How do I check a clinic is properly regulated?

You can search the General Medical Council's online register by the doctor's name to confirm they are registered and hold a licence to practise. In England, private clinics providing regulated healthcare must register with the Care Quality Commission, which publishes inspection ratings; the equivalent bodies are Healthcare Inspectorate Wales, Healthcare Improvement Scotland and the RQIA in Northern Ireland.

### What should I do if I'm worried about a severe reaction right now?

For a severe reaction such as difficulty breathing or swelling of the face or throat, call 999. For urgent advice that isn't an emergency, NHS 111 can help you decide what to do next.

### Can a private clinic test my child for food allergies?

Many private allergy clinics assess children as well as adults, including for food challenges, but services vary. Tell us your child's age and the suspected trigger so we can introduce you to a suitable professional who can quote you directly.

### Can a private GP prescribe medication?

Yes, a GMC-registered doctor can issue private prescriptions. You pay the clinic's prescription fee and the pharmacy's price for the medicine.
