## Licensing and what it means for your search

Clinics offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback, so it is a sensible place to check a clinic you are considering near you.

Some simple tests, such as blood hormone checks, may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing. But any treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre, so ask about licensing early on when you approach a clinic.

## FAQs: a male fertility clinic

### Do I need a referral from my GP to attend a male fertility clinic?

It varies between clinics, so it is worth asking when you first get in touch. Some accept self-referral for an initial consultation and tests, while others prefer a GP referral. Your GP can also arrange some initial tests, which may save time.

### What happens at a first appointment about male fertility?

You would usually discuss your history, any previous semen analysis results and possible causes of sperm problems. The clinic may arrange further tests, such as a repeat semen analysis or blood hormone checks, before talking through options. It helps to bring any results you already have.

### Can lifestyle changes really affect sperm?

Lifestyle factors do affect sperm, and clinics commonly discuss things like smoking, alcohol, weight and heat exposure. Changes take time to show in semen analysis results, so a clinic will usually advise on this alongside any tests. Ask what is most relevant in your case.

### How do I check whether a clinic near me is properly licensed?

The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool lists licensed clinics and shows inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback. Treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre. Simple tests may be offered elsewhere, so ask about licensing directly if you are unsure.

### How do I compare clinics?

Use the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool, which gives ratings, outcomes and patient feedback for licensed clinics.
