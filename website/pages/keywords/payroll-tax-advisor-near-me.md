## Qualifications and supervision are worth checking

"Tax adviser" is not a protected title in the UK, so anyone can use it. The Chartered Institute of Taxation, which sets the CTA qualification, and the Association of Taxation Technicians are the main specialist bodies, and many accountants and solicitors also advise on tax. Members of these bodies follow Professional Conduct in Relation to Taxation, a shared code covering honesty, confidentiality and dealing with HMRC.

Tax advisers who charge a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, and HMRC has been tightening standards for paid tax agents. It is therefore reasonable to ask about qualifications, supervision and professional indemnity insurance before you go ahead. ## Payroll Tax Advisor Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a payroll tax adviser if I only employ one person?

Even with a single employee, PAYE and National Insurance still apply, and benefits in kind may need reporting to HMRC. A payroll tax adviser can check that the arrangements are set up correctly from the start.

### Can a payroll tax adviser help with employment status?

Yes, employment status is one of the areas payroll tax advisers handle, and mistakes carry risk. They can look at how a worker is engaged and paid and explain the position.

### Is a payroll tax adviser the same as an accountant?

Not necessarily, though many accountants advise on tax. "Tax adviser" is not a protected title, so it is worth asking about qualifications and membership of a specialist body such as the Chartered Institute of Taxation or the Association of Taxation Technicians.

### What is a salary sacrifice arrangement?

It is an arrangement where an employee gives up part of their salary in return for a benefit, and it needs careful design. A payroll tax adviser can explain how it would affect pay, tax and National Insurance in your circumstances.

### Should I look for a payroll tax adviser or a payroll bureau?

A payroll bureau usually runs the payroll itself, calculating pay and producing payslips, while a payroll tax adviser concentrates on the tax treatment, such as benefits in kind, employment status and termination payments. Some firms do both, so it is worth asking which parts they handle and which they leave to you.

### Can a local adviser deal with HMRC on my behalf?

Advisers often act as your agent in dealings with HMRC, which usually means you authorise them to act for you. Ask how they would keep you informed of any correspondence and what they need from you before they contact HMRC.

### What should I bring to a first meeting about payroll tax?

Bring anything that shows how people are paid and engaged: recent payslips, details of any benefits you provide, contracts for self-employed workers and any letters from HMRC. That gives the adviser enough to understand your position and explain what they would do next.

### Is tax planning legal?

Using reliefs and allowances as Parliament intended is normal and legal. Aggressive avoidance schemes are a different matter and can carry serious risks. A qualified adviser will explain the difference clearly.
