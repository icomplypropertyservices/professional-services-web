## Who should be carrying out your FUE transplant

Cosmetic surgery, including FUE hair transplants, should be carried out by a surgeon on the GMC's specialist register, usually in plastic surgery or a related specialty, in a hospital or clinic registered with the CQC in England (or the equivalent regulator elsewhere in the UK). Your consultation should be with the surgeon who will actually operate, not just a salesperson or technician. The GMC also requires doctors offering cosmetic interventions to give you time to reflect before you agree to surgery, and not to use high-pressure sales tactics.
