## What 'top' means when you're choosing a private GP

There's no official ranking of private GPs, so 'top' really depends on what matters to you: a doctor who listens, convenient appointment times, clear pricing, or a clinic that feels right for you. Every doctor practising in the UK must be registered with the General Medical Council (GMC) and hold a licence to practise. You can check a GP's registration and whether they appear on the GMC's GP register by searching the GMC's online register. In England, private clinics providing regulated healthcare must also be registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which inspects and publishes ratings. In Wales, the regulator is Healthcare Inspectorate Wales; in Scotland, Healthcare Improvement Scotland; and in Northern Ireland, the RQIA.

A private GP doesn't replace your NHS GP. Good private clinics will ask for your consent to share consultation notes with your NHS practice, so your records stay complete and any medication is prescribed safely. When you contact a clinic, ask how they handle this sharing and whether they can send details to your NHS GP after your appointment.

## FAQs about top private GP near me

### How do I check if a private GP is properly qualified?

You can search the GMC's online register by the doctor's name to confirm their registration and whether they are on the GP register. In England, you can also check whether the clinic is registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC) and see its inspection rating.

### Will a private GP share information with my NHS doctor?

Good private clinics will ask for your consent to share consultation notes with your NHS GP practice, so your records stay complete and any medication is prescribed safely. It's worth asking the clinic how they handle this before you book. A private GP does not replace your NHS GP, so keeping both informed is important.

### Can I see a private GP for a certificate or report?

Yes, many private GPs can provide medical certificates or reports after a consultation, for purposes such as work, travel or insurance. Ask the clinic in advance what they can provide and what the fee will be. The GP will need to assess you first, so it's not usually something that can be done without an appointment.

### What should I do in an emergency?

If you have a serious or life-threatening emergency, call 999 or go to your nearest accident and emergency department. For urgent medical advice that isn't an emergency, you can call NHS 111. Private GPs are not an emergency service, so always use the appropriate NHS route for urgent care.

### Can I see a private GP near my workplace rather than my home?

Yes, plenty of people choose a clinic close to work so an appointment fits around the working day. ### Is a private GP near me the same as an NHS GP?

A private GP offers the same range of everyday medical care as an NHS GP, usually with longer appointments and quicker availability, but a private GP does not replace your NHS GP.

### What if I need urgent help before I can see a private GP?

A private GP is not an emergency service. If you have a serious or life-threatening emergency, call 999 or go to your nearest accident and emergency department, and for urgent advice that isn't an emergency you can call NHS 111.

### Will a private GP share my notes with my NHS GP?
