## Understanding the CIMA title and how practices are supervised

In the UK the word accountant on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can use it. Titles such as Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA), Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland) and Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports, so if audit is in scope, ask about that separately.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises a firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled. When you search for a CIMA accountant near me, proximity matters for meetings, but the fit of their management reporting experience matters more.

## CIMA Accountant Near Me: FAQs

### What is the difference between a CIMA accountant and a chartered accountant?

Chartered Accountants (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland) and Chartered Certified Accountants (ACCA) follow different qualifying routes. Many businesses benefit from a mix, so it is worth being clear about what you need.

### Can a CIMA accountant prepare my annual accounts and tax return?

Some CIMA members also hold practising certificates for compliance work, but not all do. It is worth asking directly what compliance work the practice is set up to handle, and whether anything needs to be referred elsewhere.

### How do I judge whether a CIMA accountant is right for my business?

Ask for examples of the management reports they produce and whether those resemble what you want to see each month. Check which body supervises the practice for anti-money-laundering purposes, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled. A conversation about your actual decisions and reporting needs will tell you more than any label.
