## One key thing to understand about egg freezing

Egg freezing treatment involving eggs is regulated, and clinics that offer it must be licensed by the HFEA. The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback, so you can compare local options before you enquire. Simple blood hormone checks may be offered outside HFEA licensing, but any treatment involving eggs should always be with a licensed centre.

The law allows eggs to be stored for up to fifty-five years, but this is subject to renewals, so it's worth asking how storage and renewal work in practice. Your age at the time of freezing is an important factor in the chance of success later, and the clinic can discuss what that means for you. They can also explain the realistic number of eggs that might be collected, based on your individual circumstances.

## Egg Freezing Near Me: FAQs

### How long can I store my eggs for?

The law allows eggs to be stored for up to fifty-five years, but this is subject to renewals. Your clinic can explain how the renewal process works and what you need to do to keep storage in place.

### Does my age matter for egg freezing?

Yes, your age at the time of freezing is an important factor in the chance of success if you use the eggs later. The clinic will discuss this with you and can explain what it means for your individual situation.
