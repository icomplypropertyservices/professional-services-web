## FAQs: clear aligners

### Can I get clear aligners without seeing a dentist first?

No, a dental examination before starting is essential. A dentist needs to check your teeth and gums to confirm aligners are appropriate for you.

### Why does gum health matter before aligners?

Gum health must be good first, because unhealthy gums can affect treatment and your overall dental health. Your dentist will check this at your examination.

### Will my teeth stay straight after treatment?

Retention is lifelong, so you'll need to keep wearing a retainer to hold your teeth in place. Your dentist will explain what that involves for you.

### What can I do if something goes wrong with private treatment?

Start with the practice's complaints process.

### Is a local high street clinic the same as a specialist orthodontist?

Not necessarily. Only someone on a General Dental Council specialist list can call themselves an orthodontist, so check the register if that title is used. A general dentist may still be able to provide clear aligners after examining you.

### Should I choose a practice purely because it's closest?

Convenience matters, since aligner treatment involves regular visits, but it shouldn't be the only factor. Weigh up how easily you can get there alongside whether the dentist examines your teeth and gums first and explains the quote clearly.

### What if I've seen aligners sold online or without a check-up?

A dental examination before starting is essential, because a dentist needs to check your teeth and gums and confirm aligners are appropriate. Treatment without that examination isn't something to rely on.

### Can I see a private dentist and stay with my NHS dentist?

Yes, you can use both. Some patients see a private dentist for specific treatments and keep NHS care for routine check-ups, if their NHS practice agrees.
