## How to check you are in safe hands

"Architect" is a protected title in the UK. Only people on the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance. Many practices are also RIBA Chartered Practices. Architectural designers and technologists can do excellent work too, but they are not registered architects, so it is worth knowing which you are hiring.

Before you appoint anyone, check the ARB register and ask for a written appointment that sets out services, fees and responsibilities. Building regulations approval, planning permission and party wall matters are separate processes that your architect can help you navigate.

iComply is not a regulator and does not provide the professional service itself. We introduce you to professionals who hold their own registrations, and we encourage you to check them on the public register.

## Best Architect Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need an architect for my extension?

Not always, but an architect can help you get the most out of your space, navigate planning and building regulations, and avoid costly mistakes. For complex or large projects, their expertise is often essential.

### How do I check if someone is a qualified architect?

You can check the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register online. Only people on this register can legally call themselves architects in the UK.

### What is the difference between an architect and an architectural designer?

Architects are registered with the ARB and have completed rigorous training, while architectural designers may have relevant qualifications but are not registered architects. Both can do excellent work, but it is important to know which you are hiring.

### How much does an architect cost?

Fees vary widely depending on the project's complexity, your location and the architect's experience. It is best to discuss fees openly and ask for a written quotation before appointing anyone.

### Can I speak to more than one architect before deciding?

Yes, and it is sensible to do so when you are searching for the best architect near you, because comparing approaches and quoted fees helps you judge what suits your project. Each practice can set out its own scope and quote, and you are free to appoint whichever you prefer.

### Should I choose an architect who is based close to my property?

A nearby practice can visit the site easily and will know your local planning authority, which can be helpful for extensions and alterations. That said, what matters most is relevant experience with your type of project and clear communication, so distance alone need not decide it.

### What happens at a first meeting with an architect?

Expect to discuss your plans for the building, your budget and any constraints such as a listed building or conservation area, and to hear how the architect would approach the work.
