## Understanding how HR consultancy is regulated

Most HR consultancy work is not regulated by a single body, so it pays to check credentials yourself before you engage anyone. Relevant memberships include the CIPD for HR professionals, and you can ask about their training, experience and references from similar clients. Where a consultant's work touches employment law, they should be clear about the limits of their role and when you need a solicitor or other regulated adviser.

## Warning signs to watch for

Be cautious if you notice any of the following:

- Vague deliverables
- Open-ended contracts with no review points
- No professional indemnity insurance
- Promises of guaranteed results

## Comparing HR consultants near you

Searching for HR consultants near me, rather than for a single consultant, suggests you are weighing up a few local options before deciding who to speak to. That is a sensible approach with HR work, because the right fit depends on your sector, the size of your team and the kind of issue you are facing. A consultant who is excellent on restructures may be less suited to day-to-day employee relations, so it helps to compare on relevance rather than distance alone.

When you compare, ask each consultant what proportion of their work is HR and which parts of it they handle most often. Check whether they hold CIPD membership, since that shows training, and ask for references from clients whose situation resembles yours. Be wary of anyone who is vague about the limits of their role: on high-risk matters such as dismissals or tribunal claims, a good consultant will say plainly when you need a solicitor.

It is also worth asking how they prefer to work with a local client, whether that means meeting at your premises, holding sensitive conversations face to face, or a mix of both. Ask what they would need from you before a first meeting, such as your staff handbook, contracts or a short summary of the issue, so the time is used well. Quotes are price on application, so ask what the quote covers before you commit.
