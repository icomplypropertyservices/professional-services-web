## What to know about hiring a house architect near you

In the UK, the title 'architect' is protected by law. Only people on the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance. Many practices are also RIBA Chartered Practices. Architectural designers and technologists can do excellent work too, but they are not registered architects, so it is worth knowing which you are hiring.

Before you appoint anyone, check the ARB register and ask for a written appointment that sets out services, fees and responsibilities. Building regulations approval, planning permission and party wall matters are separate processes that your architect can help you navigate. A local architect will also be familiar with your council's planning approach and typical local constraints.

## FAQs about house architect near me

### Do I need an architect for a house extension or renovation?

Not always, but a registered architect can help you get the most from your space, light and budget, and can guide you through planning and building regulations. For some projects, an architectural designer or technologist may also be suitable, so it is worth checking their qualifications and experience.

### How do I check if someone is a registered architect?

You can check the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register online or ask the professional for their registration details. Only people on the ARB register can call themselves architects in the UK.

### What should I bring to a first meeting with an architect?

Bring examples of homes you like, a rough idea of your budget, and any plans or surveys you already have. It also helps to think about how you want to live in the space and what matters most to you.

### Does a house architect near me need to visit the property?

A site visit is usually valuable, especially for an extension or new build, because the plot, light and neighbouring buildings all affect the design. Ask at the first conversation whether a visit is included and how they would approach it.

### What if my house is in a conservation area or is listed?

Extra planning considerations can apply, and a local architect will be used to how your council handles them. Ask how they would approach the application and what information they would need from you.

### Can I use an architectural designer instead of a registered architect for my house?

Architectural designers and technologists can do good work on house projects, but they are not registered architects. If you want a registered architect, check the Architects Registration Board register and ask for their registration details.

### Will an architect guarantee planning permission?

No one can guarantee planning permission. A good architect will explain local policy, the risks and how to give your application the best chance.
