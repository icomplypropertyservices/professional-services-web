## Understanding donor sperm treatment in the UK

In the UK, donor sperm treatment is tightly regulated. Clinics offering donor sperm treatment must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). This ensures that donors are screened and that treatment meets safety and ethical standards.

Donor-conceived people can access identifying information about their donor when they turn eighteen. Clinics can explain how this works and what it means for you and your future child. The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback, which can help you compare options.

## Checking fertility clinics are qualified and regulated

Clinics offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback.

Simple tests such as blood hormone checks may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing, but treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre.
