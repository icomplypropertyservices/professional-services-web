## What to understand about cloud accountants and regulation

In the UK, the word "accountant" on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can use it. Titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. It is reasonable to ask which professional body they belong to and what that means for you.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. You can ask which body supervises the firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled. Cloud accountants use software such as Xero, QuickBooks or FreeAgent to give real-time visibility of your numbers, with bank feeds and digital receipts, so ask which platforms they are certified in and how they handle set-up.

## FAQs about cloud accountant near me

### Do I need to already use cloud accounting software to work with a cloud accountant?

No, many cloud accountants can help you choose and set up suitable software from scratch. They will discuss your needs and recommend a platform they are certified in, such as Xero, QuickBooks or FreeAgent.

### What does a cloud accountant do differently from a traditional accountant?

A cloud accountant uses online software to give you real-time visibility of your numbers, often with bank feeds and digital receipts. This can make it easier to keep on top of your finances and work collaboratively with your accountant throughout the year.

### How can I check if a cloud accountant is properly qualified?

You can ask which professional body they belong to, such as ICAEW, ACCA or CIMA, and check their membership with that body. You can also ask which body supervises the firm for anti-money-laundering purposes and whether they hold professional indemnity insurance.

### Will a cloud accountant near me be able to help with forecasting?

Many cloud accountants can use the real-time data from your accounting software to help with forecasting and cash flow planning. It is worth asking how they approach this and what information they need from you.

### Do I need a chartered accountant?

Not always. For simple Self Assessment a qualified, supervised accountant may be enough. For audits, complex group structures or specialist tax work, a chartered or certified firm is often the safer choice. We can match you to either.
