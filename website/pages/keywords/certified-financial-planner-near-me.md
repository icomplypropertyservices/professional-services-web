## Certified Financial Planner Near Me: FAQs

### What does CFP mean and why does it matter?

CFP stands for Certified Financial Planner, a certification held through the CISI in the UK covering financial planning practice and ethics. It indicates a planning-led approach, and you can check CISI records to confirm someone holds it. It is not a substitute for checking FCA authorisation.

### How do I check that a local financial adviser is properly authorised?

Use the FCA register to confirm that the adviser or firm is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm.

### Should I choose independent or restricted advice?

Independent advisers consider the whole market, while restricted advisers are limited to certain products or providers. Advisers must tell you which they offer and how they charge, so you can decide what suits your situation. There is no single right answer for everyone.

### How can I judge whether a local planner is right for me?

Ask about their qualifications, whether they hold the CFP certification, whether their advice is independent or restricted, and how they charge. A first conversation should leave you clear on what they will do and what it will cost. iComply does not assess or vouch for advisers, so it is worth checking the FCA register and CISI records yourself.

### Is the first meeting free?

Many firms offer a free initial consultation to explain their service and charges. Formal advice is then charged as agreed in writing.
