## How to check you are in safe hands

Mortgage advice is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Any broker or adviser you use should be authorised, or be an appointed representative of an authorised firm, and you can check this on the FCA register. Advisers typically hold a qualification such as CeMAP.

Brokers must tell you whether they search the whole of the market or a limited panel of lenders, and how they are paid, whether by lender commission, a fee, or both. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

A broker works on your behalf to find suitable products from providers. Ask how many providers they compare and how they are paid.

## Comparing several brokers before you choose

Searching for the best mortgage brokers near me suggests you want to weigh up more than one option rather than simply take the first name you come across. That's sensible, because two brokers covering your area may work quite differently: one may search the whole market while another works from a limited panel, and their fee arrangements can vary too. Knowing this before you speak to anyone helps you compare like with like.

A useful way to judge for yourself is to ask each broker the same short set of questions and note the answers side by side. Do they search the whole of the market or a limited panel? How are they paid, and what would you pay directly? Are they authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority, and can you find them on the FCA register? Someone who answers these clearly and without hesitation is easier to feel comfortable with, and 'best' then becomes a matter of fit for your circumstances rather than a label anyone can claim.

It's also worth thinking about what you want from the relationship. Some people want a broker they can sit down with locally, especially if their income or credit history is complex and needs careful explaining; others are happy with phone and email. Being clear about this from the start means the brokers you compare are genuinely suited to how you'd like to work.

## Best Mortgage Brokers Near Me: FAQs

### How can I check if a mortgage broker is authorised?

You can check on the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) register to see if a broker or adviser is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm. The register will also show the firm's reference number and the permissions it holds.

### What does 'whole of market' mean for a mortgage broker?

A whole-of-market broker considers mortgages from a wide range of lenders across the market. A broker who works with a limited panel only considers mortgages from a selected set of lenders. Brokers must tell you which type of service they offer.

### How are mortgage brokers paid?

Mortgage brokers can be paid by lender commission, a fee from you, or both. They must explain how they are paid before you start. It's important to understand the total cost, including any fees, before you commit.

### What is a decision in principle and why get one early?

A decision in principle (also called an agreement in principle) is an indication from a lender of how much they might be willing to lend you, based on an initial check of your finances. It can help show estate agents that you are a serious buyer and give you confidence about your budget before you make an offer.

### Should I speak to more than one mortgage broker?

You can speak to as many as you find helpful, and comparing a few lets you see how each explains the market, their panel of lenders and their fees. Just be aware that each may carry out a credit search at some stage, so ask them about this before proceeding.

### How do I judge which broker is best for me?

There's no single answer, because 'best' depends on your situation. Look at whether they cover lenders that suit your deposit, income and credit history, whether they explain things in a way you understand, and whether their fees are clear before you commit.

### What happens at a first appointment with a mortgage broker?

A first appointment usually covers your income, outgoings, deposit and plans, so the broker can assess affordability and outline possible options. It's also your chance to ask how they're paid, which lenders they can access and what happens next.

### Will speaking to a broker affect my credit score?

An initial conversation does not. Formal applications usually involve a credit search, and the broker should tell you before that happens.
