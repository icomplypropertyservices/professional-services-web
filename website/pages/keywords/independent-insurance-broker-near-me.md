## Why 'independent' matters when choosing a broker

An independent insurance broker is not owned by an insurer, so they can approach a wide range of markets on your behalf. That means they can look beyond a single company's products and find cover that suits your particular needs. Many independent brokers also specialise in niche risks that high-street insurers may not handle.

All insurance brokers and intermediaries in the UK must be authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) or be appointed representatives of an authorised firm. You can check a broker on the FCA register. Many brokers also belong to the British Insurance Brokers' Association (BIBA), and some hold Chartered status from the Chartered Insurance Institute. Brokers must understand your demands and needs, explain key policy features and exclusions, and tell you how they are paid.

## Questions to ask the insurance broker

Asking a few direct questions early tells you a lot about how a practice works:

- Are you independent, and which insurers do you typically approach for a risk like mine?
- How many insurers will you compare for my case?
- What information do you need from me to get accurate quotes?
- Will you explain the key exclusions and any conditions in the policies you recommend?
- How are you paid for arranging my insurance, and will you tell me in writing?
- Are you a member of BIBA or do you hold Chartered status, and what does that mean for me?

## How fees usually work (POA)

Brokers are often paid commission by the insurer and sometimes charge a broker fee; they must disclose how they are paid if you ask. Premiums are set by insurers based on risk. The broker quotes you, POA.

Compare cover, excesses and exclusions, not just the premium. The cheapest policy is not always the best value when you need to claim.

## FAQs about independent insurance broker near me

### What does 'independent' mean when it comes to insurance brokers?

It means the broker is not owned by an insurer and can approach a range of insurers in the market. They can compare policies from different providers to find cover that suits your needs.

### How can I check that an insurance broker is properly authorised?

You can check on the FCA register to confirm the broker is authorised or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm. Many brokers also belong to BIBA, which is a useful sign of professionalism.

### Will an independent broker definitely find me a cheaper price?

Not necessarily — independence means they can compare options, but the right cover is about more than price. A broker will focus on matching cover to your needs, and you can judge whether the quote is competitive by comparing the scope of cover and exclusions.

### What should I have ready when I speak to a broker?

Have details of what you want to insure, any previous claims, and the cover you currently have, if any. The broker will need this information to understand your demands and needs and to approach insurers on your behalf.

### Is a broker more expensive than buying direct?

Not necessarily. Brokers can access insurers and schemes not available directly, and help with claims. The right choice depends on how complex your needs are.
