## How to check you are in safe hands

"Architect" is a protected title in the UK. Only people on the Architects Registration Board (ARB) register can call themselves architects, and they must follow the ARB code of conduct and hold professional indemnity insurance. Many practices are also RIBA Chartered Practices. Architectural designers and technologists can do excellent work too, but they are not registered architects, so it is worth knowing which you are hiring.

Before you appoint anyone, check the ARB register and ask for a written appointment that sets out services, fees and responsibilities. Building regulations approval, planning permission and party wall matters are separate processes that your architect can help you navigate.

## Home Extension Architect Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need planning permission for my home extension?

Some extensions are permitted development, which means you may not need a planning application. However, it depends on the size, location and type of extension, so it's best to check with your local planning authority or ask an architect to advise on your specific plans.

### What is the difference between an architect and an architectural designer?

Architectural designers and technologists can also do excellent work, but they are not registered architects, so it's worth knowing which you are hiring.

### How can I check if someone is a registered architect?

You can check the ARB register online to confirm whether someone is a registered architect. It's also a good idea to ask for a written appointment that sets out the services, fees and responsibilities before you appoint anyone.

### What are party wall matters and when do they apply?

Party wall matters can arise when you plan to build on or near a boundary with a neighbour, or when work affects a shared wall. Your architect can help you identify when the Party Wall etc. Act may apply and what steps to take, though it's a separate process from planning permission.

### Will an architect guarantee planning permission?

No one can guarantee planning permission. A good architect will explain local policy, the risks and how to give your application the best chance.
