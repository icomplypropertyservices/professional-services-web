## Qualifications and registration to look for

Employment agencies and businesses in the UK must follow the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations, enforced by the Employment Agency Standards Inspectorate. Agencies cannot usually charge work-seekers for finding them work. Membership of the Recruitment and Employment Confederation (REC) or APSCo shows commitment to an industry code.

Agencies supplying temporary workers have obligations on pay, holiday and right-to-work checks. Clear terms of business for employers, and clear contracts for workers, are essential.

## FAQs about graduate recruitment agency near me

### Do I have to pay a graduate recruitment agency to find me work?

In the UK, employment agencies cannot usually charge work-seekers for finding them work. The Employment Agency Standards Inspectorate enforces these rules. However, it's always wise to check the terms before signing up.

### When is the best time to start looking for a graduate scheme?

Graduate recruitment often follows the academic calendar, with many schemes opening applications in the autumn and winter for the following year. Starting early gives you the best chance of securing a place.

### What is an assessment centre and will I have to attend one?

An assessment centre is a selection event where candidates take part in exercises and interviews. Many graduate employers use them as part of their recruitment process. Your agency should prepare you for what to expect.

### How can I check if a graduate recruitment agency is reputable?

You can ask if the agency is a member of a professional body like the Recruitment and Employment Confederation (REC) or APSCo, which indicates a commitment to an industry code. You can also check with the Employment Agency Standards Inspectorate for any enforcement actions.

### What is a rebate period?

An agreed period after a permanent hire during which the agency refunds part of its fee or finds a replacement if the person leaves.
