## Understanding natural IVF and clinic licensing

Natural IVF uses little or no stimulation medication, so only one or a few eggs are collected each cycle. This means fewer eggs than conventional IVF, and success rates for natural IVF are generally lower per cycle. Suitability depends on your individual situation, so a consultation with a clinic is important.

Any clinic offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing or most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the HFEA. You can check a clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback using the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool. Simple blood hormone tests may be offered outside HFEA licensing, but treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre.
