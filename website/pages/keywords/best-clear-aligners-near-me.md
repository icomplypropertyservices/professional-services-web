## Checking private dentists are qualified and regulated

Dentists, dental hygienists, dental therapists and dental nurses must be registered with the General Dental Council (GDC), and you can check the GDC register online. Specialist titles such as orthodontist or endodontist can only be used by people on a GDC specialist list. Private clinics providing regulated healthcare in England must register with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which inspects and publishes ratings. In Wales the regulator is Healthcare Inspectorate Wales, in Scotland Healthcare Improvement Scotland, and in Northern Ireland the RQIA.

If something goes wrong with private dental treatment, start with the practice's complaints process. The Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints that are not resolved.

## FAQs about best clear aligners near me

### Can I get clear aligners without seeing a dentist first?

No, it is important to have a dental examination before starting clear aligners. A dentist needs to check your teeth and gums to make sure aligners are safe and suitable for you.

### How do I know if a dentist is qualified to provide clear aligners?

You can check that the dentist is registered with the General Dental Council (GDC) by looking on the GDC register online. You can also ask about their experience with aligners and how they plan treatment.

### Will I need to wear a retainer after aligner treatment?

Yes, retention is usually lifelong after any orthodontic treatment, including clear aligners. You will need to wear a retainer to keep your teeth in their new position, and your dentist will explain what this involves.

### What should I do if I have a problem with private dental treatment?

Start with the practice's complaints process. If the issue is not resolved, the Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints.

### Are clear aligners the same as braces?

No. Clear aligners are removable plastic trays that gradually move teeth, while fixed braces are attached to the teeth and can't be taken out. A dentist can explain which approach is likely to suit your teeth.

### Can I compare several dentists before deciding?

Yes, and it's sensible to. You can ask each practice what the examination involves, who will carry out the treatment and what aftercare is included, then weigh up the quotes you receive directly from the professionals.

### What happens at a first appointment for clear aligners?

The dentist will usually examine your teeth, gums and bite to check that aligners are a safe option for you, and discuss what treatment would involve. Nothing should be started until your mouth is healthy enough and you've had the chance to ask questions.

### Can I see a private dentist and stay with my NHS dentist?

Yes, you can use both. Some patients see a private dentist for specific treatments and keep NHS care for routine check-ups, if their NHS practice agrees.
