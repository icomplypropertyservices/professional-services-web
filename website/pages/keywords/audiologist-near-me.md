## Questions to ask the audiologist

Once you are introduced, these questions help you decide whether the audiologist is right for you:

- Are you registered with the Health and Care Professions Council, and what is your registration number?
- What does a hearing assessment involve and how long will it take?
- Do you offer trial periods for hearing aids, and what are the terms?
- What aftercare do you provide, including follow-up appointments and adjustments?
- How do you handle wax removal, and is it included in the price?
- What should I do if I experience sudden hearing loss or pain before my appointment?

## Checking audiologists are qualified and regulated

Hearing aid dispensers must be registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC), and you can check the register online. Clinical audiologists may also be registered through the Academy for Healthcare Science or other recognised registers. Many are members of the British Society of Audiology or the British Society of Hearing Aid Audiologists.

If you have sudden hearing loss, pain, discharge or hearing loss in one ear only, you should see a doctor promptly, as some causes need medical treatment.
