## Checking who you are booking

"Physiotherapist" and "physical therapist" are protected titles in the UK. Anyone using them must be registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC), and you can check the HCPC register online. Most physiotherapists are also members of the Chartered Society of Physiotherapy (CSP), shown by the letters MCSP.

Sports therapists, massage therapists and personal trainers can be helpful for some needs, but they are not physiotherapists. If you are dealing with back pain that may need proper assessment, check that you are booking a HCPC-registered physiotherapist. iComply does not regulate anyone or provide the service itself; we simply introduce you to a professional who quotes you directly.

## Questions to ask the physiotherapist

Once you are introduced, these questions help you decide whether the physiotherapist is right for you:

- How will you assess what is causing my back pain?
- Will I need imaging, or can we start with an assessment and exercise plan?
- What will a typical session involve, and how much will it cost?
- Should I keep active while I recover, and are there movements I should avoid?
- Will you give me exercises to do at home, and how often should I do them?
- Can you suggest changes at work or at home that might help my back?

## FAQs about back pain physiotherapist near me

### Do I need a scan before seeing a physiotherapist about back pain?

Often imaging is not needed for back pain, and a physiotherapist can assess you and build a plan without it. If a scan is appropriate, they will discuss that with you.

### Should I rest or stay active with back pain?

Staying active where possible is generally encouraged, though it depends on your situation. A physiotherapist can advise on what activity is suitable for you.

### How do I check that someone is a qualified physiotherapist?

The titles "physiotherapist" and "physical therapist" are protected, so anyone using them must be registered with the HCPC. You can check the HCPC register online before you book.

### Can a physiotherapist help with changes at work that might ease my back?

Physiotherapists can discuss workplace changes and how you sit, lift and move during the day. It is worth mentioning your work setup when you speak to them.

### Do I need a GP referral for private physio?

No, you can usually book directly as a self-paying patient. Your insurer may require a referral, so check your policy.
