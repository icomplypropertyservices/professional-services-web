## Care Home Insurance Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need specialist insurance for a care home?

Care homes face risks that ordinary business policies may not cover, such as abuse claims and the need for business interruption cover that reflects your duty to residents. A broker can explain what is essential for your setting and what is optional.

### What is abuse cover and why is it essential?

Abuse cover responds to allegations of abuse against residents or service users, which is a serious risk in care settings. It is essential because standard liability policies often exclude it, so you need it explicitly included and explained.

### How can staff training affect my insurance?

Insurers often look favourably on documented staff training and safeguarding procedures because they reduce the likelihood of claims. A broker can tell you what evidence of training might help when arranging cover.

### Can I get cover if my care home is not yet CQC registered?

CQC registration affects cover, and some insurers may have specific requirements for providers who are not yet registered or are in the process. A broker can explain what options are available and what information you will need to provide.

### What is non-disclosure?

Failing to tell an insurer something relevant. It can reduce or void a claim, so answer questions fully and ask your broker if unsure.
