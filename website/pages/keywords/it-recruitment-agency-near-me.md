## FAQs: an IT recruitment agency

### Do I have to pay an IT recruitment agency to find me a job?

Agencies in the UK cannot usually charge work-seekers for finding them work, under the Conduct of Employment Agencies and Employment Businesses Regulations. If you're looking for a job, you shouldn't be asked to pay a fee. Employers may be charged by agencies for their services.

### What do the off-payroll working rules mean for IT contractors?

The off-payroll working rules determine whether a contractor should be taxed like an employee. They can affect how much you take home and who is responsible for paying tax. An IT recruitment agency should be able to explain how they apply to your contract.

### How can I check if an IT recruitment agency is reputable?

You can ask whether the agency is a member of the Recruitment and Employment Confederation (REC) or APSCo, which shows a commitment to an industry code. You can also ask about their terms of business and how they handle pay and contracts. The Employment Agency Standards Inspectorate enforces the regulations for agencies.

### What should I look for in an IT recruitment agency near me?

Look for an agency that understands your technical specialism and can screen candidates properly, not just match keywords. If you're a contractor, check their knowledge of the off-payroll working rules and contract terms. Local knowledge of the job market in your area can also be helpful.

### What is a rebate period?

An agreed period after a permanent hire during which the agency refunds part of its fee or finds a replacement if the person leaves.
