## Qualifications and registration to look for

Conveyancing in England and Wales is a reserved legal activity. It can be carried out by solicitors regulated by the SRA or by licensed conveyancers regulated by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers (CLC). In Scotland, property transactions are handled by solicitors under Scottish law.

Many lenders only work with conveyancers on their panel, and the Law Society's Conveyancing Quality Scheme (CQS) is a common accreditation. Check that your chosen firm is on your lender's panel before you instruct them.

## FAQs about best commercial conveyancing near me

### Do I need a specialist commercial conveyancer?

Commercial property transactions involve different considerations from residential ones, such as wider due diligence, lease terms and VAT. A conveyancer who regularly handles commercial matters will be more familiar with these issues and can guide you through them.

### How do I check if a conveyancer is regulated?

In England and Wales, you can check whether a solicitor is regulated by the Solicitors Regulation Authority or a licensed conveyancer by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers. Both regulators maintain online registers where you can verify a firm's status.

### Should I choose a conveyancer who is local to the property?

It can be helpful if they know the area, but it's not essential. Many commercial conveyancers work remotely and can handle matters anywhere in England and Wales. What matters most is their experience with your type of property and transaction.

### How can I compare commercial conveyancing quotes?

Ask each conveyancer to break down their fee and explain what's included, such as VAT, disbursements and any extra work. Check that they have experience with commercial property and that you're comfortable with how they communicate.

### What is the difference between a solicitor and a licensed conveyancer?

Both are regulated and insured. Licensed conveyancers specialise in property transactions, while solicitors can also advise on wider legal issues. Either can handle a standard purchase or sale.
