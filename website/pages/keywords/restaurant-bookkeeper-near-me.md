## What restaurant owners usually need from a bookkeeper

A restaurant bookkeeper helps you stay on top of the money coming in and going out, day by day.

- You need someone to reconcile daily takings against till reports and bank deposits, so discrepancies are caught early.
- You want help recording tips and tronc payments correctly, because these have their own rules and need careful handling.
- You need supplier invoices checked and entered promptly, especially when prices change and affect your margins.
- You'd like payroll for shift-based staff handled smoothly, including variable hours and overtime.
- You want a bookkeeper who is local enough to understand your area and can meet in person if needed.

## Qualifications and registration to look for

Bookkeeping is not a regulated title in the UK, but anyone providing bookkeeping services for a fee must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by HMRC or by a professional body. Membership of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) or the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT) shows a bookkeeper has trained to a recognised standard and agreed to a code of conduct.

Ask whether a bookkeeper holds professional indemnity insurance and which body supervises them. Good bookkeepers are also clear about where their work stops and an accountant's starts, for example on year-end accounts and tax advice.

## FAQs about restaurant bookkeeper near me

### Do I need a bookkeeper who specialises in restaurants?

A bookkeeper with restaurant experience will understand daily takings, tips and shift payroll, which can save time and reduce errors. However, any qualified bookkeeper can learn your systems, so it's worth asking about their experience with similar businesses.

### How can I check a bookkeeper's credentials?

Ask whether they are supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes by HMRC or a professional body, and whether they hold professional indemnity insurance. Membership of the Institute of Certified Bookkeepers (ICB) or the Association of Accounting Technicians (AAT) shows they have trained to a recognised standard.

### Will a bookkeeper handle my payroll and tips?

Many bookkeepers do handle payroll and tips, but it's best to confirm exactly what they offer. Tips and tronc have specific rules, so ask how they record these and whether they work with your payroll provider.

### Can a bookkeeper help if I'm behind on my records?

Yes, many bookkeepers can help you catch up on overdue bookkeeping. Be upfront about the state of your records so they can quote accurately for the work involved.

### Does a restaurant bookkeeper near me need to visit my premises?

Not necessarily. Many bookkeepers work remotely and can collect till reports and invoices digitally, while others prefer to visit. It's worth asking what they prefer and whether their quote changes if they visit, since that affects how easy the arrangement is to keep up with during busy service periods.

### Can a local bookkeeper deal with cash takings and card machine records together?

Yes, reconciling cash takings against card machine records and bank deposits is core restaurant bookkeeping, and it's where discrepancies usually show up first. Ask how they would handle a night when the till report and the card statement don't match, so you know what to expect.

### What if I only need help with tips and tronc, not everything?

Bookkeepers can often take on just part of the work, such as recording tips and tronc while you or someone else handles supplier invoices. Be clear about the boundaries when you enquire, and confirm where their work stops and your accountant's begins, particularly on tax matters.

### Can a bookkeeper submit my VAT return?

Many can, through Making Tax Digital compatible software, once you authorise them. Check this is included in the scope before you start.
