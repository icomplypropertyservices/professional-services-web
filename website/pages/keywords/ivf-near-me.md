## What to understand about IVF and choosing a clinic

IVF involves stimulating the ovaries, collecting eggs, fertilising them in the lab and transferring an embryo to the womb. Success rates depend strongly on age, and single embryo transfer is often safer, so it is worth asking how these factors apply to you.

Clinics offering IVF and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback, which can help you judge for yourself which clinic suits you.

## How to check you are in safe hands

Clinics offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback.

Simple tests such as blood hormone checks may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing, but treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre.

## Costs and quotes for IVF

Fertility treatment is often quoted per cycle, but medication, tests, embryo storage and add-ons can be extra. The HFEA advises patients to ask for a full, personalised costed plan. The clinic quotes you, POA.

Be careful with add-ons. The HFEA rates many of them for evidence of benefit, and some have little or none.

## FAQs: IVF

### How do I check if a clinic is licensed for IVF?

Clinics offering IVF must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). You can use the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool to see each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback.

### What does IVF involve?

Your clinic will explain each step and discuss what is right for you.

### Why does age matter for IVF success?

Success rates depend strongly on age, so it is important to ask how a clinic's results apply to someone in your situation. The clinic can talk you through this in detail.

### Should I ask about embryo storage?

Yes, it is worth asking about storage for spare embryos, including what options are available and any associated costs. Your clinic can explain how storage works and what you need to consider.

### Am I eligible for NHS fertility treatment?

Eligibility depends on local commissioning rules, including age, how long you have been trying and previous children. Ask your GP; a private clinic can also explain options.
