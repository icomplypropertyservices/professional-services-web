## Qualifications and registration to look for

Funeral directors are not licensed in England, but many belong to the National Association of Funeral Directors (NAFD) or the National Society of Allied and Independent Funeral Directors (SAIF), which have codes of practice. Scotland has a statutory code of practice for funeral directors. Since 2022, providers of pre-paid funeral plans must be authorised by the FCA.

The Competition and Markets Authority requires funeral directors to display standardised price lists, so you can compare costs clearly.

iComply is not a regulator and does not provide the professional service itself. We introduce you to professionals who hold their own registrations, and we encourage you to check them on the public register.

## Questions to ask a funeral director

Asking a few direct questions early tells you a lot about how a practice works:

- How do you handle direct cremation?
- When is payment due?
- Can you support our religious or cultural needs?
- Are you a member of NAFD or SAIF?
- Can I see your standardised price list?
- If you sell funeral plans, are you FCA authorised?

## FAQs about funeral director near me

### What do I need to do first after a death?

If the death was expected, contact the GP or nurse to confirm it. A medical certificate is needed before the death can be registered. A funeral director can guide you through the next steps.

### What is direct cremation?

A cremation without a funeral service. Families can then hold a memorial separately.
