## Checking funeral directors are qualified and regulated

Funeral directors are not licensed in England, but many belong to the National Association of Funeral Directors (NAFD) or the National Society of Allied and Independent Funeral Directors (SAIF), which have codes of practice. Scotland has a statutory code of practice for funeral directors. Since 2022, providers of pre-paid funeral plans must be authorised by the FCA.

The Competition and Markets Authority requires funeral directors to display standardised price lists, so you can compare costs clearly.

## Funeral Director Near Me: FAQs

### How do I choose a funeral director near me?

Start by contacting a few local funeral directors and asking for their standardised price list so you can compare costs. Check whether they belong to NAFD or SAIF, and choose someone you feel comfortable speaking with about your needs.

### What is a standardised price list?

It is a price list that funeral directors are required by the Competition and Markets Authority to display, making it easier for you to compare costs between providers. Ask for it when you enquire so you can see what is included.

### Is direct cremation available from funeral directors near me?

Many funeral directors offer direct cremation as a simpler, lower-cost option. Ask the funeral director near you whether they provide it and what it includes.

### Do I need to use a funeral director who is a member of NAFD or SAIF?

It is not a legal requirement, but membership means the funeral director follows a code of practice. You can ask about membership when you contact them to help you decide.

### What do I need to do first after a death?

If the death was expected, contact the GP or nurse to confirm it. A medical certificate is needed before the death can be registered. A funeral director can guide you through the next steps.
