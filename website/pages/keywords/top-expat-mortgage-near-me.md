## Understanding the expat mortgage market

The number of lenders offering mortgages to UK nationals abroad is smaller than for UK residents, so choice can be limited. Foreign currency income is often assessed with extra checks, and larger deposits are common to offset the perceived risk.

Mortgage advice is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Any broker you use should be authorised or an appointed representative of an authorised firm, and you can check this on the FCA register. Advisers typically hold a qualification such as CeMAP.

## Top Expat Mortgage Near Me: FAQs

### Can I get a UK mortgage if I live abroad and earn in a foreign currency?

Yes, some lenders offer expat mortgages to UK nationals abroad, but they often have stricter criteria. A broker can tell you which lenders accept your currency and income type.

### What is the difference between a whole-of-market and a limited panel broker for expat mortgages?

A whole-of-market broker searches across many lenders, while a limited panel broker only uses a selected group. Brokers must tell you which they are, so you know the range of options available to you.

### How can I check that a mortgage broker is authorised to advise on expat mortgages?

You can check the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) register to see if the broker or adviser is authorised or an appointed representative.

### What should I do if I have a complaint about an expat mortgage broker?

First complain to the firm directly. If you are not satisfied, you can take your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

### Does my mortgage broker need to be physically near me if I live abroad?

Not usually. Expat mortgage work is mostly handled remotely, so a broker based in the UK can act for you wherever you live. What matters more is whether they understand expat lending and can communicate at times that suit your time zone.

### How can I tell whether a broker really deals with expat mortgages?

Ask them to talk you through how they assess foreign currency income, what deposit lenders typically look for on expat cases, and which currencies they have worked with. Their answers will show whether expat lending is routine for them or something they would be learning as they go.

### Should I speak to more than one broker before choosing?

It can help, because brokers differ in the lenders they use and how they are paid. Each broker must tell you whether they search the whole of the market or a limited panel, and how they are paid, so you can compare like with like before deciding.

### Will speaking to a broker affect my credit score?

An initial conversation does not. Formal applications usually involve a credit search, and the broker should tell you before that happens.
