## How to check you are in safe hands

Clinics offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback.

Simple tests such as blood hormone checks may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing, but treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre.

## Understanding ICSI and clinic licensing

ICSI is a technique used alongside IVF where a single sperm is injected directly into each egg. It's often recommended when there are concerns about sperm count, motility or morphology, or when previous IVF cycles have had poor fertilisation. Not everyone having IVF needs ICSI, so it's worth asking whether it's clinically necessary in your case.

Clinics that offer IVF with ICSI must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback, which can help you compare local options. Always check that any clinic you're considering is HFEA-licensed for the treatment you need.

## FAQs: ICSI

### What is ICSI and why might I need it?

ICSI stands for intracytoplasmic sperm injection, where a single sperm is injected directly into an egg. It's often used for male factor infertility or when previous IVF cycles have had poor fertilisation. Your clinic can advise whether it's appropriate for your situation.

### Do all fertility clinics offer ICSI?

Not all clinics offer ICSI, so it's important to check. Clinics that provide IVF with ICSI must be licensed by the HFEA. You can use the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool to find licensed clinics near you.

### How do I choose a clinic for ICSI near me?

Look for clinics that are HFEA-licensed and offer ICSI, and consider their inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback on the HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool. You may also want to ask about their experience with your specific fertility issue and the costs involved.

### Will ICSI lead to a successful pregnancy?

ICSI does not make a successful pregnancy certain. Success depends on many factors, including the woman's age and egg quality, and the clinic's success rates. Your clinic can discuss your individual chances.

### Am I eligible for NHS fertility treatment?

Eligibility depends on local commissioning rules, including age, how long you have been trying and previous children. Ask your GP; a private clinic can also explain options.
