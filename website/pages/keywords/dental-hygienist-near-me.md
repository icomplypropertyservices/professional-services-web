## Qualifications and registration to look for

Dentists, dental hygienists, dental therapists and dental nurses must be registered with the General Dental Council (GDC), and you can check the GDC register online. Specialist titles such as orthodontist or endodontist can only be used by people on a GDC specialist list. Private clinics providing regulated healthcare in England must register with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), which inspects and publishes ratings. In Wales the regulator is Healthcare Inspectorate Wales, in Scotland Healthcare Improvement Scotland, and in Northern Ireland the RQIA.

If something goes wrong with private dental treatment, start with the practice's complaints process. The Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints that are not resolved.

## Dental Hygienist Near Me: FAQs

### Can I see a dental hygienist without seeing a dentist first?

Yes, direct access to hygienists is allowed, and many practices will book you in without a separate referral. It is still worth asking the practice whether they would like you to have a dental check-up as well, especially if you have not been seen for a while.

### How do I check that a dental hygienist is properly registered?

Dental hygienists must be registered with the General Dental Council (GDC), and you can check the GDC register online. You can also ask the practice which regulators it is registered with, as private clinics providing regulated healthcare must register with the relevant body for their nation.

### What is airflow cleaning and is it worth asking about?

Airflow cleaning is a way of cleaning teeth that some practices offer alongside a usual scale and polish. It is worth asking about if you have staining or want your gums looked after, and the hygienist can tell you whether it suits your mouth.

### What can I do if I am unhappy with private dental treatment?

Start with the practice's own complaints process, as most concerns are sorted out there. If it is not resolved, the Dental Complaints Service can help with private dental complaints.

### Can I see a private dentist and stay with my NHS dentist?

Yes, you can use both. Some patients see a private dentist for specific treatments and keep NHS care for routine check-ups, if their NHS practice agrees.
