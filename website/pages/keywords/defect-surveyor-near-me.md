## Checking surveyors are qualified and regulated

Many surveyors are members of the Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors (RICS), and RICS-regulated firms must follow professional standards, hold insurance and offer a complaints process. Chartered surveyors use the letters MRICS or FRICS. Some specialist roles have their own bodies, such as the Faculty of Party Wall Surveyors or the Property Care Association for damp and timber specialists.

Check the RICS directory, ask which report type you are getting and confirm whether the surveyor is independent of the sale. For asbestos work, look for organisations accredited by UKAS for surveying.

iComply is not a regulator and does not provide the professional service itself. We introduce you to professionals who hold their own registrations, and we encourage you to check them on the public register.

## Understanding what a defect survey involves

A defect survey is a targeted inspection of a particular problem, such as cracking, water ingress or structural movement, and the surveyor will recommend repairs rather than survey the whole property. Because the focus is narrow, it helps to describe the symptom clearly and say how long you have noticed it, as this shapes what the surveyor looks at. Some defects turn out to need input from a structural engineer, and a good surveyor will tell you if that is the case.

Chartered surveyors use the letters MRICS or FRICS, and you can check the RICS directory to confirm someone's membership. For damp and timber problems, specialists may belong to the Property Care Association, while asbestos surveying is a separate field where you should look for organisations accredited by UKAS.

## FAQs about defect surveyor near me

### Is a defect survey the same as a homebuyer survey?

No. A defect survey focuses on one specific problem, such as cracking or damp, rather than assessing the whole property. If you are buying and already have a general survey, a defect survey can follow up on something it flagged.

### Will the surveyor carry out the repairs too?

Some surveying practices only inspect and report, while others may be able to recommend contractors or arrange further work. It is worth asking upfront so you know what to expect from the visit.

### Can a defect surveyor help with an insurance claim?

Insurers sometimes ask for a professional report on the cause and extent of damage before deciding on a claim. Tell the surveyor this is needed so the report covers what your insurer is likely to want.

### What if the problem turns out to be structural?

Defect surveyors investigate movement and cracking and can advise whether a structural engineer's input is needed. If so, they can explain what the next step should be rather than leaving you to guess.

### Is a mortgage valuation the same as a survey?

No. A mortgage valuation is carried out for the lender. A survey is carried out for you and looks at the property's condition.
