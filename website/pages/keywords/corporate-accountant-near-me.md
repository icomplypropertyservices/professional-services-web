## One key thing to understand when choosing a corporate accountant

In the UK, the term 'accountant' is not protected, so anyone can call themselves an accountant. However, titles like Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, meaning those professionals are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports, so if you need an audit, check that the firm is registered.

Every accountancy practice must be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises the firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled. This helps you judge the firm's accountability.

## Corporate Accountant Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need a corporate accountant if my company is audit-exempt?

Even if your company is audit-exempt, a corporate accountant can still help with preparing accounts, corporate tax returns and providing assurance work. They can also advise on whether voluntary assurance would benefit your stakeholders.

### How can I check if an accountant is qualified to handle corporate work?

Look for protected titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) or Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA). You can verify membership with the relevant professional body.

### What should I ask about international aspects of my business?

Ask about the accountant's experience with overseas subsidiaries, transfer pricing, double taxation treaties and foreign tax reporting. They should be able to explain how they handle cross-border issues.
