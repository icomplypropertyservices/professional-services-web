## Qualifications and registration to look for

"Counsellor" and "psychotherapist" are not protected titles in the UK. To protect yourself, choose a practitioner on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS. Practitioner psychologists, who use titles such as clinical or counselling psychologist, must be registered with the HCPC.

Accredited registers require training, supervision, a code of ethics and a complaints procedure. A good therapist will explain their approach, confidentiality and its limits in the first session.

"Therapist" covers many approaches. Ask about training, which register they are on and the type of therapy they offer.

iComply is not a regulator and does not provide the professional service itself. We introduce you to professionals who hold their own registrations, and we encourage you to check them on the public register.

## Private Therapist Near Me: FAQs

### How do I choose between therapists near me?

Check that the person is on a register accredited by the Professional Standards Authority, such as BACP, UKCP or NCPS, and that their approach and availability suit you. A short initial conversation can help you judge whether you feel comfortable speaking with them. We introduce you to a suitable professional, but the choice is yours.

### Can I see a therapist privately without seeing my GP first?

Yes, you can approach a private therapist directly without a referral. If your difficulties are affecting your health more broadly, it is still worth speaking to your GP, and NHS 111 can point you to urgent help if you need it.

### What if I would rather see someone close to home?

Tell us your area and how far you are willing to travel, and we will look for a counsellor or therapist practising locally. Seeing someone nearby can make regular appointments easier to keep. You can also ask whether they offer online sessions as an alternative.

### What does private therapy cost?

Fees are set by each therapist and are price on application, so ask directly when you speak to them. The quote you receive comes from the professional, not from us. It is reasonable to ask what the fee covers and how payment is arranged before you begin.

### Can I get therapy on the NHS?

Yes. In England you can refer yourself to NHS Talking Therapies. Waiting times vary by area, which is why some people choose private therapy.

### Can you find a private therapist in my area?

Send us your town or postcode with a short description of what you need. We look for a suitable counselling practice with capacity in your area, or one that can help remotely, and introduce you. It is free to enquire.

### How quickly will I hear back?

Straightforward enquiries are usually reviewed within a few working days. Mark urgent needs clearly. Speed also depends on practice capacity near you.

### Do I have to accept a quote?

No. An introduction is not a contract. You can ask questions, decline, or ask us for another match where capacity allows.
