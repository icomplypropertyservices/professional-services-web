## What people searching for charity insurance near me usually need

Charities often need cover that goes beyond a standard business policy, because the risks involve volunteers, trustees and fundraising events.

- You need cover for volunteers who give their time at your charity, in case something goes wrong while they are helping.
- You are planning an event or fundraiser and want to know whether your existing cover is enough or if you need extra protection for the day.
- You want trustee indemnity cover so that trustees are protected if a decision they make is later questioned.
- Your charity holds property, equipment or cash, and you need insurance that reflects what you own and where it is kept.
- You are setting up a new charity or changing what you do, and you need to arrange cover that matches your activities from the start.

## FAQs: charity insurance

### Do charities need a special kind of insurance?

Charities often need cover that addresses volunteers, trustees, events and fundraising, which may not be included in a standard business policy. A broker can explain what is typically included and help you decide what your charity needs.

### Can a local broker help if our charity runs events?

Yes, a broker can talk through your events and check whether your cover needs extending for the day. They can also explain what information they need about the event to arrange the right protection.

### What does trustee indemnity cover protect?

Trustee indemnity is designed to protect trustees if they are personally held responsible for certain decisions made on behalf of the charity. A broker can explain the scope and any limits of this cover.

### How do I check that a broker is properly authorised?

You can check whether a broker is authorised by the FCA on the FCA register. Many brokers also belong to BIBA, and some hold Chartered status from the Chartered Insurance Institute, which you can ask about.

### Is a broker more expensive than buying direct?

Not necessarily. Brokers can access insurers and schemes not available directly, and help with claims. The right choice depends on how complex your needs are.
