## Checking cosmetic surgeons are qualified and regulated

Cosmetic surgery should be carried out by a surgeon on the GMC's specialist register, usually in plastic surgery or a related specialty, in a hospital or clinic registered with the CQC (or the equivalent regulator outside England). Many surgeons are members of BAAPS or BAPRAS, and the Royal College of Surgeons offers a cosmetic surgery certification.

The GMC requires doctors offering cosmetic interventions to give patients time to reflect before agreeing to surgery, and not to use high-pressure sales. Your consultation should be with the surgeon who will operate.

## Breast Reduction Near Me: FAQs

### Will the NHS fund my breast reduction?

NHS funding is limited and criteria vary by area. Your GP can advise on local eligibility, but many people consider private treatment if they don't meet the criteria.

### Can breast reduction affect breastfeeding?

Yes, surgery can affect your ability to breastfeed. It's important to discuss this with your surgeon, especially if you plan to have children in the future.

### How long does recovery take?

Recovery takes several weeks. You'll need to avoid strenuous activity for a period and may need time off work, so plan accordingly.

### What should I expect at the consultation?

You should meet the surgeon who will operate, discuss your goals and medical history, and have time to ask questions. The surgeon should give you space to reflect before deciding.

### What is a cooling-off period?

Time between your consultation and agreeing to surgery. It gives you space to reflect and ask further questions, and reputable surgeons insist on it.
