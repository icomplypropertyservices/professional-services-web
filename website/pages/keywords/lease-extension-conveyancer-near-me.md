## Checking conveyancers are qualified and regulated

Conveyancing in England and Wales is a reserved legal activity. It can be carried out by solicitors regulated by the SRA or by licensed conveyancers regulated by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers (CLC). Both must hold professional indemnity insurance and follow strict rules on handling client money. In Scotland, property transactions are handled by solicitors under Scottish law.

Many lenders only work with conveyancers on their panel, and the Law Society's Conveyancing Quality Scheme (CQS) is a common accreditation. Check that your chosen firm is on your lender's panel before you instruct them.

## FAQs: a lease extension conveyancer

### Do I need a valuer as well as a conveyancer?

Yes, usually. A lease extension specialist handles the legal procedure, while a valuer assesses the premium. The two work together, so it helps to ask your conveyancer how they coordinate with a valuer.

### What is a statutory lease extension?

It's a route under the law that lets qualifying leaseholders extend their lease in a set way, rather than negotiating informally with the freeholder. A conveyancer can explain whether you qualify and what the process involves.

### Will a short lease affect my mortgage?

It can. Many lenders are cautious about short leases, and a short lease can also affect the value of your home. Extending the lease can help, so it's worth speaking to a conveyancer early.

### How do I check a conveyancer is properly regulated?

Solicitors are regulated by the SRA and licensed conveyancers by the Council for Licensed Conveyancers. Both must hold professional indemnity insurance, so ask which regulator they fall under before you instruct them.

### Does my lease extension conveyancer need to be local to me or to the property?

Not strictly. Lease extension work is largely handled in writing and by phone, so a conveyancer further away can act for you perfectly well. What matters most is that they regularly handle statutory lease extensions and that you can reach them easily when you need to.

### Can I use my existing conveyancer for a lease extension?

You can ask them, but it's worth checking whether they do lease extension work routinely, as it's a distinct area with its own procedure. If they don't, they may suggest you instruct someone who does. Either way, ask about their experience before you decide.

### What if my freeholder is hard to reach or won't engage?

The statutory route sets out steps that must be followed, and a conveyancer can explain what happens when a freeholder doesn't respond or disputes the claim. It's worth raising this early so you understand the options. Ask how they would handle it before you instruct them.

### When should I instruct a conveyancer?

As soon as your offer is accepted, or before you list a property for sale. Instructing early helps you avoid delays.
