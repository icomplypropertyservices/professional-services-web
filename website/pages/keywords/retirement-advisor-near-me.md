## What people searching for a retirement adviser nearby usually need

Most people at this stage want a clear plan for turning savings into a reliable income, and someone local they can talk it through with.

- You are approaching retirement and want to know whether you can afford to stop working when you hope to.
- You have pensions from several employers and want help understanding what you hold and how it fits together.
- You are unsure whether to buy an annuity, draw down income, or take a different route, and want the trade-offs explained.
- You have a defined benefit or final salary pension and need to understand the special rules before making any decision about it.
- You want to think through how care costs later in life might affect your plans, so your money lasts as long as you do.

## Questions to ask the professional

Asking a few direct questions early tells you a lot about how a practice works:

- Are you authorised by the FCA, and can you point me to your entry on the FCA register?
- Do you hold pension transfer specialist permissions if my defined benefit scheme is involved?
- How will you work out whether my savings can support the income I want?
- Will you show me how care costs later in life could affect my plan?
- How do you charge for your work, and will I get that in writing before anything begins?
- What happens if I change my mind or want to complain further down the line?

## What to tell us when you enquire

A short, clear brief saves time on both sides. Useful details include:

- Describe the pensions and savings you hold, including any workplace schemes and whether any are final salary or defined benefit.

## Why local matters less than authorisation here

Retirement advice is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), so whoever you speak to should be authorised, or an appointed representative of an authorised firm, on the FCA register. Plenty of advisers work with clients by phone or video, so someone nearby is convenient but not essential. If something goes wrong you can complain to the firm, then to the Financial Ombudsman Service, and the Financial Services Compensation Scheme may protect you if an authorised firm fails.

Pension decisions carry special rules. Advice on transferring out of a defined benefit scheme above a set value must come from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions, and safeguarded benefits deserve particular care. If you have a defined contribution pension and are over the age Pension Wise serves, free and impartial guidance is available from Pension Wise.

## Qualifications and registration to look for

Financial advice in the UK is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Check that any adviser or firm is authorised, or is an appointed representative of an authorised firm, on the FCA register.

Some pension decisions carry special rules. Advice on transferring out of a defined benefit (final salary) scheme above a set value must come from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions, and safeguarded benefits deserve particular care. Free, impartial guidance is available from Pension Wise for people over 50 with defined contribution pensions.

Adviser and advisor are simply two spellings of the same word. What matters is whether the person giving advice is qualified and, where the law requires it, regulated for that type of advice.

## When to think twice

Most professionals are honest and competent, but it is worth knowing the warning signs:

- Pressure to transfer quickly
- Firms not on the FCA register
- Offers to access a pension before age 55 (rising to 57 from 2028)
- Cold calls about your pension

## FAQs: a retirement adviser

### Can I get retirement advice without visiting an office?

Yes. Many pension advisers work with clients by phone or video, and that can widen your choice beyond firms close to home. What matters most is that the adviser or firm is authorised on the FCA register.

### I have a final salary pension — can any adviser help?

Not necessarily. Advice on transferring out of a defined benefit scheme above a set value must come from an adviser with pension transfer specialist permissions. Tell us about the scheme when you enquire so we can introduce you appropriately.

### Where can I get free guidance before I pay for advice?

Pension Wise offers free, impartial guidance for people with defined contribution pensions who are over the age it serves. It can help you understand your options before you speak to an adviser.

### How do I judge whether an adviser is right for me?

Check their entry on the FCA register, ask how they charge and get it in writing, and make sure you understand their explanation of your options. If anything feels unclear, ask again before committing.

### What is Pension Wise?

A free, impartial government-backed service offering guidance to people over 50 about their options for defined contribution pensions. It is guidance, not personal advice.
