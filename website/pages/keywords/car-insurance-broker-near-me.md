## Car Insurance Broker Near Me: FAQs

### Do I need to use a broker to buy car insurance?

No, you can buy directly from insurers, but a broker can compare policies and explain the details, which some people find helpful. They must understand your needs and tell you how they are paid.

### How do I check that a broker is authorised?

You can check on the FCA register. Brokers must be authorised by the FCA or be appointed representatives of an authorised firm.

### What if I need to make a claim?

Your broker can guide you through the claims process and help you understand what's covered. They should explain key policy features and exclusions before you buy.

### Can a broker help if I have a modified car?

Yes, brokers can find insurers who specialise in modified cars, but you must declare all modifications. This helps ensure your policy is valid.

### Will a broker definitely save me money on my car insurance?

Not necessarily. A broker's value is in helping you find suitable cover and explaining the details, not in guaranteeing a lower price. You should still compare quotes and check that the cover meets your needs.

### What information should I have ready for my first appointment?

It helps to have your car's make and model, your approximate annual mileage, where it's parked overnight, details of any modifications, and information about other drivers and any claims or convictions. This allows the broker to give you accurate options.

### Is a broker more expensive than buying direct?

Not necessarily. Brokers can access insurers and schemes not available directly, and help with claims. The right choice depends on how complex your needs are.
