## Understanding licensing when you look for a clinic near you

Clinics that freeze eggs must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool lets you look up each licensed clinic and see its inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback, which is a useful way to check a clinic you are considering near you.

Some simple tests, such as blood hormone checks, may be offered outside HFEA licensing, but anything involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre. It is worth confirming the licence before you book anything, especially if a clinic is convenient but unfamiliar to you.

## How to check you are in safe hands

Clinics offering IVF, donor treatment, egg and embryo freezing and most other fertility treatments must be licensed by the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority (HFEA). The HFEA's Choose a Fertility Clinic tool shows each licensed clinic's inspection ratings, success rates and patient feedback.

Simple tests such as blood hormone checks may be offered by clinics outside HFEA licensing, but treatment involving eggs, sperm or embryos should always be with a licensed centre.

Clinics vary from single-practitioner rooms to multi-specialist centres. Ask who will see you, their registration and what facilities are on site.
