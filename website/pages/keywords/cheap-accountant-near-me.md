## Checking accountants are qualified and regulated

In the UK, the word "accountant" on its own is not a protected title, so anyone can use it. Titles such as Chartered Accountant (ICAEW, ICAS or Chartered Accountants Ireland), Chartered Certified Accountant (ACCA) and Chartered Management Accountant (CIMA) are protected, and members are bound by their body's ethics, training and complaints rules. Only registered auditors can sign statutory audit reports.

Every practice offering accountancy services must also be supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, either by a professional body or by HMRC. It is reasonable to ask which body supervises a firm, whether they hold professional indemnity insurance, and how complaints are handled.

## Judging value when the fee is quoted on application

When you ask about a cheap accountant near you, the quote often comes as price on application rather than a figure on a page. That is not a dodge: the work varies so much between a single Self Assessment return and a limited company's accounts that a practice needs to know your situation before it can price fairly. Treat the conversation itself as part of the comparison.

Ask each accountant to set out in writing what the fee includes and, just as importantly, what it leaves out. Common extras are bookkeeping, payroll, VAT returns, year-end adjustments and the confirmation statement, so a low headline figure can climb once these are added. Comparing the whole package, not just the first number, is how you judge whether something is genuinely cheap for what you get.

It also helps to think about what a false economy looks like for your own circumstances. Missed deadlines, penalties and corrected filings usually cost more than the difference between a bargain quote and a fair one, and a practice that only files your paperwork without helping you plan may leave money on the table. A short first call is often enough to tell whether someone is listening to your situation or simply reading a price list.

## Cheap Accountant Near Me: FAQs

### How can I tell if a cheap accountant is right for me?

Ask what the fee includes and whether the person is a member of a protected title body such as ICAEW, ACCA or CIMA. A low price is only good value if the work is done properly and you understand what is covered.

### Do I need a qualified accountant for a simple tax return?

There is no legal requirement to use a qualified accountant for a Self Assessment tax return. However, a qualified member is bound by their body's rules on ethics and complaints, which can give you more reassurance.

### What is Making Tax Digital and will a cheap accountant handle it?

Making Tax Digital is HMRC's system for keeping digital records and submitting VAT returns. Ask any accountant you speak to how they handle it, as it may affect the fee.

### Can I find a cheap accountant who is also local to me?

Yes, many local practices offer competitive fees. iComply can introduce you to a UK accountant based on what you need, and they will quote you directly.

### Why do accountants quote price on application rather than a fixed fee?

Because the work depends on your circumstances: your business structure, how tidy your records are, whether you are VAT registered and which filings you need. A practice that quotes blind may later add charges, so ask for the scope in writing before you compare.

### Should I choose the lowest quote I receive?

Not automatically. Check that the quote covers everything you need, that the practice is supervised for anti-money-laundering purposes, and that you are comfortable with how they handle questions and complaints. The lowest number is only meaningful once you know what sits behind it.

A rough picture of your income and expenses, your business structure, whether you are registered for VAT or Making Tax Digital, and any deadlines coming up. With that, the accountant can give you a realistic quote and you can compare like with like.
