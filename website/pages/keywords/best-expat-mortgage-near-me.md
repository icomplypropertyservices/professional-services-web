## Checking mortgage brokers are qualified and regulated

Mortgage advice is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Any broker or adviser you use should be authorised, or be an appointed representative of an authorised firm, and you can check this on the FCA register. Advisers typically hold a qualification such as CeMAP.

Brokers must tell you whether they search the whole of the market or a limited panel of lenders, and how they are paid, whether by lender commission, a fee, or both. If something goes wrong, you can complain to the firm and then to the Financial Ombudsman Service.

## Best Expat Mortgage Near Me: FAQs

### Can I get a UK mortgage if I live abroad?

Yes, but fewer lenders offer expat deals. A broker who specialises in expat mortgages can tell you which lenders are likely to consider your situation.

### Does my foreign income count for a UK mortgage?

Lenders that accept expat applications will assess your foreign currency income, often converting it to sterling. The amount you can borrow will depend on the lender's criteria and the currency involved.

### Will I need a bigger deposit as an expat?

Larger deposits are common for expat mortgages, as lenders see overseas borrowers as higher risk. A broker can explain what deposit size lenders typically expect for your circumstances.

### How do I check a mortgage broker is authorised?

You can check on the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) register. Brokers must be authorised or be an appointed representative of an authorised firm, and they should tell you how they are paid.

### Will I need to travel to the UK to meet my mortgage broker?

Most expat mortgage advice is handled remotely by phone, email or video call, so you usually won't need to travel. The broker will let you know if any documents need to be signed in person or witnessed.

### Can I use a broker near my overseas home?

Mortgage brokers who arrange UK mortgages for expats can be based anywhere in the UK and still help you. What matters is their experience with expat lending and that they are authorised by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), which you can check on the FCA register.

### Is a broker better than going to my bank?

A broker can compare many lenders and may find options your bank does not offer. Your bank can only offer its own products. Either can suit you; a broker gives you a wider view.
