## Who we support

iComply Professional Services exists for regulated and high-trust UK practices that need practical help keeping standards clear, documented and workable day to day. We work with firms that advise clients, treat patients, handle money, or place risk — not with DIY consumers looking for fixed catalogue prices.

Our early vertical focus covers:

- **Law** — solicitors and lawyers, barristers' chambers workflows, and conveyancers managing property transactions.
- **Private healthcare** — private GPs and clinics, private dentists, and physiotherapists building clinical governance that patients can trust.
- **Accountancy and bookkeeping** — practices and bookkeeping teams that need clean processes between client work and filings.
- **Tax and financial advice** — tax advisors, financial advisors, financial planners, mortgage advisors, wealth and pensions specialists.
- **Insurance** — general insurance brokers and life insurance brokers who must evidence suitability and product governance.

Every engagement is **price on application (POA)**. We do not publish fixed sterling fees on this site. Request a quote and we scope the work to your firm size, risk profile and urgency.

## Why professional services firms ask for structured support

Regulation is not a one-page checklist. Partners, practice managers and compliance officers juggle client delivery, staff supervision, file audits, supplier due diligence, complaints handling, data protection, and the expectation that everything can be evidenced if a regulator, insurer or client asks. When growth outpaces process, gaps appear in places that look fine until a file review or inspection lands.

We help firms turn scattered policies into living operating rhythm: who owns which control, how often it is checked, what good looks like on a file, and how improvements are recorded without drowning fee-earners in bureaucracy. That matters whether you run a high-street conveyancing desk, a multi-partner solicitors' practice, a private clinic with CQC awareness, or an advice firm navigating FCA expectations.

## How engagements typically start

1. **Discovery call** — clarify your vertical, locations, headcount and the pressure points that prompted the enquiry.
2. **Scope outline** — we propose a bounded piece of work (policy pack, process map, training outline, audit readiness review, or operational coaching) with clear deliverables.
3. **Quote — POA** — written proposal with assumptions; no surprise catalogue pricing.
4. **Delivery** — workshops, document drafting, file sampling frameworks, or implementation coaching as agreed.
5. **Handover** — you keep ownership of your compliance; we leave you with artefacts your team can maintain.

Nothing on this preview site is an offer of regulated legal, medical, financial or insurance advice to the public. We support the firms that deliver those services.

## Working with iComply Professional Services

Firms choose us when they want plain-English process design, documentation that fee-earners will actually use, and a partner who understands that compliance support is not the same as practising in their regulated profession. We stay in our lane: operational clarity, evidence habits, training outlines, and readiness coaching. You remain the regulated firm.

Internal culture matters as much as written policy. We pay attention to supervision lines, file ownership, escalation paths, and how new joiners learn how the firm works. That is often where inspection risk hides — not in the glossy handbook nobody opens.

Busy seasons expose weak handovers. A conveyancing spike, year-end accountancy rush, or clinical rota gap can push evidence tasks aside. We help firms design lightweight controls that still run when diaries are full, so quality does not depend on one heroic compliance partner working weekends forever.

Suppliers and outsourced functions need the same discipline as employed staff. Cleaning contracts, IT providers, locum pools and outsourced typing all touch confidential information or client outcomes. Due diligence and onboarding checklists stop being optional once something goes wrong; we help make them routine before that day.

Data protection sits beside professional conduct. Retention schedules, access controls, and subject access readiness belong in the same operating conversation as file audits. We do not replace your DPO or solicitor — we help you see where process gaps create avoidable stress.
