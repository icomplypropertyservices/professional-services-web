## Need a professional? We connect you

Looking for a **solicitor**, **barrister**, **conveyancer**, **private dentist**, **private GP**, **accountant**, **mortgage adviser**, **IFA**, or **insurance broker**? iComply Professional Services is the middleman: you tell us what you need, we introduce you to a suitable UK practice, and that practice takes the work.

We are not a marketing agency for firms. We do not sell website or search packages. We help people find professionals — and we help practices receive that demand as clear introductions. Every commercial conversation is **price on application (POA)**. There are no fixed sterling catalogue fees on this site.

## How it works

1. **Enquire** — use [Contact](/contact/) with your need, location and urgency.
2. **Brief** — we clarify the problem (for example conveyancing, dental check-up, limited-company accounts, remortgage, or cover review).
3. **Match** — we introduce a practice with the right specialty and capacity.
4. **Connect** — the regulated firm contacts you and owns the professional relationship.
5. **POA** — any fee conversation with iComply or the practice is scoped honestly after discovery.

Nothing here is regulated legal, medical, financial or insurance advice from iComply. Advice and treatment stay with the professional you are introduced to.

## Why people use a middleman

Finding the right professional takes time. Directories are noisy. Personal recommendations help, but only if you already know someone in that field. We capture a clear brief — what you need, where, and how soon — then introduce a practice that is set up to take the conversation. You are not buying a website. You are asking to be connected.

Practices that work with us receive introductions aligned to their profession, not junk leads. Capacity and specialty matter. If we cannot make a good match, we say so rather than force a poor introduction.

## What to include in your enquiry

- What you need (profession and short problem statement)
- Town or region
- Time pressure (routine versus urgent)
- Preferred contact method and a reachable email or phone
- Any constraints (language, accessibility, remote versus in-person)

Do not paste confidential medical records, full financial files, or privileged legal papers into the first message. Share enough for a sensible introduction; the practice will ask for detail securely later.

## FAQs

### How do I find a solicitor, dentist or accountant through iComply?
Use the [Contact](/contact/) form. Say which profession you need, your location, and a short description of the problem. We review the brief and introduce a suitable UK practice where we can.

### What happens after I enquire?
We acknowledge the enquiry, clarify anything unclear, then introduce you to a matched practice when fit and capacity allow. The practice follows up on the professional work. Quotes involving iComply stay **POA**.

### How long does an introduction take?
Straightforward briefs are usually reviewed within a few working days. Complex or urgent matters may need a short clarifying call first. Timelines depend on practice capacity in your area.

### Do you charge fixed prices on this website?
No. Everything commercial is **POA**. We do not publish catalogue sterling fees for introductions or related support.

### Are you giving legal, medical or financial advice?
No. iComply is the middleman. Regulated advice and treatment come from the professional practice you are connected with.

### Can a practice request client introductions?
Yes. Practices should enquire via [Contact](/contact/) with vertical, locations and capacity. Terms are POA after scoping.
