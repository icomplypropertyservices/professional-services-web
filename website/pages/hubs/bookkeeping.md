## Monthly cadence, not year-end theatre

Bookkeeping bureaux win or lose on **monthly rhythm**: bank feeds, reconciliations, queries, payroll touchpoints, and clean handovers to accountants. iComply Professional Services centres this hub on cadence and software reality — deliberately different from the accountants hub’s year-end advisory arc.

When every client has a private spreadsheet ritual, coverage collapses if someone is ill. We help you standardise close checklists by client tier while still allowing justified exceptions that are written down.

## Software stack and access control

Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent and hybrid stacks create different failure modes. Access rights, MFA, client approval of drafts, and how you store source documents all need owners. We facilitate a stack map: which tool is source of truth, who grants access, and how offboarding a team member works the same day.

Support engagements are **POA**. We do not publish fixed bureau package prices for your end clients either.

## Handover to accountants and tax advisers

Month-end is not finished until the accountant can trust the trial balance narrative. Query logs, open-item ageing, and a short handover note reduce bounce-backs. We help design that interface so bookkeepers and accountants stop re-discovering the same missing receipt by email archaeology.

New joiner induction should cover data protection, phishing awareness, and when to escalate unusual transactions — not only which buttons to click.

## Client onboarding that prevents month-three chaos

Most bureau pain begins at onboarding: incomplete bank access, unclear payroll boundaries, missing opening balances, and nobody named for query turnaround. We help bookkeeping firms design an onboarding gate that must be green before monthly fees start in earnest. That gate includes ID of the client contact who can approve, software invite accepted, and a written scope of what you will and will not chase.

Scope creep — “can you just file this VAT while you are in there” — needs a change note. Otherwise goodwill erodes into unpaid advisory. Process design makes the change note normal, not awkward.

## Payroll adjacency and parallel calendars

Even when payroll is outsourced or separate, bookkeepers touch payroll journals and liabilities. Parallel calendars for RTI dates, VAT periods, and management account deadlines should be visible to the whole team. We help you build a shared calendar habit and a cover plan when someone is on leave during a filing week.

Corrections after accountant review should flow back into checklists. If the same class of error repeats across clients, that is a training item, not only a client-specific nag. We set up a simple error taxonomy so patterns are visible.

## Cyber hygiene for people who live in inboxes

Bookkeepers are prime phishing targets because they move money instructions and hold identity documents. Operational support includes onboarding MFA, how you verify changed bank details, and what to do if a client email feels wrong. We coordinate with your IT providers rather than pretending to be them. Pricing for our work stays **POA**.

## Pricing integrity for your own packages

While this site never lists fixed sterling fees for anyone, your firm may sell packages to clients. Internal clarity about what a package includes — transaction caps, meeting frequency, payroll add-ons — reduces disputes. We can facilitate a proposition workshop that writes those boundaries down for your team, without publishing your prices on our pages.
