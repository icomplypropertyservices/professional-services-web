## Portfolio construction and committee rhythm

Wealth management firms need a visible **investment process**: research inputs, committee decisions, model portfolio changes, and how exceptions are approved. iComply Professional Services structures this hub around that committee rhythm — not cashflow-first planning and not mortgage product research.

Whether you run discretionary, advisory, or hybrid models, clients and auditors ask how risk profiles connect to holdings. We help you document the path from fact-find to portfolio and from market event to client communication without creating unread novels.

## Discretionary versus advisory operating differences

Discretionary permissions change who must act and how quickly. Advisory models change evidence of client consent before trades. Mixing the two without clear file markers creates avoidable risk. Workshops separate the modes and name owners for dealing errors, rebalances, and corporate actions.

Support pricing is **POA**. No invented platform or management fee tables for your clients appear here.

## Reporting, custody interfaces and outsourcing

Quarterly reports, custodian feeds, and outsourced research all need quality checks. Who notices a broken feed? How are performance comments reviewed before they reach clients? We help design light control points that fit real team sizes.

New joiner induction should cover market-abuse awareness themes at an operational level, information barriers where relevant, and escalation contacts — coordinated with your compliance leadership, not replacing it.

## Risk profiling to portfolio: the join that auditors ask about

Wealth files are often strong on risk questionnaires and strong on holdings lists, yet weak on the join between them. We help firms describe how a profile maps to a model, when outliers are allowed, and who approves an off-model holding. That join is the heart of this hub’s uniqueness versus planners who lead with cashflow goals.

Rebalance rules — threshold, calendar, or hybrid — need written owners. Drift logs explain why a client stayed overweight after a market move.

## Client communication in volatile weeks

When markets fall, silence creates complaints. A pre-agreed communication ladder (email note, call cascade, ad hoc webinar) lets relationship managers act without inventing tone under stress. We help draft the ladder and the approval path for firm-wide messages so marketing and compliance are not surprised.

Performance commentary should separate fact, opinion, and forward-looking language per your rules. QA on a sample of letters each quarter catches drift.

## Conflicts, inducements and research access

Operational registers for conflicts and research access are only useful if fee-earners know they exist. Induction and refreshers should show where to record a conflict, not only that a policy PDF lives on the intranet. We design those micro-habits with your compliance officer’s priorities as the steer. Our fees stay **POA**; we do not trade or custody assets.

## Outsourcing custody, admin and IP

Third-party administrators and model providers need due diligence cadence: security questionnaires, SLA reviews, and exit plans. Boutique firms sometimes skip exit plans until a provider fails. We put exit thinking into the supplier pack early, while options still exist.
