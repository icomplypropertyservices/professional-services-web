## Life-stage planning as the page spine

Financial planning is not the same as product-led advice chasing. This hub is built around **life stages and cashflow**: accumulation, family protection moments, pre-retirement, decumulation, and later-life reviews. iComply Professional Services keeps the structure distinct from the mortgage-advisors product-research arc and the wealth-management portfolio-construction hub.

Suitability evidence, file notes, and review diaries are how planning firms show they meant what they recommended. We help design meeting agendas, assumption logs, and review triggers that planners will actually use between busy client weeks.

## Cashflow modelling discipline

Models are only as good as their assumptions and version history. Who changes growth rates? How are client goals restated when life events hit? When does a plan need a full rewrite versus a light update? We help you write those rules in plain English aligned to your advice process — without pretending to be your FCA compliance officer of record.

Our work is **POA**. Never invent client advice fees on this site.

## Ongoing service versus project planning

Some clients buy a one-off plan; others sit on an ongoing service proposition. Mixing those without clear onboarding creates expectation gaps and weak evidence at review. We facilitate proposition clarity workshops: what is included, how often you contact clients, and what happens if a client goes silent.

Paraplanning handovers need checklists too — especially when outsourced. Confidentiality, turnaround standards, and query loops belong in the operating pack.

## Goal language clients recognise at review

Plans fail in the file when goals were recorded as slogans (“comfortable retirement”) without measurable markers. We help planning firms define how goals are stated, how priorities are ranked when trade-offs appear, and how a review meeting re-opens the goal list before product talk. That discipline serves suitability evidence and serves clients who forgot what they said two years ago.

Cashflow outputs should be explained in the note: which scenario was shown, which was preferred, and what was explicitly discarded. Screenshots alone rarely tell the story.

## Vulnerability, capacity and sensitive life events

Divorce, bereavement, redundancy, and health shocks change planning capacity. Operational prompts should help planners slow down, offer extra time, and record why a recommendation waited. We place those prompts in agendas and CRM stages — aligned to your vulnerable-customer policy — without turning every meeting into a scripted interrogation.

Specialist referrals (tax, legal, protection) need a clean handoff note so advice does not fragment. We design the handoff fields your CRM should capture.

## Platform and research change control

When a platform migrates or a research house changes a risk score method, client files need a trail of what changed and who assessed impact. Committee or lead-planner notes beat silent spreadsheet edits. We help you define a light change-control habit proportionate to firm size. Engagements remain **POA**.

## Capacity planning for advice weeks

Diary design is compliance-relevant when rushed meetings produce thin notes. Blocking prep time, paraplanner buffers, and review batches prevents the “all new business, no reviews” spiral. We facilitate capacity conversations with principals so service propositions remain deliverable — a common root cause of file quality issues.
