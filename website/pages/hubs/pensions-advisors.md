## Accumulation and decumulation as twin tracks

Pensions advice work splits naturally into **accumulation** and **decumulation** — with transfers, retirement targeting, and later-life income choices carrying different evidence burdens. iComply Professional Services uses that twin-track structure here so the hub is not a clone of wealth or mortgage pages.

File notes must show why a recommendation suited the client’s objectives, risk profile, and tax context at the time. We help firms design stage checklists and peer-review triggers for higher-risk case types, working with your compliance leads — not instead of them.

## Employer and auto-enrolment adjacent operations

Some pensions specialists support employers with scheme governance admin interfaces. Contribution failures, staging legacy issues, and employee communications need clear owners. We help map those admin paths when they sit inside your firm’s proposition, without claiming to be your legal advisers on employment law.

Engagements are **POA**. Never invent member charging schedules on this site.

## Transfer case choreography

Pension transfer cases demand choreography: data gathering, analysis, client education, recommendation, and implementation. Skipping education steps to “save time” is how files look thin later. We help firms lock a stage-gate path with peer review at defined points, especially where overseas or high-risk features appear. You remain the advice firm; we remain the operating-design partner at **POA**.

Implementation checklists should confirm scheme discharge, tax registration points you handle, and client notifications — with owners named for each chase.

## Retirement targeting conversations

Clients often ask for a number without accepting the behaviour changes required. Notes should show the trade-offs presented: later retirement, higher contributions, different risk, or lower income targets. We provide agenda modules that make those trade-offs hard to skip, aligned to your advice process.

Decumulation options (annuity, drawdown hybrids, phased) need comparison records that a third reader can follow. Template tables help without forcing identical advice.

## Scheme data quality and employer interfaces

Incomplete scheme statements stall cases. A data-chase SLA with clients and providers reduces awkward open files. For employer-facing teams, contribution failure escalations need a named operations owner separate from the advising specialist where possible, so advice and admin do not blur under pressure.

## Training for complex case types

Not every adviser should take every transfer on day one. Permissions matrices and buddy reviews protect clients and advisers. We help you write the matrix and the criteria for unlocking case types after observed competence — a supervision habit, not a slogan.
