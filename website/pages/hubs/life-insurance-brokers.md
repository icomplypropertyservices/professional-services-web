## Needs analysis before product talk

Life and protection broking should open with **needs analysis**: dependants, debts, income replacement, and existing cover — not a product brochure. iComply Professional Services centres this hub on needs analysis, underwriting support, and trust or ownership admin evidence, keeping it distinct from general commercial lines broking.

Fact-find quality drives underwriting smoothness later. We help firms define what must be captured before an application is submitted and how vulnerable-customer flags are surfaced to supervisors.

## Underwriting support path

Outstanding GP reports, tele-interviews, and disclosure follow-ups stall cases. An underwriting tracker with owners and aged items prevents silent delays and awkward client calls. We design that tracker with your team’s real insurer mix in mind.

Support work is **POA**. We never invent premium illustrations as fixed public prices.

## Trust planning admin and replacements

Where trusts or ownership structures are discussed, admin evidence and referral boundaries matter. We help you document when to involve specialist legal input and how replacement business justifications are recorded per your policies — without giving legal advice ourselves.

Training outlines for new protection advisers should include disclosure ethics, illustration explanations, and complaint triggers common to life sales.

## Disclosure conversations that are hard to rush

Protection selling pressure can compress disclosure talks. Operational design should force time for medical history, lifestyle, and existing cover before illustrations become the centrepiece. We help firms place a disclosure completeness gate in the CRM so applications are not submitted half-ready, reducing deleverages and client frustration.

Joint life and business protection cases need extra clarity on who is the client for advice purposes. File markers prevent mixed audiences in one note.

## Illustration and quotation version control

Illustrations change when ages, terms, or underwriting classes move. Saving the version shown to the client matters when complaints arise months later. We define a simple artefact rule: what must be retained, naming conventions, and who checks before issue. **POA** for our design work; no public premium price lists.

## Beneficiary, trust and ownership follow-through

Many cases stall after policy issue because trust paperwork or ownership changes sit in someone’s inbox. A post-issue checklist with aged chasing prevents silent incompletes. When legal drafting is required, the referral out should be explicit. We do not provide legal drafting.

## Mortgage and protection joint journeys

Where protection is sold alongside mortgages, dual process maps stop one side assuming the other captured vulnerability or affordability context. We facilitate joint journey workshops between mortgage and protection leads so evidence sits in the right file without duplication theatre.
