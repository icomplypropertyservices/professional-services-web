## Risk review process as the operating spine

General insurance brokers create value through **risk review**: understanding the client, matching cover, explaining exclusions, and servicing claims pathways. iComply Professional Services builds this hub around that review cycle for commercial and personal lines — distinct from life-insurance needs analysis and from financial planners’ cashflow spine.

TOBAs, binder arrangements, and agency permissions shape what staff may quote. Operating packs should make those boundaries visible to new joiners before they pick up the phone.

## Commercial versus personal lines evidence

Commercial schedules, surveys, and warranties differ from personal lines speed and aggregation. Separate checklists reduce errors. We help broker principals define file standards for new business, mid-term adjustments, and renewals — including how referrals up the line are recorded.

Our support fees are **POA**. We do not publish fake premium prices or claim to place cover as your broker of record on this brand’s public pages.

## Product governance and complaint learning

Product governance evidence, fair-value considerations, and complaint root-cause habits sit with your compliance leadership. We help organise meeting cadences, action logs, and training outlines so improvements are traceable. We do not replace your regulated permissions.

Claims notification scripts and insurer communication standards deserve the same clarity as sales processes — especially when clients are distressed.

- New business risk-review checklist (commercial / personal)

- Referral and authority matrix

## Fact-find depth that matches line of business

A shop multi-risk and a haulage fleet do not deserve the same shallow fact-find. We help brokerages define minimum fact-find depth by line and by premium band, so authority levels and file reviews have something concrete to test. Personal lines can stay faster without becoming careless: essential questions still need capture before bind.

Statements of fact and statement-of-fact processes need version control when insurers update wording. Staff should know which version was shown.

## Mid-term adjustments and disclosure prompts

MTAs are where undisclosed changes hide. Operational prompts at MTA — “what else changed?” — catch vehicle swaps, new premises activities, or security downgrades. We place those prompts in workflow tools where clicks already happen.

Claims-made versus losses-occurring nuances belong in training outlines for relevant lines. We do not teach underwriting as an insurer; we help your internal training calendar stay specific.

## Aggregators, portals and staff authority

Portal journeys can bypass peer review if authority matrices are unclear. We map which staff may bind which products online and what second pair of eyes is required above thresholds. That map should match TOBA reality. Support engagements are **POA**.

## Fair value and product committee artefacts

Meeting minutes that only say “all products remain fair value” without artefacts will not help you later. We help committees attach evidence pointers — claims experience summaries, complaint themes, distribution costs notes — in a consistent folder structure. Your compliance leadership decides substance; we help the habit stick.
